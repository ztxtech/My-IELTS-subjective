# 传统故事

## 回答

> Describe a traditional story that is well-known in your country.
> 描述一个在你的国家广为人知的传统故事。

A:

1. 介绍故事名称和背景

**I'd like to talk about** the story of "The Cowherd and the Weaver Girl". **It's a very famous** traditional Chinese love story that has been told for thousands of years.

我想谈谈"牛郎织女"的故事。这是一个非常著名的中国传统爱情故事，已经流传了几千年。

2. 描述故事主要内容

**The story is about** a cowherd who falls in love with a fairy weaver girl. **Although** they come from different worlds, they get married and have two children. **However**, the girl's mother, the Queen of Heaven, separates them by creating a river in the sky - the Milky Way.

这个故事讲述了一个牛郎爱上仙女织女的故事。虽然他们来自不同的世界，但他们结婚了并有两个孩子。然而，女孩的母亲，王母娘娘，通过在天上创造一条河流——银河，将他们分开。

3. 说明故事的特别之处

**What makes this story special is that** they can only meet once a year, on the seventh day of the seventh lunar month. **On this day**, magpies form a bridge across the Milky Way so they can be together. **This day has become** the Chinese Valentine's Day.

这个故事特别之处在于他们每年只能在农历七月初七见一次面。在这一天，喜鹊会在银河上搭起一座桥让他们相聚。这一天已经成为了中国的情人节。

4. 分享个人感受

**I first heard this story** from my grandmother when I was a child. **Even now**, I still find it very touching because it shows that true love can overcome any difficulty.

我小时候第一次从我奶奶那里听到这个故事。即使现在，我仍然觉得它很感人，因为它表明真爱可以克服任何困难。
