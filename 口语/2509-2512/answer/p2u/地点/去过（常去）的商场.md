# 去过（常去）的商场

## 回答

> Describe a shopping mall you often go to.
> 描述一个你常去的购物中心

A:

1. （介绍商场位置和基本情况）  
There's a shopping mall near my university that I often visit. It's called "Global Center" and it's only a 15-minute walk from my dorm.  
我大学附近有一个我经常去的购物中心。它叫"环球中心"，从我宿舍走只要15分钟。

2. （描述商场的环境和设施）  
The mall is really big and modern, with four floors full of different shops. My favorite part is the food court on the top floor where they have all kinds of delicious food.  
这个商场很大很现代，有四层楼都是不同的商店。我最喜欢的是顶楼的美食广场，那里有各种好吃的食物。

3. （说明为什么经常去和喜欢的原因）  
I go there almost every weekend because it's a great place to relax after studying. Sometimes I go shopping with my roommate Ruijie, and other times I just go there to watch movies or enjoy some bubble tea.  
我几乎每个周末都去那里，因为这是在学习后放松的好地方。有时候我和室友Ruijie一起去购物，其他时候我只是去看电影或者喝奶茶。

4. （分享一个特别的经历）  
Actually, something funny happened there last month - I was buying snacks and entered a lucky draw, and surprisingly I won the first prize! That was such a happy surprise.  
实际上，上个月在那里发生了一件有趣的事 - 我在买零食时参加了一个抽奖活动，没想到我中了一等奖！那真是个惊喜。

5. （总结感受）  
So this shopping mall is not just a place to buy things, but also where I have made many good memories with friends.  
所以这个购物中心不仅仅是个买东西的地方，也是我和朋友们创造美好回忆的地方。

---

### 分析

#### 话题词汇/短语

1. shopping mall - 购物中心
2. food court - 美食广场
3. lucky draw - 抽奖活动
4. bubble tea - 奶茶
5. relax after studying - 学习后放松

#### 实用词汇/短语/句式

1. There's a... that I often visit - 有一个我经常去的...
2. only a 15-minute walk - 只要走15分钟
3. My favorite part is... - 我最喜欢的部分是...
4. all kinds of delicious food - 各种好吃的食物
5. Actually, something funny happened - 实际上，发生了一件有趣的事
6. not just... but also... - 不仅仅是...而且是
