# 有想象力的人

## 回答

> Describe an imaginative person you know.
> 描述一个你认识的有想象力的人

A:

1. （介绍人物）我想描述我的导师Prof. Deng，他是我认识的最有想象力的人。

I'd like to talk about my supervisor, Prof. Deng, who is the most imaginative person I know.

2. （背景信息）他是电子科技大学的计算机教授，专门研究时间序列分析。

He is a computer science professor at UESTC, specializing in time series analysis.

3. （具体例子）在做研究时，他总能想出别人想不到的新方法。比如有一次，我们遇到一个难题，大家都觉得没办法解决，但他提出了一个完全不同的思路，最后真的成功了。

When doing research, he always comes up with new methods that others can't think of. For example, once we faced a difficult problem that everyone thought was unsolvable, but he proposed a completely different approach that actually worked in the end.

4. （个人影响）他的想象力不仅帮助了我们的研究项目，也激励我要更有创造力地思考问题。

His imagination not only helped our research project but also inspired me to think more creatively about problems.

5. （总结感受）这就是为什么我认为他是我认识的最有想象力的人。

That's why I consider him the most imaginative person I know.

---

### 分析

#### 话题词汇/短语

1. imaginative person
2. come up with new methods
3. research project
4. think creatively
5. different approach

#### 实用词汇/短语/句式

1. I'd like to talk about... (用于开头介绍)
2. specializing in... (说明专业领域)
3. For example, once... (举例说明)
4. not only... but also... (连接两个相关观点)
5. That's why I consider... (用于总结观点
