# 榜样

## 回答

> Describe a person you admire or who has been a role model to you.
> 描述一个你钦佩或视为榜样的人

A:

1. 介绍榜样人物
**I'd like to talk about** my professor, Prof. Deng. **He is** my computer science teacher at university.
我想谈谈我的教授邓教授。他是我的大学计算机科学老师。

2. 说明如何认识他
**I first met him** when I started my research project. **He was** my supervisor and helped me with my studies.
我第一次见到他是在开始研究项目时。他是我的导师，在学习上帮助我。

3. 解释为什么认为他成功
**I think he's successful because** he has published many papers in international journals. **Also, he** always gives good advice to students and encourages us to try new things.
我认为他很成功，因为他在国际期刊上发表了很多论文。而且，他总是给学生很好的建议，鼓励我们尝试新事物。

4. 说明他对我的影响
**He has taught me** to be patient when facing problems. **For example,** when my research wasn't going well, he showed me how to keep trying until I succeeded.
他教会我在面对问题时要耐心。例如，当我的研究不顺利时，他告诉我如何继续尝试直到成功。

5. 总结感受
**That's why** I really admire him. **He is not only** a good teacher **but also** a kind person who cares about his students.
这就是为什么我很钦佩他。他不仅是个好老师，也是个关心学生的善良的人。

---

### 分析

#### 话题词汇/短语

1. professor - 教授
2. research project - 研究项目
3. supervisor - 导师
4. international journals - 国际期刊
5. good advice - 好建议

#### 实用词汇/短语/句式

1. I'd like to talk about... - 我想谈谈...
2. He is not only... but also... - 他不仅...而且...
3. That's why... - 这就是为什么...
4. For example,... - 例如...
5. I think he's successful because... - 我认为他成功
