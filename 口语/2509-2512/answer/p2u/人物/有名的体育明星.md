# 有名的体育明星

## 回答

> Describe a famous sports star you know
> 描述一个你认识的著名体育明星

A:

1. （介绍人物）我想谈谈姚明，他是中国最著名的篮球运动员之一。

I'd like to talk about Yao Ming, who is one of the most famous basketball players in China.

2. （背景信息）他曾经在美国NBA打球，效力于休斯顿火箭队，身高超过两米二。

He used to play in the NBA in America for the Houston Rockets team, and he's over 2.2 meters tall.

3. （个人看法）虽然他已经退役了，但我认为他仍然很受欢迎，因为他不仅球打得好，还是个很好的人。

Although he has retired, I think he is still very popular because he not only played basketball well but also was a very good person.

4. （具体事例）我记得他经常参加慈善活动，帮助需要帮助的人，这让我很敬佩。

I remember he often participated in charity events to help people in need, which made me really admire him.

5. （总结感受）总的来说，姚明不仅是个优秀的运动员，也是个值得学习的榜样。

Overall, Yao Ming is not only an excellent athlete but also a role model worth learning from.

---

### 分析

#### 话题词汇/短语

1. basketball player
2. NBA
3. retired
4. charity events
5. role model

#### 实用词汇/短语/句式

1. I'd like to talk about... (用于开头介绍)
2. who is one of the most... (用于描述重要性)
3. Although..., I think... (表达转折观点)
4. not only... but also... (并列描述优点)
5. which made me really... (表达个人
