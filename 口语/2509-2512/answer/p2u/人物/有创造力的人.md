# 有创造力的人

## 回答

> Describe a creative person whose work you admire.
> 描述一个你欣赏其作品的富有创造力的人

A:

1. 介绍人物背景
**I'd like to talk about** my roommate, Ruijie Liu, who is really good at music.
我想谈谈我的室友刘瑞杰，他非常擅长音乐。

2. 描述具体创意表现
**He can** play the guitar very well and often writes his own songs in our dorm.
他吉他弹得很好，经常在宿舍里写自己的歌。

3. 说明为什么有创造力
**What makes him creative is that** he mixes different music styles together to make something new.
他的创造力在于他把不同的音乐风格混合在一起，创造出新的东西。

4. 举例说明
**For example**, last month he wrote a song that combines traditional Chinese music with pop music.
例如，上个月他写了一首歌，把中国传统音乐和流行音乐结合在一起。

5. 表达个人感受
**I really admire** his ability to create beautiful music from different ideas.
我真的很佩服他能从不同的想法中创造出美妙的音乐。

---

### 分析

#### 话题词汇/短语

1. creative person
2. play the guitar
3. write songs
4. music styles
5. traditional Chinese music

#### 实用词汇/短语/句式

1. I'd like to talk about...
2. He can...very well
3. What makes him creative is that...
4. For example,...
5. I really admire...
