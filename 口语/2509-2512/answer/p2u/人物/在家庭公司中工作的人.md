# 在家庭公司中工作的人

## 回答

> Describe a person you know who works in a family business
> 描述一个你认识的在家族企业工作的人

A:

1. 介绍人物基本信息
   I'd like to talk about my cousin, Li Wei, who works in his father's restaurant business.
   我想谈谈我的表弟李伟，他在他父亲的餐饮企业工作。

2. 说明企业性质和工作内容
   It's a small local restaurant that serves traditional Chinese food. Li Wei helps with everything from cooking to serving customers.
   这是一家提供传统中餐的小型本地餐馆。李伟从烹饪到服务顾客什么都做。

3. 描述工作体验和感受
   Although the work is sometimes tiring, he enjoys it because he gets to work with his family. He says it makes him feel closer to them.
   虽然工作有时很累，但他很喜欢，因为他能和家人一起工作。他说这让他感觉和家人更亲近。

4. 说明未来的计划
   He plans to take over the business one day and maybe even open more restaurants in the future.
   他计划有一天接管这个生意，甚至将来可能开更多的餐馆。

---

### 分析

#### 话题词汇/短语

1. family business - 家族企业
2. local restaurant - 本地餐馆
3. traditional Chinese food - 传统中餐
4. take over the business - 接管生意

#### 实用词汇/短语/句式

1. I'd like to talk about... - 我想谈谈...
2. helps with everything from...to... - 从...到...什么都帮忙
3. Although..., he enjoys it because... - 虽然...，但他喜欢因为...
4. plans to...one day - 计划有一天...
