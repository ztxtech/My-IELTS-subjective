# 好朋友

## 回答

> Describe a good friend you have.  
> You should say:  
> when you met this friend  
> why you became friends  
> and explain why this friend is important to you.  

A:

1. 介绍朋友的基本信息  
**I'd like to talk about my roommate, Ruijie Liu.**  
我想谈谈我的室友刘瑞杰。

2. 说明相遇时间  
**We met two years ago when we started university.**  
我们两年前上大学时认识的。

3. 解释成为朋友的原因  
**We became friends because we both study computer science and like music.**  
我们成为朋友是因为我们都学计算机专业而且都喜欢音乐。

4. 补充朋友的特点  
**He plays guitar very well and often practices in our dorm.**  
他吉他弹得很好，经常在宿舍练习。

5. 说明朋友的重要性  
**He's important to me because we help each other with homework and share fun stories.**  
他对我来说很重要，因为我们互相帮助做作业，分享有趣的故事。

6. 举例说明友谊  
**Sometimes we have small arguments about habits, but we always make up quickly.**  
有时我们会因为生活习惯有小争执，但总能很快和好。

7. 总结友谊价值  
**He's a funny and honest friend who makes university life more enjoyable.**  
他是个有趣又真诚的朋友，让大学生活更加愉快。

---

### 分析

#### 话题词汇/短语

1. roommate
2. computer science
3. play guitar
4. practice in dorm
5. help with homework
6. share stories
7. make up quickly
8. university life

#### 实用词汇/短语/句式

1. I'd like to talk about...
2. We met...when...
3. We became friends because...
4. He's important to me because...
5. Sometimes we...but we always...
6. He's a...friend who...
