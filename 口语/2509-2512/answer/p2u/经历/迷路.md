# 迷路

## 回答

> Describe a time when you got lost.
> 描述一次你迷路的经历

A:

1. **介绍背景和地点**  
I want to talk about the time I got lost in a big shopping mall near my university.  
我想谈谈我在大学附近一个大商场迷路的经历。

2. **说明原因和经过**  
I went there to buy some snacks, but the mall was huge with many similar-looking stores. After shopping, I couldn't remember which exit to take.  
我去那里买零食，但商场很大，有很多看起来相似的商店。购物后，我不记得该走哪个出口。

3. **描述感受和反应**  
I felt a bit worried because it was getting late. I tried to find a map or ask for help, but I was too shy to talk to strangers.  
我有点担心，因为天快黑了。我试图找地图或求助，但太害羞不敢和陌生人说话。

4. **讲述解决方式**  
Finally, I used my phone to check the map and found my way back to the subway station. It took me about 20 minutes to get out.  
最后，我用手机查地图，找到了回地铁站的路。我花了大约20分钟才出去。

5. **总结感想**  
Although it was a bit stressful, I learned to pay more attention to my surroundings. Now I always note landmarks when I go to new places.  
虽然有点紧张，但我学会了更注意周围环境。现在我去新地方总会记下地标。
