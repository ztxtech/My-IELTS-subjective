# 突然停电

## 回答

> Describe a time when there was a sudden power outage.
> 描述一次突然停电的经历

A:

1. **介绍基本情况**  
I was working on my computer in the dormitory when suddenly all the lights went out.  
我正在宿舍用电脑工作，突然所有的灯都熄灭了。

2. **描述具体场景**  
It was around 8 PM and completely dark outside. My roommate Ruijie was playing guitar, and he stopped immediately.  
大概是晚上8点，外面完全黑了。我的室友Ruijie正在弹吉他，他立刻停了下来。

3. **说明应对方式**  
We used our phone flashlights to find candles. Luckily, I had some in my drawer because I like to use them when I study.  
我们用手机闪光灯找到了蜡烛。幸运的是，我抽屉里有一些，因为我学习时喜欢用它们。

4. **描述后续发展**  
We sat and talked for about an hour. Ruijie told funny stories, and we actually enjoyed the quiet time without electricity.  
我们坐着聊了大约一个小时。Ruijie讲了有趣的故事，我们其实很享受没有电的安静时光。

5. **总结感受**  
Although it was inconvenient, it turned out to be a nice break from our usual busy routine.  
虽然不方便，但这实际上是我们平时忙碌生活中的一个不错的休息。

---

### 分析

#### 话题词汇/短语
1. power outage - 停电
2. lights went out - 灯熄灭了
3. phone flashlights - 手机闪光灯
4. candles - 蜡烛
5. without electricity - 没有电

#### 实用词汇/短语/句式
1. when suddenly... - 当突然...时
2. Luckily, I had... - 幸运的是，我有...
3. We used... to find... - 我们用...来找...
4. Although..., it turned out to be... - 虽然...，但结果是...
5. enjoyed the quiet time - 享受安静
