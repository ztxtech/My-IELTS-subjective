# 需要等某人

## 回答

> Describe a time you had to wait for someone.
> 描述一次你需要等某个人的经历。

A:

1. (介绍等待的对象和原因)
   **I'd like to talk about waiting for my roommate, Ruijie Liu.** He's my university roommate and we're both computer science students. I had to wait for him because we planned to go to the gym together after class.
   **我想谈谈等我的室友刘瑞杰。** 他是我的大学室友，我们都是计算机专业的学生。我必须等他，因为我们计划下课后一起去健身房。

2. (描述等待的具体情况和感受)
   **Actually, I waited for about twenty minutes outside our dormitory.** He was late because he stayed after class to ask our professor some questions. At first, I felt a bit impatient, but then I understood his reason.
   **实际上，我在宿舍外面等了大约二十分钟。** 他迟到了，因为他下课后留下来问教授问题。起初我有点不耐烦，但后来我理解了他的原因。

3. (说明等待的结果和意义)
   **In the end, it was worth waiting because we had a good workout session together.** This experience taught me that sometimes waiting for others shows respect and understanding in friendships.
   **最后，等待是值得的，因为我们一起进行了很好的锻炼。** 这次经历教会我，有时等待他人体现了友谊中的尊重和理解。

---

### 分析

#### 话题词汇/短语

1. wait for someone
2. university roommate  
3. go to the gym together
4. after class
5. felt impatient
6. worth waiting

#### 实用词汇/短语/句式

1. **I'd like to talk about...** (用于开头介绍话题)
2. **Actually,...** (用于补充具体信息)
3. **At first,... but then...** (用于表达情绪转变)
4. **In the end, it was worth...** (用于总结积极结果)
5. **This experience taught me that...** (用于表达学到的道理
