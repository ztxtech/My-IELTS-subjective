# 别人有自己也想学的好习惯

## 回答

> Describe a good habit that someone else has and you would like to learn.
> 描述别人有而你也想学习的好习惯

A:

1. 介绍拥有好习惯的人

**I'd like to talk about** my roommate Ruijie Liu, who has a really good habit that I admire.

我想谈谈我的室友Ruijie Liu，他有一个我真的很羡慕的好习惯。

2. 具体描述这个好习惯

**He has this amazing habit of** practicing guitar every single day for at least one hour. **What's really impressive is that** he never skips a day, even when he's busy with schoolwork.

他有一个很棒的习惯，就是每天至少练习吉他一个小时。真正令人印象深刻的是，他从不间断，即使学业繁忙也是如此。

3. 解释为什么想学习这个习惯

**I really want to develop this habit because** I love music too, but I often make excuses not to practice. **If I could be as disciplined as him**, I could probably play guitar much better.

我真的很想培养这个习惯，因为我也喜欢音乐，但我经常找借口不练习。如果我能像他一样自律，我可能能弹得更好。

4. 说明这个习惯的好处

**This habit not only helps him** improve his musical skills **but also** teaches him patience and persistence. **I believe that** having such a regular practice routine would be really beneficial for me too.

这个习惯不仅帮助他提高音乐技能，还教会他耐心和坚持。我相信有这样规律的练习习惯对我也很有好处。

---

### 分析

#### 话题词汇/短语

1. practice guitar - 练习吉他
2. every single day - 每一天
3. never skips - 从不跳过
4. disciplined - 自律的
5. regular practice routine - 规律的练习习惯

#### 实用词汇/短语/句式

1. I'd like to talk about... - 我想谈谈...
2. He has this amazing habit of... - 他有一个很棒的习惯...
3. What's really impressive is that... - 真正令人印象深刻的是...
4. I really want to develop this habit because... - 我真的很想培养这个习惯因为...
5. If I could be as... as him - 如果我能像他一样
