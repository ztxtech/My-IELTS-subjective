# 朋友们不同意的事

## 回答

> Describe a time when you and your friends disagreed on something
> 描述一次你和朋友们在某件事上意见不同的经历

A:

1. 介绍背景和人物
**I'd like to talk about** a disagreement I had with my roommate Ruijie about our study schedule.
我想谈谈我和室友Ruijie在学习时间安排上的不同意见。

2. 说明分歧的具体内容
**The thing is,** I prefer to study late at night because I feel more focused, but Ruijie likes to study early in the morning.
问题是，我喜欢深夜学习因为我觉得更专注，但Ruijie喜欢早上学习。

3. 描述双方的观点和理由
**From my perspective,** nighttime is quieter and I can concentrate better. **However,** Ruijie believes that morning study is more effective for memory.
在我看来，晚上更安静，我能更好地集中注意力。然而，Ruijie认为早上学习对记忆更有效。

4. 讲述如何解决分歧
**In the end,** we decided to compromise. I study in the library at night, and we both use our room during daytime.
最后，我们决定妥协。我晚上在图书馆学习，白天我们都在宿舍使用房间。

5. 总结经验和感受
**This experience taught me that** it's important to respect different opinions and find solutions that work for everyone.
这次经历教会我，尊重不同意见并找到对大家都适用的解决方案很重要。

---

### 分析

#### 话题词汇/短语

1. study schedule - 学习时间安排
2. disagree on something - 在某事上意见不同
3. different opinions - 不同意见
4. find solutions - 寻找解决方案
5. compromise - 妥协

#### 实用词汇/短语/句式

1. I'd like to talk about... - 我想谈谈...
2. The thing is... - 问题是...
3. From my perspective... - 在我看来...
4. However... - 然而...
5. In the end... - 最后...
6. This experience taught me that... - 这次经历教会我...
