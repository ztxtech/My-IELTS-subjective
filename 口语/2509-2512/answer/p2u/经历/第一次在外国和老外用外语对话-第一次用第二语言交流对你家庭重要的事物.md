# 第一次用第二语言交流对你家庭重要的事物

## 回答

> Describe a time you used a second language to communicate about something important to you.
> 描述一次你用第二语言交流对你来说重要的事情的经历。

A:
1.  描述背景：去新加坡旅行，想找一家特色小店。

    **I'm going to talk about** **a time** I used English for something important. **It was** when I went to `Singapore` for a trip. I really wanted to find a special local `food shop` to try a famous `noodle dish`.
    
    我将要谈论一次我用英语做一件重要事情的经历。那是我去新加坡旅行的时候。我真的很想找到一家特别的当地美食店，尝尝一种有名的面条。

2.  描述遇到的问题和尝试交流。

    **When I got** there, I saw a `small shop`, but I was not **sure** if `it was` the right one. Also, I could not `read the menu` well. **So**, I saw an `old lady` sitting outside, and I thought she might know.
    
    我到了那里，看到一家小店，但我不确定是不是对的那家。而且，我看不懂菜单。于是，我看到一位老奶奶坐在外面，我想她可能知道。

3.  描述交流过程，以及感受。

    **Although** I felt a `little bit shy`, I knew `I really wanted` to try that `food`. **So**, I walked to her and said, "Excuse me, **do you know** if `this shop` sells the famous `Laksa`?" **She smiled** and then `slowly told` me, "Yes, `this is` the place! And `it's` very good."
    
    虽然我有点害羞，但我知道我真的很想尝尝那种食物。于是，我走向她，说：“打扰了，您知道这家店卖不卖有名的叻沙吗？”她笑了笑，然后慢慢地告诉我：“是的，就是这家！而且很好吃。”

4.  描述结果和体会。

    **I was** `so happy`! `It was` the `first time` I really used English like that to ask for something `I cared about`, and `it felt great` when I `understood her` and got the answer. **This experience** made me feel `more brave` to speak English.
    
    我非常开心！那是我第一次真正那样用英语去问我关心的事情，当我听懂她的话并得到答案时，感觉很棒。这次经历让我更勇敢地讲英语了。

---

### 分析

#### 话题词汇/短语

1.  `Singapore`: 目的地名称，简单易懂。
2.  `food shop`: 用来指代卖食物的店铺，比 restaurant 更口语和简单。
3.  `noodle dish`: 指面条菜肴，具体化想尝试的食物。
4.  `small shop`: 描述店铺的特征，简单词汇。
5.  `read the menu`: 动词短语，表示看懂菜单，非常实用。
6.  `old lady`: 口语中称呼年长女士的常用方式。
7.  `Laksa`: 特定食物的名称，展示细节。
8.  `first time`: 第一次做某事。

#### 实用词汇/短语/句式

1.  **I'm going to talk about...**: 介绍 Part 2 话题的经典开头句型，简洁明了。
    *   例句: **I'm going to talk about** my favorite teacher.
2.  **It was when I went to...**: 描述事件发生的时间和地点。
    *   例句: **It was when I went to** the park last weekend.
3.  **When I got there, I saw...**: 叙述性连接词，引导到达某地后的观察。
    *   例句: **When I got there, I saw** many children playing.
4.  **I was not sure if...**: 表达不确定性，日常对话中非常常用。
    *   例句: **I was not sure if** the bus was coming soon.
5.  **Also, I could not...**: 补充说明另一个情况或原因。
    *   例句: **Also, I could not** find my keys.
6.  **So, I saw an old lady...**: 表达因果关系，引出后续行动。
    *   例句: **So, I saw a friend** and waved to him.
7.  **Although I felt a little bit shy, I knew I really wanted to...**: 复合句，用 "Although" 表达转折，展现语法复杂度，同时 "a little bit shy" (有点害羞) 和 "really wanted to" (真的很想) 都是非常简单的情绪表达。
    *   例句: **Although I felt a little bit tired, I knew I really wanted to** finish my homework.
8.  **Do you know if...?**: 询问信息的常用句型，礼貌且直接。
    *   例句: **Do you know if** the library is open today?
9.  **She smiled and then slowly told me...**: 描述他人动作和说话方式，通过 "and then" 连接动作顺序。
    *   例句: **He waved and then quickly walked away.**
10. **I was so happy!**: 表达强烈的情绪，简单直接。
    *   例句: **I was so happy!** when I got the present.
11. **It felt great when I understood her...**: 表达感受，并用 "when" 连接原因。
    *   例句: **It felt great when I finished** the difficult task.
12. **This experience made me feel more brave to speak English**: 总结经验带来的影响，使用 `make sb feel + adj` 的句型，非常实用。
    *   例句: **This book made me feel more interested** in history.
