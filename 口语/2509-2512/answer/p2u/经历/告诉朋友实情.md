# 告诉朋友实情

## 回答

> Describe a time when you told your friend the truth.
> 描述一次你告诉朋友实情的经历

A:

1. 介绍背景和人物
I want to talk about my roommate Ruijie Liu. We study computer science together at university and share a dorm room.

我想谈谈我的室友刘瑞杰。我们一起在大学学习计算机科学，并共享一个宿舍房间。

2. 说明具体情境
One day, I noticed he was practicing guitar very late at night. Although I didn't want to hurt his feelings, I knew I should tell him the truth because it was disturbing my sleep.

有一天，我注意到他深夜还在练习吉他。虽然我不想伤害他的感情，但我知道我应该告诉他实情，因为这打扰了我的睡眠。

3. 描述说实话的过程
I said to him, "I really enjoy your music, but could you please practice earlier? The late night playing makes it hard for me to sleep." I was honest but also tried to be kind.

我对他说："我真的很喜欢你的音乐，但你能不能早点练习？深夜弹奏让我很难入睡。"我很诚实，但也尽量保持友善。

4. 说明结果和感受
He understood immediately and apologized. After that, he changed his practice time. I felt good about telling the truth because it solved our problem without damaging our friendship.

他立刻理解了并道歉。之后，他改变了练习时间。我对说实话感到高兴，因为它解决了我们的问题而没有损害我们的友谊。

---

### 分析

#### 话题词汇/短语

1. tell the truth - 说实话
2. share a dorm room - 共享宿舍房间
3. practice guitar - 练习吉他
4. disturb sleep - 打扰睡眠
5. solve the problem - 解决问题

#### 实用词汇/短语/句式

1. Although..., I knew... - 虽然...，但我知道...
2. I noticed that... - 我注意到...
3. I said to him... - 我对他说...
4. He understood immediately - 他立刻理解了
5. I felt good about... - 我对...感到
