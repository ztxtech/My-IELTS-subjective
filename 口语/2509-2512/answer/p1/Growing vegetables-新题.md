# Growing vegetables

## 题目

1. Is growing vegetables popular in your country?
2. Do many people grow vegetables in your city?
3. Do you think it's easy to grow vegetables?
4. Should schools teach students how to grow vegetables?

## 回答

> Is growing vegetables popular in your country?
> (在你们国家，种蔬菜流行吗？)

A: **Yes, definitely.** Many people enjoy growing their own vegetables, especially in smaller towns and villages. It's a great way to get fresh food and spend time outdoors. **I think** it's becoming more popular in cities too.
(是的，当然。很多人喜欢自己种蔬菜，尤其是在小镇和村庄里。这是获得新鲜食物和在户外度过时间的好方法。我认为在城市里也越来越流行了。)

> Do many people grow vegetables in your city?
> (在你的城市里，很多人种蔬菜吗？)

A: **Well, actually,** not so many in my city because most people live in apartments. But some people grow small plants on their balconies, like tomatoes or herbs. **I know** a few friends who do this.
(嗯，实际上，在我的城市里不是很多，因为大多数人住在公寓里。但有些人在阳台上种小植物，比如西红柿或香草。我知道有几个朋友这样做。)

> Do you think it's easy to grow vegetables?
> (你认为种蔬菜容易吗？)

A: **It depends on** what you're growing. Some vegetables like lettuce are quite easy, but others need more care. **You need to** water them regularly and make sure they get enough sun. **So** it takes some effort.
(这取决于你种什么。像生菜这样的蔬菜很容易，但其他的需要更多的照顾。你需要定期浇水并确保它们得到足够的阳光。所以需要一些努力。)

> Should schools teach students how to grow vegetables?
> (学校应该教学生如何种蔬菜吗？)

A: **Yes, I think that's a good idea.** It teaches children where food comes from and how to care for plants. **Also,** it's a fun outdoor activity. **For example,** they could have a small garden at school.
(是的，我认为这是个好主意。它教孩子们食物从哪里来以及如何照顾植物。而且，这是一个有趣的户外活动。例如，他们可以在学校有一个小花园。)
