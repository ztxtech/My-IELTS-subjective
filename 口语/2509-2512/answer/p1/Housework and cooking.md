# Housework and cooking

## 题目

1. Did you do some house cleaning when you were young?
2. What housework do you like or dislike doing?
3. Do you like cooking?
4. How often do you cook?
5. Do you enjoy cooking with others?
6. Do you like to cook for a large group of people?
7. What do you think of working as a waiter?

## 回答

> Did you do some house cleaning when you were young?
> 你小时候做过家务清洁吗？

A: **Yes, I did.** I helped my parents clean our house on weekends. **For example,** I often swept the floor and made my bed. It was a good way to learn responsibility.
是的，我做过。我周末帮父母打扫房子。例如，我经常扫地、整理床铺。这是学习责任感的好方法。

> What housework do you like or dislike doing?
> 你喜欢或不喜欢做什么家务？

A: **I really enjoy** cooking because it's creative. **However, I'm not a fan of** washing dishes because it's boring. **But** I still do it to help my family.
我真的很喜欢做饭，因为它很有创意。但是，我不喜欢洗碗，因为它很无聊。但我仍然会做来帮助家人。

> Do you like cooking?
> 你喜欢烹饪吗？

A: **Yes, definitely!** I find cooking very relaxing. **I especially like** trying new recipes. It's fun to make delicious food.
是的，当然！我觉得烹饪很放松。我特别喜欢尝试新食谱。做美味的食物很有趣。

> How often do you cook?
> 你多久做一次饭？

A: **I usually cook** about three times a week. **Mostly,** I make simple meals like fried rice or noodles. **Sometimes** I cook more on weekends.
我通常每周做大约三次饭。主要是做一些简单的饭菜，比如炒饭或面条。有时周末我会做更多。

> Do you enjoy cooking with others?
> 你喜欢和别人一起做饭吗？

A: **Yes, I do.** Cooking with friends is more fun. **We can** share ideas and learn from each other. **It makes** the experience more enjoyable.
是的，我喜欢。和朋友一起做饭更有趣。我们可以分享想法并互相学习。这让体验更加愉快。

> Do you like to cook for a large group of people?
> 你喜欢为一大群人做饭吗？

A: **Actually, I prefer** cooking for small groups. **It's easier to** manage and everyone gets to enjoy the food. **Large groups** can be a bit stressful for me.
实际上，我更喜欢为小团体做饭。这样更容易管理，每个人都能享受食物。大团体对我来说可能有点压力。

> What do you think of working as a waiter?
> 你对当服务员的工作有什么看法？

A: **I think** being a waiter is a tough job. **They need to** be patient and work fast. **But** it's a good way to meet people and learn communication skills.
我认为当服务员是一份艰苦的工作。他们需要有耐心并且工作迅速。但这是结识人和学习沟通技巧的好方法。
