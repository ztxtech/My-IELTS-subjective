# Doing something well

## 题目
1. Do you have an experience that you did something well?
2. Do you have an experience that your teacher thought you did a good job?
3. Do you often tell your friends when they do something well?

## 回答

> Do you have an experience that you did something well?
> 你有过某件事做得特别好的经历吗？

A: **Yes, definitely.** I remember when I finished my first `research paper` and it got published. **I felt really proud of myself** because I worked hard on it for months.
是的，当然有。我记得我完成第一篇研究论文并被发表时。我为自己感到非常自豪，因为我为此努力了好几个月。

> Do you have an experience that your teacher thought you did a good job?
> 你有过老师认为你做得特别好的经历吗？

A: **Actually, yes.** My `professor` was very happy with my last presentation. He said my `analysis` was clear and easy to understand. **That made me feel really good.**
实际上，有的。我的教授对我上次的演讲非常满意。他说我的分析清晰易懂。这让我感觉特别好。

> Do you often tell your friends when they do something well?
> 当朋友做得好时，你经常告诉他们吗？

A: **Yes, I always do.** I think it's important to `encourage` friends when they succeed. **For example,** when my roommate got a good grade, I told him "Well done!" right away.
是的，我总是这样做。我认为当朋友成功时鼓励他们很重要。比如，当我室友取得好成绩时，我立刻对他说"做得好！"

## 分析

### 思路与结构
1. 使用"Yes, definitely/actually, yes"直接回答问题
2. 提供具体例子支撑观点
3. 描述个人感受和结果
4. 使用简单连接词使回答连贯

### 话题词汇/短语
1. `research paper` - 研究论文
2. `professor` - 教授
3. `analysis` - 分析
4. `encourage` - 鼓励

### 实用词汇/短语/句式
1. **I felt really proud of myself** - 我为自己感到非常自豪
2. **That made me feel really good** - 这让我感觉特别好
3. **For example** - 比如
4. **Well done!** - 做得好！
