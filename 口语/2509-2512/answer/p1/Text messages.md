# Text messages

## 题目

1. How often do you send text messages?
2. Is sending messages popular in your country?
3. Did you send more messages when you were younger?
4. Do you reply to messages as soon as you receive them?

## 回答

> How often do you send text messages?
> 你多久发一次短信？

A: **Actually**, I send text messages almost every day. **I mainly use** WeChat to talk with my friends and family. **It's** very convenient **for** quick conversations.
(实际上，我几乎每天都发短信。我主要用微信和朋友家人聊天。对于快速对话来说非常方便。)

> Is sending messages popular in your country?
> 在你们国家发短信流行吗？

A: **Yes, definitely**. In China, **almost everyone uses** messaging apps like WeChat. **It's become** an important part of daily life **for** communication.
(是的，绝对是。在中国，几乎每个人都用微信这样的消息应用。这已经成为日常生活中沟通的重要部分。)

> Did you send more messages when you were younger?
> 你年轻的时候发的短信更多吗？

A: **Actually**, I think I send more messages now. **When I was** in high school, **I didn't have** a smartphone. **Now I use** my phone all the time for messaging.
(实际上，我觉得我现在发的更多。我上高中的时候还没有智能手机。现在我整天都用手机发消息。)

> Do you reply to messages as soon as you receive them?
> 你一收到消息就回复吗？

A: **It depends on** the situation. **If it's** from family or close friends, **I usually reply** quickly. **But sometimes when** I'm busy with study, **I might respond** later.
(这要看情况。如果是家人或好朋友发的，我通常很快回复。但有时候学习忙的时候，我可能会晚点回。)

## 分析

### 思路与结构

1. 直接回答问题并提供具体频率
2. 确认普遍性并举例说明
3. 对比过去和现在的使用情况
4. 根据关系亲疏和忙碌程度区分回复速度

### 话题词汇/短语

1. `text messages` - 短信
2. `WeChat` - 微信
3. `messaging apps` - 消息应用
4. `quick conversations` - 快速对话

### 实用词汇/短语/句式

1. **Actually** - 实际上（用于开始回答）
2. **I mainly use** - 我主要使用（说明主要方式）
3. **It's very convenient for** - 这对...非常方便（解释优点）
4. **Yes, definitely** - 是的，绝对是（强烈肯定）
5. **It depends on** - 这取决于（表示情况不同）
6. **If it's... I usually...** - 如果是...我通常...（条件句式
