# Puzzles

## 题目

1. Did you do puzzles in your childhood?
2. When do you do puzzles, during your trip or when you feel bored?
3. Do you like doing word puzzles or number puzzles? Which is more difficult for you?
4. Do you think it is good for old people to do puzzles?

## 回答

> Did you do puzzles in your childhood?
> 你小时候玩过益智游戏吗？

A: **Yes, definitely.** I often played with `jigsaw puzzles` and `memory games` when I was a kid. **It was a fun way to** pass the time and **I really enjoyed it.**
是的，当然。我小时候经常玩拼图和记忆游戏。这是一种打发时间的有趣方式，我真的很喜欢。

> When do you do puzzles, during your trip or when you feel bored?
> 你什么时候玩益智游戏，是在旅行时还是感到无聊时？

A: **I usually do puzzles when** I feel a bit bored or have some free time. **For example,** on long train rides or **just to relax** at home.
我通常在感到有点无聊或有空闲时间时玩益智游戏。例如，在长途火车上或只是为了在家放松。

> Do you like doing word puzzles or number puzzles? Which is more difficult for you?
> 你喜欢玩文字游戏还是数字游戏？哪个对你来说更难？

A: **I prefer** `word puzzles` like crosswords. **I find** `number puzzles` **a bit more challenging** because math isn't my strongest subject.
我更喜欢像填字游戏这样的文字游戏。我觉得数字游戏有点挑战性，因为数学不是我的强项。

> Do you think it is good for old people to do puzzles?
> 你认为老年人玩益智游戏有好处吗？

A: **Absolutely, I think it's** very beneficial. **It helps** keep their minds active and **can even improve** their memory. **It's a great way for them to** stay sharp.
当然，我认为这非常有益。它有助于保持他们思维活跃，甚至可以改善他们的记忆力。这是他们保持敏锐的好方法。
