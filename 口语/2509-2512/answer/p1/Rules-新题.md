# Rules

## 题目

1. Are there any rules for students at your school?
2. Do you think students would benefit more from more rules?
3. Do you have a teacher who does his or her job very well?

## 回答

> Are there any rules for students at your school?
> 你学校对学生有什么规定吗？

A: **Yes, definitely.** We have rules about being on time for class and not using phones during lessons. **For example,** we need to arrive before 8 am every day.
是的，确实有。我们有关于准时上课和上课不用手机的规定。比如说，我们每天需要在早上8点前到校。

> Do you think students would benefit more from more rules?
> 你认为学生能从更多规定中受益吗？

A: **Well, I think** having some rules is good, but too many can be stressful. **Actually,** the most important thing is that students understand why the rules exist.
嗯，我认为有一些规定是好的，但太多会让人压力大。实际上，最重要的是学生要明白为什么有这些规定。

> Do you have a teacher who does his or her job very well?
> 你有老师工作做得特别好吗？

A: **Yes, absolutely.** My computer science teacher, Professor Deng, is excellent. **Not only** does he explain things clearly, **but he also** gives us opportunities to do research projects.
是的，绝对有。我的计算机老师邓教授非常出色。他不仅讲解清晰，还给我们做科研项目的机会。

## 分析

### 思路与结构

1. 第一题：直接确认有规则，举例说明具体规则
2. 第二题：承认规则的好处，但提出过多规则的负面影响
3. 第三题：明确肯定回答，具体描述老师的优点

### 话题词汇/短语

1. `rules` - 规则
2. `on time` - 准时
3. `research projects` - 科研项目

### 实用词汇/短语/句式

1. **Yes, definitely.** - 肯定的开场
2. **For example,** - 举例说明
3. **Well, I think** - 表达个人观点
4. **Actually,** - 转折或强调
5. **Yes, absolutely.** - 强烈肯定
6. **Not only... but also...** - 并列句式
