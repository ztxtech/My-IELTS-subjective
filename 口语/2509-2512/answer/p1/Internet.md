# Internet

## 题目

1. When did you start using the internet?
2. How often do you go online?
3. How does the internet influence people?
4. Do you think you spend too much time online?
5. What would you do without the internet?

## 回答

> When did you start using the internet?
> 你什么时候开始使用互联网？

A: **I first started** using the internet when I was in primary school. **At that time,** I **mainly used it** to play simple online games and watch cartoons. **It was** really exciting to discover this new world.
我第一次使用互联网是在小学的时候。那时我主要用它玩简单的在线游戏和看动画片。发现这个新世界真的很令人兴奋。

> How often do you go online?
> 你多久上一次网？

A: **I go online** almost every day because **I need to** check emails for school and do research. **I also like to** browse social media in my free time to relax. **It has become** a part of my daily routine.
我几乎每天都上网，因为我需要查看学校的邮件和做研究。我也喜欢在空闲时间浏览社交媒体来放松。这已经成为我日常生活的一部分。

> How does the internet influence people?
> 互联网如何影响人们？

A: **The internet has** a big influence on people. **It helps us** learn new things and stay connected with friends. **However,** sometimes **it can be** distracting if we use it too much. **Overall,** **it makes** life more convenient.
互联网对人们有很大的影响。它帮助我们学习新事物并与朋友保持联系。然而，如果我们使用太多，它有时会让人分心。总的来说，它让生活更方便。

> Do you think you spend too much time online?
> 你觉得你在网上花的时间太多了吗？

A: **Sometimes I think** I spend a bit too much time online, especially when I'm scrolling through videos. **But I try to** balance it with other activities like reading or exercising. **It's important to** take breaks from screens.
有时我觉得我在网上花的时间有点多，尤其是当我刷视频的时候。但我试着用其他活动来平衡，比如阅读或锻炼。从屏幕前休息一下很重要。

> What would you do without the internet?
> 如果没有互联网你会做什么？

A: **Without the internet,** **I would probably** read more books and spend time outdoors. **I might also** hang out with friends in person instead of texting. **Life would be** simpler but less convenient.
如果没有互联网，我可能会读更多的书并在户外度过时间。我也可能亲自和朋友出去玩，而不是发短信。生活会更简单但不太方便。

## 分析

### 思路与结构

1. 每个回答都从直接回应问题开始，然后提供具体细节或例子
2. 使用简单的对比结构（如"However"）来展示多角度思考
3. 结尾总结个人感受或观点

### 话题词汇/短语

1. `online games` - 在线游戏
2. `social media` - 社交媒体  
3. `stay connected` - 保持联系
4. `scroll through videos` - 刷视频
5. `take breaks from screens` - 从屏幕前休息

### 实用词汇/短语/句式

1. **I first started** - 我第一次开始（用于讲述过去经历）
2. **I need to** - 我需要（表达必要性）
3. **It has become** - 它已经成为（表示变化）
4. **However,** sometimes - 然而，有时（表达对比）
5. **Without..., I would probably** - 如果没有...，我可能会（虚拟语气表达假设）
