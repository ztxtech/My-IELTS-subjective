# Being busy

## 题目

1. Are you often busy?
2. Why are you busy?
3. When are you busy?
4. Are you busier now than when you were a child?

## 回答

> Are you often busy?
> 你经常忙吗？

A: **Yes, I'm usually quite busy.** I have classes and research work to do every day. But I try to make time for my hobbies too.
是的，我通常很忙。我每天都有课和研究工作要做。但我也尽量为我的爱好腾出时间。

> Why are you busy?
> 你为什么忙？

A: **Mainly because of my studies.** I'm a computer science student, so I have many assignments and projects. Also, I do research in time series analysis, which takes a lot of time.
主要是因为我的学习。我是计算机专业的学生，所以有很多作业和项目。另外，我研究时间序列分析，这需要很多时间。

> When are you busy?
> 你什么时候忙？

A: **I'm busiest during weekdays.** Mornings are for classes, and afternoons are for research work. Evenings are when I can relax a bit, unless I have deadlines.
我工作日最忙。上午上课，下午做研究工作。晚上可以放松一下，除非我有截止日期要赶。

> Are you busier now than when you were a child?
> 你现在比小时候更忙吗？

A: **Definitely, much busier now.** When I was a child, I just went to school and played. Now I have more responsibilities with studies and research. But I enjoy being productive.
当然，现在忙多了。小时候，我只是上学和玩耍。现在我在学习和研究方面有更多责任。但我喜欢高效地工作。

## 分析

### 思路与结构

1. 直接回答问题，然后提供具体原因和补充信息
2. 使用简单的时间描述来组织回答（weekdays, mornings, afternoons）
3. 通过对比过去和现在来回答比较类问题
4. 每个回答都保持积极结尾

### 话题词汇/短语

1. `classes` 课程
2. `research work` 研究工作
3. `assignments` 作业
4. `projects` 项目
5. `deadlines` 截止日期

### 实用词汇/短语/句式

1. **Yes, I'm usually quite...** 是的，我通常很...
2. **Mainly because of...** 主要是因为...
3. **I'm busiest during...** 我在...期间最忙
4. **Definitely, much... now** 当然，现在...多了
