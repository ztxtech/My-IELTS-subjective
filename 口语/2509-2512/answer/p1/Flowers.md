# Flowers

## 题目

1. Do you love flowers?
2. Do you know anyone who loves flowers?
3. Are there a lot of flowers where you live?
4. Do you take photos of flowers in your daily life?
5. Do people in your country often send flowers to others?

## 回答

> Do you love flowers?
> 你喜欢花吗？

A: **Yes, I do.** I think flowers are beautiful and they make me feel happy. **For example**, I like roses because they smell nice.
(是的，我喜欢。我觉得花很漂亮，它们让我感到开心。比如，我喜欢玫瑰，因为它们闻起来很香。)

> Do you know anyone who loves flowers?
> 你认识喜欢花的人吗？

A: **Actually**, my roommate really loves flowers. He often buys some and puts them in our room. **It seems** they make him relaxed.
(实际上，我的室友非常喜欢花。他经常买一些花放在我们房间里。看起来这些花让他感到放松。)

> Are there a lot of flowers where you live?
> 你住的地方有很多花吗？

A: **Yes, there are.** In Chengdu, you can see flowers in many parks and streets. **Especially** in spring, the city looks very colorful.
(是的，有很多。在成都，你可以在很多公园和街道上看到花。特别是在春天，这个城市看起来非常多彩。)

> Do you take photos of flowers in your daily life?
> 你在日常生活中会给花拍照吗？

A: **Sometimes I do.** I enjoy photography, and flowers are a good subject. **But** I don't do it very often because I'm usually busy.
(有时候会。我喜欢摄影，花是一个很好的拍摄对象。但我不经常这样做，因为我通常很忙。)

> Do people in your country often send flowers to others?
> 在中国，人们经常送花给别人吗？

A: **Yes, they do.** People often give flowers on special days, like birthdays or Valentine's Day. **It's a common way** to show love or congratulations.
(是的，他们会。人们经常在特殊的日子送花，比如生日或情人节。这是一种表达爱意或祝贺的常见方式。)

5. **Especially** (特别是) - 强调特定情况
6. **Sometimes I do.** (有时候会。) - 部分肯定回答
7. **But** (但) - 转折连接词
