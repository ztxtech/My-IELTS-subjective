# Saying thank you

## 题目

1. Have you ever sent a thank you card to others?
2. Do people in my country often say thank you?
3. On what occasions do I say thank you?
4. Why do people need to say thank you?

## 回答

> Have you ever sent a thank you card to others?
> 你曾经给别人寄过感谢卡吗？

A: **Yes, I have.** I sent one to my professor last month to thank him for his help with my research paper. It was a small card but I wrote a heartfelt message inside.
是的，我寄过。上个月我给教授寄了一张，感谢他在我的研究论文上给予的帮助。那是张小卡片，但我在里面写了真诚的留言。

> Do people in my country often say thank you?
> 在我国家，人们经常说谢谢吗？

A: **Definitely.** Saying "thank you" is very common in daily life. People say it when receiving help, gifts, or even small favors like holding the door open.
当然。在日常生活中说“谢谢”非常普遍。人们在得到帮助、收到礼物，甚至像有人帮忙开门这样的小事时都会说。

> On what occasions do I say thank you?
> 我在什么场合会说谢谢？

A: **I say thank you in many situations.** For example, when someone gives me a gift, helps me with homework, or treats me to a meal. I also say it to bus drivers when I get off the bus.
我在很多场合都会说谢谢。比如，当有人送我礼物、帮我做作业或请我吃饭时。我下车时也会对公交车司机说谢谢。

> Why do people need to say thank you?
> 人们为什么需要说谢谢？

A: **I think** it shows appreciation and makes others feel valued. Saying thank you helps build good relationships and creates a positive atmosphere between people.
我认为这能表达感激之情，让别人感到被重视。说谢谢有助于建立良好的人际关系，并在人与人之间营造积极的氛围。

## 分析

### 思路与结构

1. 每个回答都先直接回应问题（Yes/No/Definitely/I think），然后提供具体例子或解释
2. 使用个人经历（给教授寄卡）和文化观察（公交车司机）使答案更真实可信
3. 结尾总结强调感谢的重要性，使回答完整

### 话题词汇/短语

1. `thank you card` - 感谢卡
2. `heartfelt message` - 真诚的留言  
3. `show appreciation` - 表达感激
4. `build good relationships` - 建立良好关系

### 实用词汇/短语/句式

1. **Yes, I have** - 简单的肯定回答结构
2. **Definitely** - 表达非常肯定的态度
3. **For example** - 举例连接词
4. **I think** - 表达个人观点的开头方式
