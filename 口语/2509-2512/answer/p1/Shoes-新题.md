# Shoes

## 题目

1. Do you like buying shoes? How often?
2. Have you ever bought shoes online?
3. How much money do you usually spend on shoes?
4. Which do you prefer, fashionable shoes or comfortable shoes?

## 回答

> Do you like buying shoes? How often?
> 你喜欢买鞋吗？多久买一次？

A: **Yes, I do.** I enjoy shopping for new `shoes`. **I usually buy** a new pair every few months.
是的，我喜欢。我喜欢买新鞋。我通常每隔几个月买一双新的。

> Have you ever bought shoes online?
> 你曾经在网上买过鞋吗？

A: **Yes, definitely.** I've bought `sneakers` online before. **It's really convenient** because I can compare prices easily.
是的，当然。我以前在网上买过运动鞋。这真的很方便，因为我可以轻松比较价格。

> How much money do you usually spend on shoes?
> 你通常花多少钱买鞋？

A: **I'd say** around 300 to 500 yuan. **I don't like to spend** too much on `shoes` because I have other things to buy.
我想大概是300到500元。我不喜欢在鞋上花太多钱，因为我还有其他东西要买。

> Which do you prefer, fashionable shoes or comfortable shoes?
> 你更喜欢时尚的鞋还是舒适的鞋？

A: **I prefer** `comfortable shoes`. **Even though** fashionable ones look nice, **I think** comfort is more important for everyday wear.
我更喜欢舒适的鞋。虽然时尚的鞋看起来不错，但我认为日常穿着舒适更重要。
