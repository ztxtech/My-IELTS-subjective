# Singing

## 题目

1. Do you like singing? Why?
2. Have you ever learnt how to sing?
3. Who do you want to sing for?
4. Do you think singing can bring happiness to people?

## 回答

> Do you like singing? Why?
> 你喜欢唱歌吗？为什么？

A: **Yes, I really enjoy singing.** It helps me relax and feel happy when I'm stressed.
是的，我真的很喜欢唱歌。当我感到有压力时，它帮助我放松并感到快乐。

> Have you ever learnt how to sing?
> 你曾经学过唱歌吗？

A: **No, I haven't had any formal lessons,** but I sometimes practice by myself while listening to music.
没有，我没有上过正式的课，但我有时在听音乐的时候自己练习。

> Who do you want to sing for?
> 你想为谁唱歌？

A: **I'd love to sing for my close friends and family** because they always support me and make me feel confident.
我很乐意为我的亲密朋友和家人唱歌，因为他们总是支持我，让我感到自信。

> Do you think singing can bring happiness to people?
> 你认为唱歌能给人带来快乐吗？

A: **Definitely, singing is a great way to express emotions** and it can make both the singer and listeners feel joyful.
当然，唱歌是表达情感的好方法，它能让唱歌的人和听歌的人都感到快乐。

## 分析

### 思路与结构

1. 每个回答都直接回应问题，结构简单清晰
2. 使用"yes/no + 原因/细节"的基本结构
3. 结尾都提供了简单的解释或例子

### 话题词汇/短语

1. `enjoy singing` - 喜欢唱歌
2. `formal lessons` - 正式课程
3. `practice by myself` - 自己练习
4. `express emotions` - 表达情感

### 实用词汇/短语/句式

1. **It helps me...** - 它帮助我...
2. **I haven't had...** - 我没有...
3. **I'd love to...** - 我很乐意...
4. **Definitely,...** - 当然...
