# Weather

## 题目

1. What's the weather like where you live?
2. Do you prefer cold or hot weather?
3. Do you prefer dry or wet weather?
4. Are you in the habit of checking the weather forecast? When and how often?
5. What do you think are the effects of climate change in recent years?
6. Would you like to visit other cities that have different climates from where you live?

## 回答

> What's the weather like where you live?
> **你**住的地方天气怎么样？

A: Where I live, the weather is often `cloudy` and a bit `wet`. In `summer`, it can be quite `hot`, but in `winter`, it's not too `cold`. So, it's a `comfortable` place most of the time.
**我**住的地方，天气常常是**多云**，而且有点**潮湿**。**夏天**会很**热**，但**冬天**又不太**冷**。所以，大部分时候**这**是一个**舒适**的地方。

---

### 分析

#### 思路与结构

1.  **开头**直接回答天气特点，点出**多云**和**潮湿**。
2.  **接着**分别描述**夏天**和**冬天**的温度情况，形成对比。
3.  **最后**用一个总结性的词**描述**整体感受，让回答更完整。

#### 话题词汇/短语

1.  `cloudy`: **多云的** (Describes the sky)
2.  `wet`: **潮湿的** (Describes the amount of rain or moisture)
3.  `summer`: **夏天** (A season)
4.  `hot`: **热的** (Describes temperature)
5.  `winter`: **冬天** (A season)
6.  `cold`: **冷的** (Describes temperature)
7.  `comfortable`: **舒适的** (Describes a pleasant feeling)

#### 实用词汇/短语/句式

1.  **Where I live, ...**: **在**我住的地方，**…** (一种**介绍**地点，然后描述情况的**常用**句式)
2.  **often**: **经常** (表示**频率**的词)
3.  **a bit**: **一点点** (表示**程度**的词，让描述更**自然**)
4.  **In summer/winter, ...**: **在夏天**/**冬天**，**…** (**介绍**季节，然后描述情况的**常用**句式)
5.  **can be quite ...**: **可能**会相当**…** (表示**可能性**和**程度**，让描述更**准确**)
6.  **but ... not too ...**: 但**是**…**不太**… (**连接**两个**相反**或**对比**的**情况**，让句子更**丰富**)
7.  **So, ... most of the time**: **所以**，**…**大部分时候**…** (用 **“So”** 做**总结**，**“most of the time”** 表示**普遍**情况)
