# Praise/Encouragement

## 题目

1. Have you had any achievements recently?
2. How do you feel when you are praised?
3. When was the last time you praised someone?
4. Do you think parents should often praise and encourage their children? Have you ever been praised or encouraged by your teacher?

## 回答

> Have you had any achievements recently?
> 你最近有什么成就吗？

A: **Yes, actually.** I finished a `research paper` for school. It was about `time series analysis`. **I worked really hard on it.**
是的，我最近完成了学校的一篇研究论文。是关于时间序列分析的。我为此付出了很多努力。

> How do you feel when you are praised?
> 当你受到表扬时，你感觉如何？

A: **It feels great!** Praise makes me feel happy and `motivated`. **For example,** when my teacher says "good job", I want to work even harder.
感觉非常好！表扬让我感到开心和有动力。比如，当我的老师说"做得好"时，我就想更加努力。

> When was the last time you praised someone?
> 你上次表扬别人是什么时候？

A: **Just yesterday.** I told my roommate that his new `photo` looked amazing. **He's really good at** `photography`.
就在昨天。我告诉我的室友他的新照片看起来很棒。他真的很擅长摄影。

> Do you think parents should often praise and encourage their children? Have you ever been praised or encouraged by your teacher?
> 你认为父母应该经常表扬和鼓励孩子吗？你有被老师表扬或鼓励过吗？

A: **Definitely yes.** Praise helps children feel `confident` and `happy`. **And yes,** my teacher often encourages me when I do well in class.
当然应该。表扬能帮助孩子感到自信和快乐。是的，当我在课堂上表现好时，我的老师经常鼓励我。

## 分析

### 思路与结构

1. 每个回答都先直接回应问题，然后提供具体例子或原因
2. 使用简单的连接词如"for example"和"and"来组织思路
3. 保持回答简短但完整，每问3-4句话

### 话题词汇/短语

1. `research paper` - 研究论文
2. `time series analysis` - 时间序列分析  
3. `motivated` - 有动力的
4. `photo` - 照片
5. `photography` - 摄影
6. `confident` - 自信的
7. `happy` - 快乐的

### 实用词汇/短语/句式

1. **Yes, actually.** - 用于开始回答，表示确认
2. **I worked really hard on it.** - 说明付出的努力
3. **It feels great!** - 表达积极感受
4. **For example,** - 举例说明
5. **Just yesterday.** - 具体说明时间
6. **He's really good at** - 称赞别人的能力
7. **Definitely yes.** - 强烈同意
8. **And yes,** - 连接两个相关问题的回答
