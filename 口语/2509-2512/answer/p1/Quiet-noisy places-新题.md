# Quiet/noisy places

## 题目

1. Do you like quiet or noisy places?
2. Would you like to go to quiet or noisy places on weekends when you are free?

## 回答

> Do you like quiet or noisy places?
> (你喜欢安静的地方还是嘈杂的地方？)

A: **I prefer** quiet places **because** they help me relax and focus. **For example**, I often study in the library where it's peaceful. **Also**, I find noisy places **a bit** tiring **after a while**.  
(我更喜欢安静的地方，因为它们能帮助我放松和集中注意力。例如，我经常在安静的图书馆学习。而且，我觉得嘈杂的地方待久了会有点累。)

> Would you like to go to quiet or noisy places on weekends when you are free?
> (周末有空的时候，你愿意去安静的地方还是嘈杂的地方？)

A: **On weekends**, I usually choose quiet places **to recharge**. **Sometimes** I go to a park or a cafe **where** it's not too loud. **However, if** my friends want to hang out somewhere lively, I **don't mind** joining them.  
(周末我通常会选择安静的地方来放松。有时我会去公园或不太吵闹的咖啡馆。不过，如果朋友们想去热闹的地方，我也不介意一起去。)
