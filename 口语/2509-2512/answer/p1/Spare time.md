# Spare time

## 题目

1. Do you often have free time?
2. What do you usually do in your spare time?
3. Which day do you have more free time on, Saturday or Sunday?
4. Would you like to have more free time in the future?

## 回答

> Do you often have free time?
> 你经常有空闲时间吗？

A: **Yes, I usually have some free time** in the evenings and on weekends. **I think** it's important to relax after studying.
是的，我通常在晚上和周末有一些空闲时间。我觉得学习后放松很重要。

> What do you usually do in your spare time?
> 你在空闲时间通常做什么？

A: **I often** listen to music or do some `baking`. **Sometimes I also** go to the `gym` near my school.
我经常听音乐或做些烘焙。有时我也会去学校附近的健身房。

> Which day do you have more free time on, Saturday or Sunday?
> 周六和周日，你哪一天有更多空闲时间？

A: **I have more free time on** Sunday **because** I usually finish my homework on Saturday. **So** Sunday is better for relaxing.
我周日有更多空闲时间，因为我通常在周六完成作业。所以周日更适合放松。

> Would you like to have more free time in the future?
> 你将来想要有更多空闲时间吗？

A: **Yes, definitely. I'd love to have** more time for my `hobbies` like photography. **But I also know** I need to focus on my studies.
是的，当然。我想要有更多时间给我的爱好，比如摄影。但我也知道我需要专注于学习。

## 分析

### 思路与结构

1. 先直接回答问题，然后补充简单理由
2. 使用具体活动举例说明（烘焙、健身）
3. 通过对比说明时间安排（周六写作业，周日休息）
4. 表达愿望的同时保持平衡（想要更多时间但也知道要学习）

### 话题词汇/短语

1. `baking` - 烘焙
2. `gym` - 健身房
3. `hobbies` - 爱好

### 实用词汇/短语/句式

1. **Yes, I usually** - 是的，我通常...
2. **I think** - 我认为
3. **I often** - 我经常
4. **Sometimes I also** - 有时我也
5. **I have more free time on** - 我在...有更多空闲时间
6. **because** - 因为
7. **So** - 所以
8. **Yes, definitely. I'd love to** - 是的，当然。我想要...
9. **But I also know** - 但我也知道
