# Public Places

## 题目

1. Have you ever talked with someone you don't know in public places?
2. Do you wear headphones in public places?

## 回答

> Have you ever talked with someone you don't know in public places?
> 你在公共场所和陌生人说过话吗？

A: **Yes, sometimes**. For example, **when** I was waiting in line at a coffee shop, I chatted with a person about the weather. It was a short but nice conversation.
(是的，有时候。比如，我在咖啡店排队时，和一个人聊了聊天气。虽然简短但很愉快。)

> Do you wear headphones in public places?
> 你在公共场所戴耳机吗？

A: **Yes, I often do**. **I usually** wear them when I'm on the bus or walking to class. **It helps me** relax and enjoy my music without being disturbed.
(是的，我经常戴。我通常在坐公交或去上课的路上戴耳机。这能帮助我放松，享受音乐而不被打扰。)

## 分析

### 思路与结构

1. 使用"Yes, sometimes/Yes, I often do"直接回答问题
2. 用"for example/when"引出具体例子
3. 用"It was/It helps me"简单描述感受或原因

### 话题词汇/短语

1. `public places` (公共场所)
2. `coffee shop` (咖啡店)
3. `headphones` (耳机)
4. `on the bus` (在公交车上)

### 实用词汇/短语/句式

1. **Yes, sometimes** (是的，有时候)
2. **For example** (例如)
3. **I usually** (我通常)
4. **It helps me** (这帮助我)

---

**提示**: 记住使用简单的时间状语从句(when...)和原因状语从句(because...)来扩展你的回答，这会让你的表达更自然流畅
