# Working with old people

## 题目

1. Have you ever worked with old people?
2. Are you happy to work with people who are older than you?
3. What are the advantages of working with old people?

## 回答

> Have you ever worked with old people?
> 你曾经和老年人一起工作过吗？

A: **Yes, I have.** I worked with my professor on a research project. He is over 60 years old. **It was a great experience.**
是的，我有过。我和我的教授一起做一个研究项目。他60多岁了。那是一次很棒的经历。

> Are you happy to work with people who are older than you?
> 你愿意和比你年长的人一起工作吗？

A: **Definitely, yes.** Older people have a lot of experience. **They can teach me many things.** I really enjoy learning from them.
当然愿意。年长的人有很多经验。他们可以教我很多东西。我真的很喜欢向他们学习。

> What are the advantages of working with old people?
> 和老年人一起工作有什么好处？

A: **Well, there are several advantages.** First, they are very patient and wise. Also, they share interesting stories about the past. **This makes work more enjoyable.**
嗯，有几个好处。首先，他们非常耐心和智慧。而且，他们会分享关于过去的趣事。这让工作更有趣。

## 分析

### 思路与结构

1. 第一题：直接回答+具体例子+个人感受
2. 第二题：明确态度+理由+具体好处
3. 第三题：总起句+两个具体优点+总结感受

### 话题词汇/短语

1. `research project` (研究项目)
2. `experience` (经验)
3. `patient and wise` (耐心和智慧)
4. `interesting stories` (有趣的故事)

### 实用词汇/短语/句式

1. **Yes, I have.** (是的，我有过) - 肯定回答的简单句式
2. **It was a great experience.** (那是一次很棒的经历) - 表达积极体验
3. **Definitely, yes.** (当然愿意) - 强调肯定态度
4. **They can teach me many things.** (他们可以教我很多东西) - 表达学习机会
5. **Well, there are several advantages.** (嗯，有几个好处) - 列举优点的开头句式
6. **This makes work more enjoyable.** (这让工作更有趣) - 总结好处
