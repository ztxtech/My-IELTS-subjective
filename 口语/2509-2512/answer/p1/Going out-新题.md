# Going out

## 题目

1. Do you bring food or snacks with you when going out?
2. Do you always take your mobile phone with you when going out?

## 回答

> Do you bring food or snacks with you when going out?
> 你出门时会带食物或零食吗？

A: **Yes, I usually** pack some small snacks like nuts or fruits. **It's because** I get hungry easily when I'm out. **For example**, if I'm shopping or studying in a cafe, having a snack helps me stay focused. **So**, I almost always have something with me.
是的，我通常会带一些小零食，比如坚果或水果。这是因为我外出时容易饿。例如，如果我在购物或在咖啡馆学习，吃点零食能帮助我保持专注。所以，我几乎总是随身带些东西。

> Do you always take your mobile phone with you when going out?
> 你出门时总是带手机吗？

A: **Definitely, I can't imagine** leaving home without my phone. **It's essential for** staying connected with friends and family. **Also**, I use it for maps, taking photos, or even paying for things. **So**, it's always in my pocket or bag.
当然，我无法想象不带手机出门。它对与朋友和家人保持联系至关重要。而且，我用它来看地图、拍照，甚至付款。所以，它总是在我的口袋或包里。

## 分析

### 思路与结构

1. 第一题：直接肯定回答 → 解释原因（容易饿）→ 举例说明（购物/学习）→ 总结习惯
2. 第二题：强烈肯定回答 → 说明重要性（联系他人）→ 补充用途（导航/拍照/支付）→ 强调随身携带

### 话题词汇/短语

1. `pack snacks`（带零食）
2. `stay focused`（保持专注）
3. `staying connected`（保持联系）
4. `paying for things`（付款）
