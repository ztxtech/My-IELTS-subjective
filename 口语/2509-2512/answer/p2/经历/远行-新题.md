# A Long Journey

## 题目

### P2

Describe a long journey you had
You should say:
Where you went
Who you had the journey with
Why you had the journey
And explain how you felt about the journey

### P3

1. How easy is it to travel around your country?

## 回答

> Describe a long journey you had
> 描述你的一次长途旅行

A:

1. 直接回答旅行地点和同伴
**Well, I'd like to talk about** my trip to **Chengdu** last summer. **I went there with** my roommate, Ruijie Liu.
(嗯，我想聊聊去年夏天去成都的旅行。我和我的室友刘瑞杰一起去的。)

2. 解释旅行原因
**The main reason for** this journey **was that** we wanted to experience the famous Sichuan food and see the pandas.
(这次旅行的主要原因是想体验著名的四川美食和看熊猫。)

3. 描述旅行感受-积极方面
**I felt really excited during** the journey because everything was new and interesting. **For example**, the hotpot was delicious and the city was very lively.
(旅途中我感到非常兴奋，因为一切都很新奇有趣。比如，火锅很好吃，城市也很热闹。)

4. 描述旅行感受-具体经历
**What made it special was** that we visited many local markets and tried different street foods. **It was** like a food adventure.
(特别的是我们去了很多当地市场，尝试了不同的街头小吃。就像一次美食探险。)

5. 总结感受
**Overall, I had** a wonderful time **and** this journey made me love traveling even more.
(总的来说，我玩得很开心，这次旅行让我更加喜欢旅行了。)

> How easy is it to travel around your country?
> 在你的国家旅行有多容易？

A:

1. 表达观点
**I think** traveling around China **is quite convenient** because we have good transportation systems.
(我认为在中国旅行相当方便，因为我们有很好的交通系统。)

2. 解释原因
**This is mainly because** there are many high-speed trains and buses connecting different cities.
(这主要是因为有很多高铁和公交车连接不同的城市。)

3. 举例说明
**For instance**, last year I took a train from my hometown to Chengdu, and it only took a few hours.
(例如，去年我从家乡坐火车到成都，只花了几个小时。)

## 分析

### 话题词汇/短语

1. `long journey` - 长途旅行
2. `Chengdu` - 成都
3. `Sichuan food` - 四川食物
4. `high-speed trains` - 高速火车
5. `transportation systems` - 交通系统

### 实用词汇/短语/句式

1. **I'd like to talk about** - 我想聊聊...
2. **The main reason for...was that** - ...的主要原因是
3. **I felt really excited during** - 在...期间我感到很兴奋
4. **What made it special was** - 让它特别的是
5. **I think...is quite convenient** - 我认为...相当方便
