## 题目

Why you had the journey

1. How easy is it to travel around your country?

## 回答

> Describe a long journey you had  
> 描述一次你经历过的远行  

A:

1. 回应题目  
**One** **long journey** I had was from **Chengdu** to **Chizhou**, my **hometown**, by high-speed train.  
我有一次从**成都**回**池州**老家的**长途旅行**，坐的是高铁。

2. 人物  
I made the trip with my **roommate**, **Ruijie Liu**, who wanted to see the famous **Jiuhua Mountain** nearby.  
我和**室友刘睿杰**一起去的，他想看看附近的**九华山**。

3. 原因  
We took the journey during the **winter holiday** to visit my **family** and to take **photos** of the **mountain** with my **Nikon Z30 camera**.  
我们寒假去是为了看望**家人**，还想用我的**尼康Z30相机**拍**山景**。

4. 感受1  
I felt **excited** because the **scenery** along the way was **amazing** and the train was **fast and clean**.  
我很**兴奋**，因为沿途**风景**太美了，而且高铁又快又干净。

5. 感受2  
Also, sharing snacks and laughing together made the **long hours** feel **short and sweet**.  
还有，我们一路分享零食、说说笑笑，让**漫长的时光**变得**短暂而甜蜜**。

6. 观点1  
The **journey** showed me that **traveling** with a good **friend** can turn simple **transport** into a fun **adventure**.  
这次**远行**让我明白，和好朋友一起，普通的**交通**也能变成有趣的**冒险**。

7. 观点2  
It also reminded me how **lucky** I am to live in a country where **high-speed trains** connect cities so **easily**.  
它也让我意识到，生活在一个有**高铁**把城市连得如此**便捷**的国家，是多么**幸运**。

8. 总结  
So, that **long trip** home was not only about **moving** from A to B, but about **memories**, **friendship**, and **love** for my **hometown**.  
所以，那次回家的**长途旅行**不仅是**移动**，更是**回忆**、**友谊**和对**家乡**的**爱**。

---

> How easy is it to travel around your country?  
> 在你的国家旅行方便吗？

A:

1. 观点  
It’s **very easy** to travel around **China** today.  
在中国旅行**非常方便**。

2. 原因  
Because we have **high-speed trains** that link almost every **big city**, and **cheap flights** if you need to go **far**.  
因为几乎每个**大城市**都有**高铁**相连，去**远的地方**还有**便宜的航班**。

3. 例子  
For **instance**, I can leave **Chengdu** in the morning and be in **Beijing** for **dinner**, all in one day.  
比如，我早上从**成都**出发，晚上就能到**北京**吃晚饭。

6. **scenery** – 风景
