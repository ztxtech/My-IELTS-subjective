# 决意等待

## 题目

### P2

Describe a time you made a decision to wait for something You should say: When it happened What you waited for Why you made the decision And explain how you felt while waiting

### P3

1. What do people in your country often do while waiting?
2. Why do some people like a slow-paced life? Is being patient good for people? Why?
3. Are people less patient now than people in the past? Why?
4. Why do children lack patience?
5. How can people become more patient?

## 回答

> Describe a time you made a decision to wait for something
> 描述一次你决定等待某事的经历

A:

1. (直接回应题目，说明时间和事件)
**I'd like to talk about** the time I waited for my research paper to be accepted. This happened last year when I was working on a `time series` project with my professor.

我想谈谈我等待研究论文被接受的那次。这是去年我和我的教授做一个时间序列项目时发生的。

2. (说明等待的具体内容)
**What I waited for was** the acceptance letter from an international journal. I had spent months working on this paper with my supervisor, Professor Deng.

我等待的是国际期刊的录用通知。我和我的导师邓教授花了几个月时间完成这篇论文。

3. (解释做出等待决定的原因)
**The reason I decided to wait patiently was** because I knew the review process takes time. My professor told me that good research needs careful checking by experts.

我决定耐心等待是因为我知道审稿过程需要时间。我的教授告诉我，好的研究需要专家仔细检查。

4. (描述等待期间的具体感受)
**While waiting, I felt** quite anxious at first. I checked my email every day, hoping for good news. Sometimes I couldn't sleep well because I was thinking about it.

等待期间，起初我感到相当焦虑。我每天检查邮件，希望有好消息。有时我睡不好，因为一直在想这件事。

5. (描述感受的变化)
**However, as time passed**, I learned to be more calm. I focused on my other studies and hobbies like `photography` to keep myself busy.

然而，随着时间的推移，我学会了更加冷静。我专注于其他学习和爱好，比如摄影，让自己保持忙碌。

6. (说明最终的积极结果)
**In the end**, the waiting was worth it. When the acceptance email finally came, I felt extremely happy and proud of our work.

最终，等待是值得的。当录用邮件终于到来时，我感到非常高兴并为我们的工作感到骄傲。

7. (总结经历的意义)
**This experience taught me** that patience is important for achieving important goals. Good things often take time to happen.

这次经历教会我，耐心对于实现重要目标很重要。好事往往需要时间才能发生。

> What do people in your country often do while waiting?
> 在你们国家，人们等待时经常做什么？

A:

1. (直接回答普遍行为)
**In China, people often use their phones** while waiting. They might check social media, watch short videos, or play mobile games.

在中国，人们等待时经常使用手机。他们可能会查看社交媒体、看短视频或玩手机游戏。

2. (补充其他常见活动)
**Some people also** read books or listen to music. In waiting rooms, you'll see many people wearing headphones and looking at their screens.

有些人也读书或听音乐。在等候室里，你会看到很多人戴耳机看屏幕。

3. (举例说明具体场景)
**For example**, when waiting for public transportation, most people stand there scrolling through their phones to pass the time.

例如，等公共交通时，大多数人站在那里刷手机来消磨时间。

> Why do some people like a slow-paced life? Is being patient good for people? Why?
> 为什么有些人喜欢慢节奏生活？耐心对人有益吗？为什么？

A:

1. (回答喜欢慢生活的原因)
**Some people prefer slow-paced life** because it's less stressful. They have more time to enjoy simple things and don't feel rushed.

有些人喜欢慢节奏生活是因为压力较小。他们有更多时间享受简单的事物，不会感到匆忙。

2. (直接回答耐心是否有益)
**Yes, being patient is definitely good** for people. It helps us make better decisions and avoid mistakes.

是的，耐心绝对对人有益。它帮助我们做出更好的决定并避免错误。

3. (解释耐心的好处)
**When we are patient**, we can think more clearly and handle difficult situations better. This leads to better outcomes in work and relationships.

当我们有耐心时，我们能更清晰地思考并更好地处理困难情况。这能在工作和人际关系中带来更好的结果。

> Are people less patient now than people in the past? Why?
> 现在的人比过去的人更缺乏耐心吗？为什么？

A:

1. (直接回答比较问题)
**I think people are indeed less patient** nowadays compared to the past. Technology has made us expect instant results.

我认为现在的人确实比过去更缺乏耐心。科技让我们期待即时结果。

2. (解释主要原因)
**Because everything is faster now** - we get instant messages, fast food, and quick deliveries. This makes us used to getting what we want immediately.

因为现在一切都更快——我们得到即时消息、快餐和快速配送。这让我们习惯于立即得到想要的东西。

3. (举例说明)
**For example**, if a website takes more than a few seconds to load, most people will just close it and move on. In the past, people would wait much longer.

例如，如果一个网站加载需要超过几秒钟，大多数人就会关闭它并继续前进。过去，人们会等待更久。

> Why do children lack patience?
> 为什么儿童缺乏耐心？

A:

1. (直接回答原因)
**Children lack patience mainly because** they haven't learned self-control yet. Their brains are still developing this ability.

儿童缺乏耐心主要是因为他们还没有学会自我控制。他们的大脑仍在发展这种能力。

2. (解释发展阶段)
**At young ages**, children live in the present moment and want immediate satisfaction. They don't understand that some things take time.

在年幼时，儿童活在当下并想要即时满足。他们不理解有些事情需要时间。

3. (举例说明)
**For instance**, a toddler will cry if they don't get a toy right away. They haven't developed the understanding that waiting is sometimes necessary.

例如，幼儿如果没能立即得到玩具就会哭。他们还没有发展出理解等待有时是必要的。

> How can people become more patient?
> 人们如何能变得更有耐心？

A:

1. (提出具体方法)
**People can become more patient by** practicing mindfulness. This means focusing on the present moment instead of worrying about the future.

人们可以通过练习正念变得更有耐心。这意味着专注于当下而不是担心未来。

2. (建议实际活动)
**They can also try** activities that require waiting, like gardening or baking. These hobbies teach us that good results take time.

他们也可以尝试需要等待的活动，比如园艺或烘焙。这些爱好教会我们好的结果需要时间。

3. (强调练习的重要性)
**Like any skill**, patience gets better with practice. The more we consciously choose to wait calmly, the more patient we become.

像任何技能一样，耐心通过练习会变得更好。我们越有意识地选择冷静等待，就越有耐心。
