# 告诉朋友实情

## 题目

### P2

Describe a time when you told your friend an important truth. You should say: Who your friend is, What the truth was, What your friend's reaction was, And explain why you think it was important to tell your friend the truth

### P3

1. Do you think it's more important to win a game or follow the rules in sports?
2. Sometimes people should tell lies. Do you agree?
3. How do you know when others are telling lies?
4. Do you think we should tell the truth at all times?

## 回答

> Describe a time when you told your friend an important truth. You should say: Who your friend is, What the truth was, What your friend's reaction was, And explain why you think it was important to tell your friend the truth
> 描述一次你告诉朋友一个重要真相的经历。你应该说：你的朋友是谁，真相是什么，你朋友的反应是什么，并解释为什么你认为告诉朋友这个真相很重要

A:

1. 介绍朋友和事件背景 (中文思路)
**Well, I want to talk about my roommate, Ruijie Liu.** He is my good friend at university. We study computer science together and live in the same dorm.
(嗯，我想谈谈我的室友刘瑞杰。他是我在大学的好朋友。我们一起学习计算机科学，住在同一个宿舍。)

2. 说明真相内容 (中文思路)
**One day, I had to tell him an important truth.** I accidentally broke his favorite guitar pick while cleaning our room. I felt really bad about it.
(有一天，我不得不告诉他一个重要的真相。我在打扫房间时不小心弄坏了他最喜欢的吉他拨片。我对此感到非常抱歉。)

3. 描述朋友的反应 (中文思路)
**His reaction was actually quite understanding.** At first, he looked a bit disappointed, but then he smiled and said, "It's okay, these things happen. Thanks for being honest with me."
(他的反应其实很理解。起初他看起来有点失望，但随后他笑着说："没关系，这种事情难免发生。谢谢你对我诚实。")

4. 解释为什么重要 (中文思路)
**I think it was important to tell him the truth because** trust is the foundation of friendship. If I had hidden it, he might have found out later and felt worse. **Also,** being honest shows respect for our friendship.
(我认为告诉他真相很重要，因为信任是友谊的基础。如果我隐瞒了，他可能以后会发现并感觉更糟。而且，诚实表明对我们友谊的尊重。)

> 1. Do you think it's more important to win a game or follow the rules in sports?
> 1. 你认为在体育比赛中赢得比赛更重要还是遵守规则更重要？

5. 表达观点 (中文思路)
**In my opinion,** following the rules is more important than winning. **Because** sports are about fair play and respect. If you break rules to win, it's not a real victory.
(在我看来，遵守规则比赢得比赛更重要。因为体育关乎公平竞争和尊重。如果你违反规则获胜，那不是真正的胜利。)

> 2. Sometimes people should tell lies. Do you agree?
> 2. 有时候人们应该说谎。你同意吗？

6. 表达有条件同意 (中文思路)
**I agree, but only in special situations.** For example, if telling the truth might hurt someone's feelings badly, a small lie might be kinder. **However,** most of the time, honesty is better.
(我同意，但仅限于特殊情况。例如，如果说真话可能会严重伤害某人的感情，那么一个小小的谎言可能更友善。然而，大多数时候，诚实更好。)

> 3. How do you know when others are telling lies?
> 3. 你怎么知道别人在说谎？

7. 说明识别方法 (中文思路)
**There are some signs that might show someone is lying.** They might avoid eye contact, speak nervously, or give answers that don't match. **But** it's not always easy to tell for sure.
(有一些迹象可能表明有人在说谎。他们可能会避免眼神接触，紧张地说话，或者给出不匹配的答案。但并不总是容易确定。)

> 4. Do you think we should tell the truth at all times?
> 4. 你认为我们应该始终说实话吗？

8. 表达平衡观点 (中文思路)
**I don't think we should tell the truth at all times.** While honesty is important, sometimes we need to consider people's feelings. **For example,** if a friend asks about their new haircut and it doesn't look good, a gentle response might be better than harsh truth.
(我认为我们不应该始终说实话。虽然诚实很重要，但有时我们需要考虑人们的感受。例如，如果朋友问他们的新发型怎么样，而看起来不好看，温和的回应可能比严厉的真相更好。)
