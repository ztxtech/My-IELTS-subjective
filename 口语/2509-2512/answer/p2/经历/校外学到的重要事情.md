>    你认为有些孩子行为良好是因为他们受到父母的影响吗？

A:

1.  （回答目的：直接肯定，并强调父母影响的重要性）

`**Yes, I definitely think so.**` `Parents` are the `first teachers` for children, and they `spend` the `most time` with them. `**So,**` what `parents do` and `say` has a `big effect` on how children behave.
是的，我绝对这样认为。父母是孩子们的第一任老师，他们与孩子们相处的时间最长。所以，父母的言行对孩子的行为有很大的影响。

`**Children look up to their parents**` and `**try to be like them.**` If `parents` are `polite`, `kind`, and `respectful`, children are `more likely to be` `polite`, `kind`, and `respectful` too. `**It's like children are mirrors**` `reflecting` their `parents' actions`.
