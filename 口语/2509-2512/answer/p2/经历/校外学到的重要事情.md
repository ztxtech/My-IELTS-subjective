> 1. What can children learn from parents?
> 1. 孩子们能从父母那里学到什么？

A:

1. (直接回答)
**Children can learn many important things from their parents, like good `manners` and how to be `responsible`.** 
孩子们可以从父母那里学到很多重要的东西，比如好的礼貌和如何负责任。

2. (举例说明)
**For example, parents can teach children to say "please" and "thank you," and to clean up their toys after playing.** 
例如，父母可以教孩子们说"请"和"谢谢"，并在玩完后收拾玩具。

3. (进一步解释)
**These basic skills help children become better people when they grow up.** 
这些基本技能帮助孩子们长大后成为更好的人。

> 2. Do you think some children are well-behaved because they are influenced by their parents?
> 2. 你认为一些孩子行为良好是因为受到父母的影响吗？

A:

1. (表达观点)
**Yes, definitely. I think parents have a big `influence` on how children behave.** 
是的，当然。我认为父母对孩子的行为有很大的影响。

2. (解释原因)
**Children often copy what their parents do, so if parents are kind and polite, children will learn to be the same.** 
孩子们经常模仿父母的行为，所以如果父母善良有礼貌，孩子们也会学会这样。

3. (总结)
**That's why good `role models` at home are so important for children's development.** 
这就是为什么家里有好榜样对孩子的成长如此重要。

> 3. Is it necessary for adults to learn new things? How can people learn new things?
> 3. 成年人有必要学习新东西吗？人们如何学习新东西？

A:

1. (回答问题)
**Yes, I think it's very necessary for adults to keep learning new things.** 
是的，我认为成年人继续学习新东西非常必要。

2. (解释原因)
**The world is always changing, so learning helps adults stay `relevant` in their jobs and personal lives.** 
世界总是在变化，所以学习帮助成年人在工作和个人生活中保持相关性。

3. (回答第二部分)
**People can learn through `online courses`, reading books, or by trying new `hobbies` and activities.** 
人们可以通过在线课程、阅读书籍或尝试新的爱好和活动来学习。

> 4. Does the internet make learning easier or more difficult?
> 4. 互联网让学习更容易还是更困难？

A:

1. (表达观点)
**In my opinion, the internet mostly makes learning easier.** 
在我看来，互联网主要让学习更容易。

2. (解释原因)
**Now we can find information on any topic quickly, and there are many free educational `resources` available online.** 
现在我们可以快速找到任何主题的信息，并且网上有很多免费的教育资源。

3. (承认另一面)
**However, sometimes there is too much information, which can be confusing if you don't know what to trust.** 
然而，有时信息太多，如果你不知道相信什么，可能会让人困惑。

> 5. Can people acquire more knowledge now than before?
> 5. 人们现在能比过去获得更多知识吗？

A:

1. (直接回答)
**Yes, absolutely. People today have access to much more knowledge than in the past.** 
是的，绝对是的。今天的人们比过去能接触到更多的知识。

2. (解释原因)
**Because of the internet and technology, we can learn from experts around the world anytime we want.** 
因为互联网和技术，我们可以随时向世界各地的专家学习。

3. (举例说明)
**For example, I can watch `video tutorials` to learn almost any skill, which wasn't possible 20 years ago.** 
例如，我可以通过观看视频教程来学习几乎任何技能，这在20年前是不可能的
