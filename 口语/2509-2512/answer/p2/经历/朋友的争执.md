# 朋友的争执

## 题目

### P2

Describe an argument two of your friends had You should say: When it happened What it was about How it was solved And how you felt about it

### P3

1. Do you think arguments are important?
2. What do family members usually have arguments about?
3. Is it easier for you to have arguments with your family or with your friends?
4. Do you think people should change the way they think when having arguments?
5. When two people have an argument, do you think they should find a third party to ask for advice?
6. What qualities make a good lawyer?

## 回答

> Describe an argument two of your friends had You should say: When it happened What it was about How it was solved And how you felt about it
> 描述你两个朋友之间的一次争执。你应该说：什么时候发生的，是关于什么的，是如何解决的，以及你对此的感受

A:

1. **直接回应问题，说明争执对象和时间** (开门见山说明是哪个朋友和什么时候)

I want to talk about an argument between my roommate `Ruijie Liu` and me. It happened last month in our dorm.

我想谈谈我的室友`刘瑞杰`和我之间的一次争执。发生在上个月在我们的宿舍。

2. **解释争执的原因** (说明具体争执内容)

The argument was about `music`. He loves playing guitar late at night, but I need to sleep early for class. I told him to stop, but he said music helps him relax.

争执是关于`音乐`的。他喜欢深夜弹吉他，但我需要早睡上课。我告诉他停止，但他说音乐帮助他放松。

3. **描述解决方案** (说明如何解决问题)

We solved it by `talking calmly`. I suggested he use headphones, and he agreed. Now he plays guitar quietly or goes to the music room.

我们通过`冷静交谈`解决了问题。我建议他使用耳机，他同意了。现在他安静地弹吉他或去音乐室。

4. **表达个人感受** (说明对此事的感受)

I felt `relieved` after we talked. It showed me that even good friends can have problems, but `communication` is key to solving them.

我们交谈后我感到`松了一口气`。这告诉我即使是好朋友也会有矛盾，但`沟通`是解决问题的关键。

> Do you think arguments are important?
> 你认为争执重要吗？

A:

1. **提出观点** (直接回答问题)

Yes, I think arguments are important sometimes.

是的，我认为争执有时是重要的。

2. **解释原因** (说明为什么重要)

They help people share different ideas and find better solutions.

它们帮助人们分享不同的想法并找到更好的解决方案。

3. **举例说明** (用具体例子支持观点)

For example, when my friend and I argued about music, we found a better way that works for both of us.

例如，当我和朋友为音乐争执时，我们找到了对双方都更好的方法。

---

> What do family members usually have arguments about?
> 家人通常因为什么而争执？

A:

1. **陈述常见争执主题** (列举家庭常见争执内容)

Family members often argue about small daily things.

家人经常为日常小事争执。

2. **具体举例** (给出具体例子)

For example, about housework, money, or what to watch on TV.

例如，关于家务、钱或者看什么电视节目。

3. **补充说明** (说明这些争执的性质)

These are usually not serious arguments, just normal family discussions.

这些通常不是严重的争执，只是正常的家庭讨论。

---

> Is it easier for you to have arguments with your family or with your friends?
> 与家人或朋友争执，哪个对你来说更容易？

A:

1. **表达个人偏好** (直接回答哪个更容易)

It's easier to argue with friends.

与朋友争执更容易。

2. **解释原因** (说明为什么)

With friends, I can speak more directly because we are at the same age.

与朋友一起，我可以更直接地说话，因为我们同龄。

3. **对比说明** (与家人对比)

With family, I need to be more careful and respectful.

与家人一起，我需要更加小心和尊重。

---

> Do you think people should change the way they think when having arguments?
> 你认为人们在争执时应该改变思维方式吗？

A:

1. **肯定回答** (直接表明态度)

Yes, definitely.

是的，当然。

2. **解释原因** (说明为什么需要改变)

People should try to understand the other person's view instead of just wanting to win.

人们应该尝试理解对方的观点，而不是只想赢。

3. **说明好处** (解释改变思维方式的好处)

This helps find real solutions and keeps relationships good.

这有助于找到真正的解决方案并保持关系良好。

---

> When two people have an argument, do you think they should find a third party to ask for advice?
> 当两个人发生争执时，你认为他们应该找第三方寻求建议吗？

A:

1. **表达观点** (直接回答问题)

Sometimes yes, but not always.

有时是的，但不总是。

2. **说明适用情况** (解释什么时候需要)

If they cannot solve it themselves, a third person can help see things more clearly.

如果他们自己无法解决，第三方可以帮助更清楚地看待事情。

3. **补充说明** (说明局限性)

But the two people should still try to talk directly first.

但两个人还是应该先尝试直接交谈。

---

> What qualities make a good lawyer?
> 什么样的品质能造就一个好律师？

A:

1. **列举重要品质** (直接说明关键品质)

A good lawyer needs to be smart and good at talking.

一个好律师需要聪明且善于交谈。

2. **详细解释** (解释这些品质的重要性)

They must understand laws well and explain things clearly to help their clients.

他们必须很好地理解法律并清晰地解释事情来帮助客户。

3. **补充其他品质** (添加其他重要品质)

Also, they should be honest and care about helping people.

此外，他们应该诚实并关心帮助他人。
