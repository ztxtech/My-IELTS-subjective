# 和他人一起计划活动

## 题目

### P2

Describe a time when you and some other people made a plan to do an activity You should say: What it was When and where you made it What the activity was And explain how you felt about the plan

### P3

1. What kind of plans do young people often make?
2. Why can't people always follow their plans?
3. Why do people make plans?
4. Is it important to make plans?
5. Why do some people like to make plans while others just don't like to? Are there any people who never make plans?

## 回答

> Describe a time when you and some other people made a plan to do an activity You should say: What it was When and where you made it What the activity was And explain how you felt about the plan
> 描述一次你和其他人一起计划活动的经历。你应该说：计划是什么；你们在何时何地制定的计划；活动内容是什么；并解释你对这个计划的感受

A:

1. 直接回答问题，说明计划内容
   
   I want to talk about a plan I made with my roommate `Ruijie Liu` to go to the gym together.
   
   我想说说我和室友刘瑞杰一起去健身房的计划。

2. 说明制定计划的时间和地点
   
   We made this plan last month in our dorm room after class.
   
   我们是上个月下课后在宿舍制定的这个计划。

3. 描述具体的活动内容
   
   The activity was going to the gym near our school three times a week. We planned to do weight training and cardio exercises.
   
   活动内容是每周去学校附近的健身房三次。我们计划进行力量训练和有氧运动。

4. 解释对计划的感受
   
   I felt really good about this plan because it helped me stay healthy and we could motivate each other. It was also fun to spend time with my friend.
   
   我对这个计划感觉很好，因为它帮助我保持健康，而且我们可以互相激励。和朋友一起度过时光也很有趣。

> 1. What kind of plans do young people often make?
> 1. 年轻人经常制定什么样的计划？

A:

1. 给出主要观点
   
   Young people often make plans for studying, exercising, and hanging out with friends.
   
   年轻人经常制定学习、锻炼和与朋友聚会的计划。

2. 提供具体例子
   
   For example, they plan study schedules for exams or make gym plans to stay fit.
   
   例如，他们为考试制定学习计划，或者制定健身计划来保持健康。

3. 补充其他常见计划
   
   They also plan weekend activities like going to movies or having dinner together.
   
   他们还会计划周末活动，比如看电影或一起吃饭。

> 2. Why can't people always follow their plans?
> 2. 为什么人们不能总是遵循他们的计划？

A:

1. 说明主要原因
   
   People can't always follow their plans because unexpected things happen.
   
   人们不能总是遵循计划，因为会发生意外的事情。

2. 举例说明
   
   For example, they might get sick or have sudden homework to do.
   
   例如，他们可能生病或者有突然的作业要做。

3. 补充其他原因
   
   Sometimes they feel tired or lazy and just don't want to do what they planned.
   
   有时他们感到疲倦或懒惰，就是不想做计划的事情。

> 3. Why do people make plans?
> 3. 为什么人们要制定计划？

A:

1. 给出主要原因
   
   People make plans to organize their time and achieve their goals.
   
   人们制定计划是为了组织时间和实现目标。

2. 解释具体好处
   
   Plans help them know what to do and when to do it, which reduces stress.
   
   计划帮助他们知道要做什么以及何时做，这样可以减少压力。

3. 说明最终目的
   
   Making plans also helps people be more productive and successful.
   
   制定计划还能帮助人们更高效和成功。

> 4. Is it important to make plans?
> 4. 制定计划重要吗？

A:

1. 直接回答重要性
   
   Yes, I think making plans is very important.
   
   是的，我认为制定计划非常重要。

2. 解释原因
   
   It helps people manage their time better and avoid forgetting important things.
   
   它帮助人们更好地管理时间，避免忘记重要的事情。

3. 说明好处
   
   Good plans can lead to better results and less last-minute stress.
   
   好的计划可以带来更好的结果，减少最后一刻的压力。

> 5. Why do some people like to make plans while others just don't like to? Are there any people who never make plans?
> 5. 为什么有些人喜欢制定计划而有些人不喜欢？有人从不制定计划吗？

A:

1. 解释性格差异
   
   Some people like making plans because they like being organized, while others don't like it because they prefer to be spontaneous.
   
   有些人喜欢制定计划是因为他们喜欢有条理，而有些人不喜欢是因为他们更喜欢随性。

2. 说明不同生活方式
   
   People who are very busy often make plans, but those with more free time might not feel the need.
   
   很忙的人经常制定计划，但空闲时间多的人可能觉得不需要。

3. 回答第二部分问题
   
   I think there are very few people who never make any plans at all. Everyone makes at least some simple plans.
   
   我认为几乎没有人从不制定任何计划。每个人至少会制定一些简单的计划。
