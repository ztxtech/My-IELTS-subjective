# 看过的体育比赛

## 题目

### P2

Describe a sports competition you watched You should say: What it was When/where you watched it Who you watched it with And explain why you watched it

### P3

1. What kind of competitions do people in your country like to watch? Why?
2. Do you think there should be more matches or competitions broadcast on TV? What is the most popular sport in China?
3. Do you think it is necessary to award prizes and incentives to athletes?

## 回答

> Describe a sports competition you watched You should say: What it was When/where you watched it Who you watched it with And explain why you watched it
> 描述一场你看过的体育比赛。你应该说：它是什么；你何时/何地观看的；你和谁一起看的；并解释你为什么观看它

A:

1. **直接回答问题，介绍比赛** (Introduce the competition directly)
   **I want to talk about** a basketball game I watched last month. **It was** between my university team and another school.
   **我想谈谈** 我上个月看的一场篮球赛。**是** 我们大学队和另一个学校的比赛。

2. **说明观看时间和地点** (State when and where I watched it)
   **I watched it** in our school gym on a Saturday afternoon. **It was** very crowded and exciting.
   **我是在** 周六下午在我们学校体育馆看的。**现场** 非常拥挤和激动人心。

3. **说明和谁一起观看** (Mention who I watched it with)
   **I went with** my roommate Ruijie. **We both** love sports, so it was fun to watch together.
   **我是和** 我的室友瑞杰一起去的。**我们都** 喜欢运动，所以一起看很有趣。

4. **解释观看原因1** (Explain first reason for watching)
   **The main reason I watched was** to support our school team. **I think** it's important to cheer for your friends.
   **我观看的主要原因是** 支持我们校队。**我认为** 为朋友加油很重要。

5. **解释观看原因2** (Explain second reason for watching)
   **Also, I really enjoy** watching basketball because it's fast and exciting. **The players** were very skilled.
   **而且，我真的很喜欢** 看篮球，因为它又快又刺激。**球员们** 技术很好。

6. **描述比赛氛围** (Describe the atmosphere)
   **The best part was** when our team scored the winning point. **Everyone** cheered loudly and clapped.
   **最棒的部分是** 当我们队得分获胜时。**每个人** 都大声欢呼和鼓掌。

7. **总结感受** (Conclude with personal feeling)
   **Overall, it was** a great experience. **I would definitely** watch another game like this.
   **总的来说，这是** 一次很棒的体验。**我肯定会** 再看一场这样的比赛。

> What kind of competitions do people in your country like to watch? Why?
> 你们国家的人喜欢看什么样的比赛？为什么？

A:

1. **给出观点** (Give opinion)
   **In China, people** really like watching table tennis and basketball competitions.
   **在中国，人们** 真的很喜欢看乒乓球和篮球比赛。

2. **解释原因** (Explain reason)
   **This is because** these sports are very popular and many people play them.
   **这是因为** 这些运动很受欢迎，很多人都会玩。

3. **举例说明** (Give example)
   **For example,** during the Olympics, everyone watches and supports our national teams.
   **例如，** 奥运会期间，每个人都会观看并支持我们的国家队。

> Do you think there should be more matches or competitions broadcast on TV? What is the most popular sport in China?
> 你认为应该有更多比赛在电视上转播吗？中国最受欢迎的运动是什么？

A:

1. **回答问题1** (Answer first question)
   **Yes, I think** showing more sports on TV is a good idea.
   **是的，我认为** 在电视上播放更多体育节目是个好主意。

2. **解释原因** (Explain reason)
   **It helps** people learn about different sports and stay healthy.
   **这有助于** 人们了解不同的运动并保持健康。

3. **回答问题2** (Answer second question)
   **The most popular sport** is probably table tennis or basketball.
   **最受欢迎的运动** 可能是乒乓球或篮球。

> Do you think it is necessary to award prizes and incentives to athletes?
> 你认为有必要给运动员奖励和激励吗？

A:

1. **给出观点** (Give opinion)
   **Yes, I believe** athletes should receive prizes for their hard work.
   **是的，我相信** 运动员应该为他们的辛勤工作获得奖励。

2. **解释原因** (Explain reason)
   **This encourages** them to train harder and do their best.
   **这鼓励** 他们更加努力训练，做到最好。

3. **补充说明** (Add supporting point)
   **Also, it shows** that we appreciate their efforts for our country.
   **而且，这表明** 我们感谢他们为我们国家做出的努力。

## 分析

### 话题词汇/短语

1. `basketball game` - 篮球比赛
2. `university team` - 大学队
3. `school gym` - 学校体育馆
4. `cheer for` - 为...加油
5. `winning point` - 制胜分
6. `table tennis` - 乒乓球
7. `national teams` - 国家队
8. `train harder` - 更加努力训练

### 实用词汇/短语/句式

1. **I want to talk about** - 我想谈谈... (很好的开头方式)
2. **The main reason was** - 主要原因是... (解释原因)
3. **I really enjoy** - 我真的很喜欢... (表达喜好)
4. **The best part was** - 最棒的部分是... (描述亮点)
5. **Overall, it was** - 总的来说，这是... (总结句型)
6. **This is because** - 这是因为... (解释原因)
7. **For example** - 例如 (举例说明)
8. **Yes, I think** - 是的，我认为 (表达观点)
9. **It helps** - 这有助于 (说明好处)
10. **Yes, I believe** - 是的，我相信 (表达信念)
11. **This encourages** - 这鼓励 (说明作用)
12. **Also, it shows** - 而且，这表明 (补充说明)
