# 争论

## 题目

### P2

Describe a disagreement you had with someone You should say: Who you had the disagreement with What the disagreement was What happened And explain how you felt about it

### P3

1. What do you do if you disagree with someone?
2. How can we stop an argument from escalating into a fight?
3. Who do you think should teach children to respect their teacher?
4. What disagreements do parents and children usually have?
5. Why do some people avoid arguing with others?
6. How do we show respect to others when we disagree with them?

## 回答

> Describe a disagreement you had with someone You should say: Who you had the disagreement with What the disagreement was What happened And explain how you felt about it
> 描述你与某人发生的一次争论。你应该说：你和谁发生了争论，争论的内容是什么，发生了什么，并解释你对此的感受

A:

1. (介绍人物和背景)  
**I had a small disagreement with my roommate, Ruijie Liu.**  
我和我的室友刘瑞杰有过一次小争论。

2. (说明争论内容)  
**We disagreed about cleaning our room.** He wanted to clean it every day, but I thought twice a week was enough.  
我们对于打扫房间有不同意见。他想每天打扫，但我认为一周两次就够了。

3. (描述事件经过)  
**One day, he started cleaning when I was busy with my homework.** I told him I would help later, but he seemed a bit upset.  
有一天，我在忙作业时他开始打扫。我告诉他我晚点会帮忙，但他看起来有点不高兴。

4. (说明后续发展)  
**After that, we talked about it calmly.** I explained that I have a lot of schoolwork, and he said he likes a tidy place.  
之后，我们平静地谈了谈。我解释说我功课很多，他说他喜欢整洁的环境。

5. (提出解决方案)  
**We decided to make a cleaning schedule together.** Now we clean on Saturdays, and it works for both of us.  
我们决定一起制定一个打扫计划。现在我们在每周六打扫，这样对我们两个都合适。

6. (表达个人感受)  
**At first, I felt a little stressed because I was busy.** But after we talked, I felt much better.  
起初，因为我很忙，所以感到有点压力。但在我们交谈之后，我感觉好多了。

7. (总结学习收获)  
**I learned that it's important to listen to each other.** Now our room is clean, and we are still good friends.  
我学到了倾听彼此很重要。现在我们的房间很干净，我们仍然是好朋友。

> What do you do if you disagree with someone?
> 如果你和某人意见不合，你会怎么做？

A:

1. (表达个人做法)  
**I try to stay calm and listen to their opinion.**  
我尽量保持冷静，并倾听他们的观点。

2. (说明原因)  
**Because sometimes they have a good point that I didn't think about.**  
因为他们有时有好的观点，是我没想到的。

3. (举例说明)  
**For example, if my friend wants to watch a different movie, I might agree if his choice is interesting.**  
例如，如果我的朋友想看另一部电影，如果他的选择有趣，我可能会同意。

> How can we stop an argument from escalating into a fight?
> 我们如何防止争论升级为争吵？

A:

1. (提出方法)  
**We can take a deep breath and speak in a quiet voice.**  
我们可以深呼吸，用平静的语气说话。

2. (说明原因)  
**This helps everyone stay calm and think clearly.**  
这有助于每个人保持冷静，清晰思考。

3. (举例说明)  
**For instance, if I feel angry, I might say 'Let's talk about this later' to avoid saying something bad.**  
例如，如果我感到生气，我可能会说"我们晚点再谈这个"，以避免说些不好的话。

> Who do you think should teach children to respect their teacher?
> 你认为谁应该教孩子尊重老师？

A:

1. (表达观点)  
**I think parents should teach children to respect their teacher.**  
我认为父母应该教孩子尊重老师。

2. (说明原因)  
**Because children spend more time at home first, and parents are their first teachers.**  
因为孩子首先在家时间更多，父母是他们的第一任老师。

3. (补充说明)  
**Also, teachers can remind children about respect in school too.**  
而且，老师也可以在学校提醒孩子要尊重。

> What disagreements do parents and children usually have?
> 父母和孩子通常有哪些分歧？

A:

1. (说明常见分歧)  
**They often disagree about homework time and bedtime.**  
他们经常在作业时间和睡觉时间上有分歧。

2. (具体说明)  
**Parents want children to study more and sleep early, but children want to play or watch TV.**  
父母希望孩子多学习、早睡觉，但孩子想玩或看电视。

3. (举例说明)  
**For example, my little cousin argues with his mom about playing video games before finishing homework.**  
例如，我的小表弟在完成作业前玩游戏时会和他妈妈争论。

> Why do some people avoid arguing with others?
> 为什么有些人避免与他人争论？

A:

1. (说明原因)  
**Some people avoid arguments because they don't like conflict.**  
有些人避免争论是因为他们不喜欢冲突。

2. (进一步解释)  
**They feel uncomfortable or worried that it might hurt the relationship.**  
他们感到不舒服或担心这会伤害关系。

3. (举例说明)  
**For instance, I sometimes avoid arguing with close friends to keep our friendship happy.**  
例如，我有时避免与亲密朋友争论，以保持我们的友谊愉快。

> How do we show respect to others when we disagree with them?
> 当我们与他人意见不同时，如何表现出尊重？

A:

1. (说明方法)  
**We can show respect by listening carefully and not interrupting them.**  
我们可以通过认真倾听和不打断他们来表现尊重。

2. (说明原因)  
**This shows that we value their opinion even if we think differently.**  
这表明即使我们想法不同，我们也重视他们的观点。

3. (举例说明)  
**For example, I nod and say 'I understand your point' before sharing my own idea.**  
例如，在分享自己的想法前，我会点头说"我理解你的观点"。

### 话题词汇/短语

1. **disagreement** - 意见不合，分歧
2. **roommate** - 室友
3. **cleaning schedule** - 打扫计划
4. **stay calm** - 保持冷静
5. **respect teachers** - 尊重老师
6. **avoid conflict** - 避免冲突

### 实用词汇/短语/句式

1. **We decided to...** - 我们决定... (表达共同决定)
2. **I learned that...** - 我学到了... (表达收获)
3. **I try to...** - 我尽量... (表达努力做某事)
4. **Because sometimes...** - 因为有时... (给出理由)
5. **For example,...** - 例如... (举例说明)
6. **This helps...** - 这有助于... (说明好处)
7. **They often...** - 他们经常... (描述常见情况)
8. **Some people... because...** - 有些人...因为... (说明原因)
