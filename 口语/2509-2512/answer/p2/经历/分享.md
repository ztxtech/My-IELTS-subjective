# 分享

## 题目

### P2

Describe a time when you had to share something with others You should say: What it was When it happened Who you shared it with Why you had to share it with others And explain how you felt about it

### P3

1. Do you think kids like to share? Why?
2. How can parents teach their children to share?
3. What do you think is the benefit of sharing for children?
4. Is there anything that parents should persuade children to share with others?
5. How can governments encourage shared transport?
6. Why is it important to share food with others during a celebration?

## 回答

> Describe a time when you had to share something with others You should say: What it was When it happened Who you shared it with Why you had to share it with others And explain how you felt about it
> 描述一个你必须与他人分享某物的时刻。你应该说：它是什么，什么时候发生的，你和谁分享的，为什么你必须与他人分享，并解释你对此的感受

A:

1. **直接回应问题，介绍分享的物品**
I want to talk about a time when I had to share my `Nikon Z30 camera` with my roommate, Ruijie Liu.
我想谈谈我和室友刘瑞杰分享我的尼康Z30相机的经历。

2. **说明时间和原因**
This happened last semester when we were working on a university project together. We needed to take some photos for our presentation, but Ruijie didn't have a camera.
发生在上学期我们一起做大学项目的时候。我们需要为演示拍些照片，但瑞杰没有相机。

3. **解释分享的具体情况**
I had to share my camera with him because it was the only good camera we had. We used it to take pictures of our project and also some campus scenery.
我必须和他分享我的相机，因为这是我们唯一的好相机。我们用它拍项目照片和一些校园风景。

4. **描述分享过程**
We took turns using the camera. I showed him how to use the basic functions, and he was really careful with it.
我们轮流使用相机。我教他如何使用基本功能，他非常小心地使用它。

5. **表达感受（第一部分）**
At first, I felt a little worried because it's my favorite possession. But seeing how responsible he was made me feel better.
起初我有点担心，因为这是我最喜欢的物品。但看到他那么负责任，我感觉好多了。

6. **表达感受（第二部分）**
In the end, I felt happy that we could work together and create something nice. Sharing the camera actually made our project better.
最后，我很高兴我们能一起合作并创造出好东西。分享相机实际上让我们的项目更好了。

7. **总结感受**
The experience taught me that sharing can lead to better results than keeping things to yourself.
这次经历教会我，分享可以带来比独自拥有更好的结果。

> 1. Do you think kids like to share? Why?
> 1. 你认为孩子喜欢分享吗？为什么？

A:

1. **表达观点**
I think most kids don't naturally like to share at first.
我认为大多数孩子最初并不天然喜欢分享。

2. **解释原因**
This is because they see their toys and things as their own special possessions.
这是因为他们把自己的玩具和物品视为自己特别的财产。

3. **举例说明**
For example, young children often say "mine" when someone tries to take their toys.
例如，当有人想拿他们的玩具时，小孩子经常说"我的"。

> 2. How can parents teach their children to share?
> 2. 父母如何教孩子分享？

A:

1. **提出方法**
Parents can teach sharing by setting a good example themselves.
父母可以通过自己树立好榜样来教孩子分享。

2. **具体说明**
They can share things with their children and explain why it's nice to share.
他们可以与孩子分享东西，并解释为什么分享是好的。

3. **建议活动**
Also, they can arrange playdates where children need to share toys to have fun.
此外，他们可以安排游戏约会，让孩子们需要分享玩具才能玩得开心。

> 3. What do you think is the benefit of sharing for children?
> 3. 你认为分享对孩子有什么好处？

A:

1. **指出主要好处**
The biggest benefit is that it helps children make friends more easily.
最大的好处是它帮助孩子更容易交朋友。

2. **解释原因**
When children share, other kids like to play with them and they learn to cooperate.
当孩子分享时，其他孩子喜欢和他们玩，他们也学会了合作。

3. **说明长期影响**
This skill helps them throughout their life in school and later at work.
这个技能在学校和以后的工作中都会帮助他们。

> 4. Is there anything that parents should persuade children to share with others?
> 4. 有什么是父母应该劝说孩子与他人分享的吗？

A:

1. **明确回答**
Yes, parents should encourage children to share toys and games during playtime.
是的，父母应该鼓励孩子在游戏时间分享玩具和游戏。

2. **解释原因**
These are things that are meant to be enjoyed together, and sharing makes play more fun.
这些东西本来就是一起享受的，分享让游戏更有趣。

3. **说明限度**
However, personal items like toothbrushes shouldn't be shared for health reasons.
然而，像牙刷这样的个人物品出于健康原因不应该分享。

> 5. How can governments encourage shared transport?
> 5. 政府如何鼓励共享交通？

A:

1. **提出建议**
Governments can make shared transport like buses and subways cheaper and more convenient.
政府可以让公交车和地铁等共享交通更便宜、更方便。

2. **具体措施**
They can create more bus lanes and increase the frequency of public transportation.
他们可以创建更多的公交专用道，增加公共交通的频率。

3. **补充建议**
Also, building safe bike-sharing stations can encourage people to share bikes for short trips.
此外，建设安全的共享单车站可以鼓励人们共享自行车进行短途出行。

> 6. Why is it important to share food with others during a celebration?
> 6. 为什么在庆祝活动中与他人分享食物很重要？

A:

1. **说明重要性**
Sharing food during celebrations brings people together and creates a happy atmosphere.
在庆祝活动中分享食物能把人们聚在一起，创造快乐的氛围。

2. **解释原因**
It shows care and friendship when everyone eats from the same dishes.
当每个人都吃同样的菜肴时，这显示了关心和友谊。

3. **文化意义**
In many cultures, sharing food is a way to show that we are all equal and connected.
在许多文化中，分享食物是表明我们都是平等和相连的方式。
