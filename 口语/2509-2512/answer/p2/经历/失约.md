## 题目

### P2

Describe a time when you forgot/missed an appointment You should say: What the appointment was for Who you made it with Why you forgot/missed it And explain how you felt about the experience

### P3

1. Is punctuality important to people?
2. What do you think is a good way to record things? Why?
3. Which is more important, a work-related appointment or an appointment with a friend? Why?

## 回答

> Describe a time when you forgot/missed an appointment. You should say: What the appointment was for, Who you made it with, Why you forgot/missed it, And explain how you felt about the experience.
> 请描述一次你忘记或错过约会的经历。你应该说明：约会是为了什么，你和谁约的，你为什么忘记或错过了，并解释你对这次经历的感受。

A:

1. **思路与结构**: 首先引入一个简单的约会场景，说明约会目的和对象。
   `Well, I remember **a time** when I `forgot`an`appointment`. It was a `dinner` `appointment`with my`good friend`from`school`.`
   好的，我记得有一次我忘记了一个约会。那是我和一位学校里的好朋友约好一起吃晚饭的约会。

2. **思路与结构**: 接着解释忘记的原因，结合学生的身份（学习忙碌）。
   `We planned to `meet`at a`new restaurant`near our`university`. `However`, I was `very busy`with`my studies`that`week`. I had to `read many papers`for my`computer science` `research`. I was so `focused`on`my work`that I`completely forgot`about the`time`and`our dinner plan`.`
   我们计划在我们大学附近的一家新餐馆见面。然而，那周我非常忙于学习。我必须为我的计算机科学研究阅读许多论文。我太专注于我的工作了，以至于完全忘记了时间和我们的晚餐计划。

3. **思路与结构**: 讲述发现错过后的感受和补救措施。
   `When my `friend` `called me`, I `realized`what`happened`. I felt `very bad`and`sorry`right away. I`apologized`to him on the`phone`.
