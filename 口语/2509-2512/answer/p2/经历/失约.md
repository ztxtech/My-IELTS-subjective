# 失约

## 题目

### P2

Describe a time when you forgot/missed an appointment You should say: What the appointment was for Who you made it with Why you forgot/missed it And explain how you felt about the experience

### P3

1. Is punctuality important to people?
2. What do you think is a good way to record things? Why?
3. Which is more important, a work-related appointment or an appointment with a friend? Why?
4. If someone doesn't really like whom they are going to meet, they may deliberately miss their appointment. Is that true? Why?
5. Do you think people should remember family history?
6. How do people who are busy remember things they need to do?

## 回答

> Describe a time when you forgot/missed an appointment You should say: What the appointment was for Who you made it with Why you forgot/missed it And explain how you felt about the experience
> 描述一次你忘记或错过约会的经历。你应该说：约会是为了什么，你和谁约的，你为什么忘记或错过了，并解释你对这次经历的感受

A:

1. 直接回应问题，介绍约会的背景

**I'd like to talk about a time I missed an appointment with my roommate, Ruijie.** We planned to go to the `gym` near our school together last month.

我想谈谈我错过和室友瑞杰约会的那次。我们上个月计划一起去学校附近的健身房。

2. 说明约会的具体内容和对象

**The appointment was for our regular workout session.** We always go to the `fitness center` on Wednesday evenings to exercise.

这次约会是为了我们常规的锻炼课程。我们总是周三晚上去健身中心锻炼。

3. 解释忘记的原因

**I forgot about it because I was too focused on my research.** I was working on a `time series` paper for Professor Deng and lost track of time.

我忘记是因为我太专注于我的研究了。我正在为邓教授做时间序列的论文，忘记了时间。

4. 描述发现错过约会时的感受

**When I realized I missed it, I felt really bad.** Ruijie had been waiting for me at the `gym` for thirty minutes.

当我意识到我错过了，我感觉很糟糕。瑞杰在健身房等了我三十分钟。

5. 说明后续的处理方式

**I immediately called him to apologize.** I explained that my `academic work` had made me forget our plan.

我立即打电话给他道歉。我解释说我的学术工作让我忘记了我们的计划。

6. 表达对这次经历的整体感受

**This experience taught me to be more careful about my schedule.** Now I always set `reminders` on my phone for important appointments.

这次经历教会我要更注意我的日程安排。现在我总是为重要的约会在手机上设提醒。

7. 总结并强调学到的教训

**Although it was an accident, I learned the importance of keeping promises.** Especially with friends who value your time.

虽然这是个意外，但我学到了遵守承诺的重要性。特别是与重视你时间的朋友。

> Is punctuality important to people?
> 守时对人们重要吗？

A:

1. 表达观点

**Yes, I think punctuality is very important for most people.** It shows respect for other people's time.

是的，我认为守时对大多数人来说很重要。它显示了对他人时间的尊重。

2. 解释原因

**When people are late, it can make others feel unimportant.** This is especially true in `work environments` and with friends.

当人们迟到时，会让别人觉得自己不重要。这在工作环境和朋友之间尤其如此。

3. 举例说明

**For example, if I'm late to meet Professor Deng, it might seem like I don't value his time.** So I always try to be on time.

例如，如果我见邓教授迟到，可能显得我不重视他的时间。所以我总是尽量准时。

> What do you think is a good way to record things? Why?
> 你认为什么是记录事情的好方法？为什么？

A:

1. 提出观点

**I think using phone calendars is the best way to record things.** It's convenient and always with you.

我认为使用手机日历是记录事情的最好方法。它很方便而且总是随身携带。

2. 解释原因

**You can set `alarms` and `notifications` that remind you at the right time.** This helps prevent forgetting important `appointments`.

你可以设置提醒你在正确时间提醒的闹钟和通知。这有助于防止忘记重要的约会。

3. 举例说明

**For instance, I now record all my `gym` sessions with Ruijie in my calendar.** This way I never miss them again.

例如，我现在把我所有和瑞杰的健身课程记录在日历里。这样我就再也不会错过了。

---

> Which is more important, a work-related appointment or an appointment with a friend? Why?
> 工作相关的约会和朋友约会哪个更重要？为什么？

A:

1. 表达观点

**I think both are important, but work appointments might be more urgent.** They often involve other people's schedules.

我认为两者都重要，但工作约会可能更紧急。它们通常涉及其他人的日程安排。

2. 解释原因

**Missing a work appointment could affect your `career` or `professional reputation`.** While friends are usually more understanding.

错过工作约会可能影响你的事业或职业声誉。而朋友通常更理解。

3. 补充说明

**However, keeping promises to friends is also very important.** It shows you value the `relationship`.

然而，对朋友遵守承诺也很重要。它显示你重视这段关系。

---

> If someone doesn't really like whom they are going to meet, they may deliberately miss their appointment. Is that true? Why?
> 如果有人不太喜欢他们要见的人，他们可能会故意错过约会。这是真的吗？为什么？

A:

1. 表达观点

**Yes, I think that can be true sometimes.** People might avoid meetings they don't enjoy.

是的，我认为有时可能是真的。人们可能会避免他们不喜欢的会议。

2. 解释原因

**It's human nature to want to spend time with people we like.** Unpleasant `appointments` can feel like a waste of time.

想要和我们喜欢的人在一起是人的天性。不愉快的约会可能感觉像是浪费时间。

3. 提出更好的做法

**But I think it's better to be honest or reschedule.** Deliberately missing appointments is not very polite.

但我认为诚实或重新安排更好。故意错过约会不太礼貌。

> Do you think people should remember family history?
> 你认为人们应该记住家族历史吗？

A:

1. 表达观点

**Yes, I believe remembering family history is important.** It helps us understand where we come from.

是的，我相信记住家族历史很重要。它帮助我们理解我们来自哪里。

2. 解释原因

**Family stories and traditions connect us to our `roots`.** They give us a sense of `identity` and belonging.

家庭故事和传统将我们与我们的根连接起来。它们给我们认同感和归属感。

3. 举例说明

**For example, knowing about my grandparents' lives helps me appreciate what they went through.** This makes me value my family more.

例如，了解我祖父母的生活帮助我感激他们经历的一切。这让我更珍惜我的家庭。

> How do people who are busy remember things they need to do?
> 忙碌的人如何记住他们需要做的事情？

A:

1. 提出方法

**Busy people often use technology to remember things.** Like `phone apps`, calendars, and `alarm systems`.

忙碌的人经常使用技术来记住事情。比如手机应用、日历和闹钟系统。

2. 解释原因

**These tools send `reminders` at the right time.** This is very helpful when you have a busy `schedule`.

这些工具在正确的时间发送提醒。当你有一个繁忙的日程时，这非常有帮助。

3. 举例说明

**For instance, I use my phone's calendar for all my `academic` meetings with Professor Deng.** It helps me stay organized and never miss important dates.

例如，我使用手机日历来安排所有与邓教授的学术会议。它帮助我保持有条理，从不错过重要日期
