# 和亲友享受的晚餐

## 题目

### P2  
Describe a great dinner you and your friend or family members enjoyed.  
You should say:  
- What you had  
- Who you had the dinner with  
- What you talked about during the dinner  
- And explain why you enjoyed it.

### P3  
1. Is it common for students to dine with each other on school days in your country?  
2. Do people prefer to eat out at restaurants or eat at home during the Spring Festival?  
3. What food do you eat on special occasions, like during the Spring Festival or the Mid-autumn Festival?

---

## 回答

> Describe a great dinner you and your friend or family members enjoyed.  
> 描述一次你和亲友一起享用的美好晚餐。

A:

1. 思路：先点题，说时间、地点和谁一起。  
Last month, I had a big **barbecue** with my **roommate Ruijie Liu** in our small dorm kitchen.  
上个月，我和室友刘瑞杰在我们小小的宿舍厨房吃了一顿大烧烤。

2. 思路：具体说吃了什么，用并列词列举。  
We grilled **beef**, **chicken wings**, and some **Sichuan-style spicy tofu**; the air was full of nice smells.  
我们烤了牛肉、鸡翅和一些川味辣豆腐；空气里都是香味。

3. 思路：说聊天内容，带出情感。  
While eating, we talked about how Prof. Deng helped us get research chances, and we laughed a lot about our first silly mistakes in the lab.  
吃饭时，我们聊邓老师怎样帮我们拿到科研机会，也笑了很多在实验室里第一次犯的傻错。

4. 思路：给出第一个享受的理由——食物。  
I really enjoyed it because the **food** was just the way we love: hot, salty, and straight from the grill.  
我很喜欢这顿饭，因为食物正是我们爱的样子：热、咸、刚离火。

5. 思路：给出第二个享受的理由——感情。  
Also, sharing that simple meal made me feel close to my friend and thankful for all the help we get.  
而且，分享这顿简单的饭让我感到和朋友更亲近，也感恩我们得到的所有帮助。

6. 思路：一句总结，回到幸福感。  
That noisy little dinner is a warm **memory** I will keep for a long time.  
那场吵闹的小晚餐，是一段我会珍藏很久的温暖回忆。

### P3

1. Is it common for students to dine with each other on school days in your country?  
在你们国家，学生平日会一起吃饭吗？

A:  
Yes, it’s very common because eating together is cheaper and more fun; for example, my classmates and I often share dishes in the canteen so we can taste many flavors without spending much money.  
很常见，因为一起吃饭更便宜也更有趣；比如我和同学常在食堂拼菜，这样花很少钱就能尝到很多味道。

2. Do people prefer to eat out at restaurants or eat at home during the Spring Festival?  
春节期间，人们更喜欢下馆子还是在家吃？

A:  
Most families still cook at home on New Year’s Eve, since the **hot pot** or **dumplings** made by parents taste like true family love, although young people may go to a restaurant for a quick lunch on the second or third day.  
大多数家庭除夕还是在家做饭，因为爸妈做的火锅或饺子才是真正的家的味道，不过年轻人可能会在初二初三去餐馆吃顿简单的午饭。

3. What food do you eat on special occasions, like during the Spring Festival or the Mid-autumn Festival?  
在春节或中秋节等特殊节日，你们吃什么食物？

A:  
At the Spring Festival we eat **dumplings** shaped like money for good luck, and at the Mid-autumn Festival we share sweet **mooncakes** while looking at the bright full moon.  
春节我们会吃饺子，它像元宝，代表好运；中秋节则一边赏月一边分吃甜甜的月饼。

### 分析

#### 话题词汇/短语  
1. **barbecue** 烧烤  
2. **Sichuan-style spicy tofu** 川味辣豆腐  
3. **hot pot** 火锅  
4. **dumplings** 饺子  
5. **mooncakes** 月饼  

#### 实用词汇/短语/句式  
1. **the air was full of nice smells** 空气里都是香味  
2. **share dishes** 拼菜 / 分着吃  
3. **taste like true family love** 尝起来是真正的家的味道  
4. **shaped like money for good luck** 形状像元宝，象征好运
