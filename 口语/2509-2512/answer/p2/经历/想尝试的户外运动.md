> What are the differences between indoor sports and outdoor sports?
> 室内运动和户外运动有什么区别？

A:
1. (给出主要区别)  
**The main difference is that** outdoor sports are in nature, while indoor sports are in buildings.  
主要区别是户外运动在大自然中，而室内运动在建筑物里。

2. (补充另一个区别)  
**Also**, outdoor sports often need good weather, but indoor sports don't.  
而且，户外运动通常需要好天气，但室内运动不需要。

3. (举例说明)  
**For example**, `basketball` can be played both inside and outside.  
例如，篮球可以在室内外都可以打。

> What outdoor sports are popular in China?
> 在中国什么户外运动流行？

A:
1. (列举流行运动)  
**I think** `running` and `badminton` are very popular.  
我认为跑步和羽毛球很流行。

2. (说明原因)  
**Because** they are easy to do and don't need special equipment.  
因为它们容易做且不需要特殊设备。

3. (补充例子)  
**Also**, many people enjoy `cycling` in parks.  
而且，很多人喜欢在公园骑自行车。

> What sports are popular among elderly people?
> 什么运动在老年人中流行？

A:
1. (说明流行运动)  
**Most elderly people** like `Tai Chi` and `square dancing`.  
大多数老年人喜欢太极和广场舞。

2. (解释原因)  
**These activities are** gentle and good for health.  
这些活动温和且对健康有益。

3. (补充细节)  
**Moreover**, they can do these sports with friends.  
此外，他们可以和朋友们一起做这些运动。

> Which outdoor sports are popular with children?
> 什么户外运动受儿童欢迎？

A:
1. (列举儿童运动)  
**Children usually enjoy** `playing football` and `flying kites`.  
儿童通常喜欢踢足球和放风筝。

2. (说明原因)  
**Because** these games are fun and exciting.  
因为这些游戏有趣又刺激。

3. (补充细节)  
**Also**, they can play with other children.  
而且，他们可以和其他孩子一起玩。

> How does weather affect outdoor sports?
> 天气如何影响户外运动？

A:
1. (说明主要影响)  
**Weather is very important for** outdoor sports.  
天气对户外运动很重要。

2. (举例说明)  
**For example**, you can't play sports when it's raining heavily.  
例如，下大雨时不能做运动。

3. (补充影响)  
**Also**, very hot weather can be dangerous.  
而且，非常热的天气可能很危险。

> Which is more dangerous, team sports or individual sports?
> 团队运动和个人运动哪个更危险？

A:
1. (给出观点)  
**I think** individual sports are more dangerous.  
我认为个人运动更危险。

2. (解释原因)  
**Because** in team sports, other players can help you if you get hurt.  
因为在团队运动中，如果受伤其他队员可以帮助你。

3. (补充说明)  
**However**, both can be safe with proper equipment.  
然而，有合适的设备两者都可以安全。
