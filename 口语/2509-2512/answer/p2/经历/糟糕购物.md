## 题目

### P2

Describe a problem you had while shopping online or in a store. You should say: When it happened What you bought What problem you had And explain how you felt about the experience

### P3

1.  What kind of customer service do you think is good?
2.  What are the differences between shopping online and in-store?
3.  What problems do customers often have while shopping?
4.  What do you think customers should do when there are problems with products bought online?
5.  Can customer complaints help improve product quality?
6.  What do you think of people complaining when they buy poor quality goods at a low price?

## 回答

> Describe a problem you had while shopping online or in a store. You should say: When it happened What you bought What problem you had And explain how you felt about the experience
> 描述一次你在网上或店里购物时遇到的问题。你应该说明：问题何时发生 你买了什么 遇到了什么问题 以及你对这次经历的感受

A:

1.  **思路**: 开头直接点明购物类型和时间，并简要概括问题。
    **Well**, I want to talk about a time I had a `problem` while shopping at a `supermarket`. This happened a few weeks ago, maybe a month or so.
    **翻译**: 嗯，我想说说我上次在超市购物时遇到的一个问题。这发生在几周前，大概一个月左右。

2.  **思路**: 描述买了什么，具体是什么问题。
    I went there to buy some `snacks`, like some cookies and chips, because I wanted to have them at home. The `supermarket` had a special sale, so I bought quite a lot. **However**, when I got home and checked the `snacks`, I found that most of them were very close to their `expiry date`. They were almost `expired`, and some would `expire` in just two or three days.
    **翻译**: 我去那里买了一些零食，比如饼干和薯片，因为我想把它们放在家里吃。超市当时有一个特价活动，所以我买了很多。然而，当我回到家检查这些零食时，我发现它们大部分都非常接近保质期。它们几乎都要过期了，有些在两三天内就要过期了。

3.  **思路**: 解释这个问题带来的感受和最终的处理方式。
    **Honestly**, I felt really `upset` and a bit `disappointed` about this. I thought it was not fair to sell `snacks` that were almost `expired` without telling customers clearly. It made me feel like the `supermarket` was not honest. So, I decided to go back to the `supermarket` and `talk to` the staff. They were very `nice` and `apologized`. They let me `exchange` all the near-`expired snacks` for new ones that had a long `shelf life`. **So**, in the end, the problem was `solved`, but it was still a bad `experience` at first.
    **翻译**: 说实话，我对此感到非常沮丧和有点失望。我觉得在没有清楚告诉顾客的情况下，销售快过期的零食是不公平的。这让我觉得超市不诚实。所以，我决定回到超市和工作人员沟通。他们非常好，并道歉了。他们让我把所有快过期的零食都换成了保质期长的新零食。所以，最终问题解决了，但一开始仍然是一次糟糕的经历。

> 1. What kind of customer service do you think is good?
>    你认为什么样的客户服务是好的？

A:

1.  **思路**: 首先给出总体看法，好的客服应该友好且乐于助人。
    **In my opinion**, good `customer service` means people who `work at a shop` should be very `friendly` and `helpful`. They should always have a `smile` and be `polite` when they talk to customers.
    **翻译**: 在我看来，好的客户服务意味着在商店工作的人应该非常友好和乐于助人。他们应该总是带着微笑，并在与顾客交谈时保持礼貌。

2.  **思路**: 进一步说明好的客服应该能解决问题，快速响应。
    **Also**, if a customer has a `problem` or a `question`, the staff should listen carefully and try their best to `solve the problem` quickly. **For example**, if I buy something that is broken, good `customer service` would mean they quickly help me `exchange` it for a new one or give me my `money back`.
    **翻译**: 此外，如果顾客有问题或疑问，工作人员应该仔细倾听并尽力快速解决问题。例如，如果我买的东西坏了，好的客户服务意味着他们会迅速帮助我换一个新的，或者把钱退给我。

> 2. What are the differences between shopping online and in-store?
>    网上购物和实体店购物有什么区别？

A:

1.  **思路**: 先说网上购物的优点：方便、选择多。
    **Okay**, there are some big differences between `online shopping` and `in-store shopping`. **First**, `online shopping` is very `convenient`. You can buy things anytime, anywhere, just using your phone or computer. You don't need to go out of your house. **Also**, `online shops` usually have many more `choices` of products than regular `stores`.
    **翻译**: 好的，网上购物和实体店购物有一些很大的区别。首先，网上购物非常方便。你可以在任何时间、任何地点，只用手机或电脑购物。你不需要出门。此外，网店通常比普通商店有更多的产品选择。

2.  **思路**: 接着说实体店购物的优点：亲身体验、即时性。
    **But**, `in-store shopping` also has its good points. You can `see`, `touch`, and even `try on` the products before you buy them. This helps you know if the quality is `good` or if the clothes `fit` well. **And**, you get the item right away; you don't need to `wait` for `delivery`. `Shopping in stores` can also be a `fun` social `activity` with `friends`.
    **翻译**: 但是，实体店购物也有它的优点。你可以在购买前看到、触摸甚至试穿产品。这有助于你了解质量是否好或者衣服是否合身。而且，你可以立即拿到商品；你不需要等待送货。在商店购物也可以是和朋友一起进行的有趣的社交活动。

> 3. What problems do customers often have while shopping?
>    顾客购物时常遇到哪些问题？

A:

1.  **思路**: 列举商品本身可能出现的问题，如质量不符、损坏。
    `Customers` can face many `problems` when they go `shopping`. **One common problem** is that the `product` they buy is not as `good` as they thought, or it's even `broken`. **For example**, sometimes you buy clothes, and after you `wash` them, the color `fades` quickly. Or you buy a `toy`, and it breaks easily.
    **翻译**: 顾客购物时可能会遇到很多问题。一个常见的问题是他们购买的产品不如他们想象的好，甚至是坏的。例如，有时你买衣服，洗过之后颜色很快就褪色了。或者你买一个玩具，它很容易就坏了。

2.  **思路**: 再说购物过程中的问题，如服务态度、排队等。
    **Another problem** can be about `customer service`. Sometimes the people `working in the shop` are not `friendly` or `helpful`. They might not answer your `questions` well. **Also**, many `people find` it `annoying` when there are very `long queues` at the `checkout`, especially when they are in a `hurry`. These `problems` can make `shopping` not `fun`.
    **翻译**: 另一个问题可能是关于客户服务。有时在商店工作的人不友好或不乐于助人。他们可能无法很好地回答你的问题。此外，很多人觉得在结账时排很长的队很烦人，尤其是当他们很着急的时候。这些问题会使购物变得不愉快。

> 4. What do you think customers should do when there are problems with products bought online?
>    购买的网购产品有问题时，你认为顾客应该怎么做？

A:

1.  **思路**: 强调沟通是第一步，先联系卖家。
    **Well**, if there are `problems` with `products` bought `online`, I think `customers` should **first** `contact` the `seller`. Most `online shops` have a way for you to `talk to` them, like a `chat box` or an `email`. You should tell them clearly what the `problem` is.
    **翻译**: 嗯，如果网购产品有问题，我认为顾客首先应该联系卖家。大多数网店都有一个你可以和他们沟通的方式，比如聊天框或电子邮件。你应该清楚地告诉他们问题是什么。

2.  **思路**: 说明后续步骤，寻求解决办法，如退换货或退款。
    **After that**, you should ask for a `solution`. This could be getting your `money back`, `exchanging` the `broken product` for a new one, or getting a `repair`. `Customers` should also take `photos` or `videos` of the `problem` as `proof`. **If the seller doesn't help**, you can then try to `contact` the `online platform` itself, like Taobao or Amazon, to ask them for `help`.
    **翻译**: 之后，你应该要求一个解决方案。这可能是退款、将损坏的产品换成新的，或者进行修理。顾客还应该拍摄问题照片或视频作为证据。如果卖家不帮忙，你可以联系在线平台本身，比如淘宝或亚马逊，向他们寻求帮助。

> 5. Can customer complaints help improve product quality?
>    客户投诉有助于提高产品质量吗？

A:

1.  **思路**: 肯定回答，投诉提供反馈，帮助商家发现问题。
    **Definitely, yes!** I think `customer complaints` can really `help improve product quality`. When `customers complain`, they are telling the `company` about a `problem` with their `product` or `service`. This is like giving `feedback`.
    **翻译**: 当然，是的！我认为客户投诉真的有助于提高产品质量。当顾客投诉时，他们是在告诉公司他们的产品或服务有问题。这就像是在提供反馈。

2.  **思路**: 进一步说明商家如何利用这些反馈来改进产品。
    The `company` might not know about these `problems` until someone `complains`. **So**, when they hear `complaints`, they can learn what went wrong. They can then try to `fix` the `problem`, make the `product better`, or change their `service`. This makes future `products` `better quality` for everyone.
    **翻译**: 在有人投诉之前，公司可能不知道这些问题。所以，当他们听到投诉时，他们就能知道哪里出了问题。然后他们可以尝试解决问题，让产品更好，或者改变他们的服务。这会使未来的产品对每个人来说质量更好。

---

> 6. What do you think of people complaining when they buy poor quality goods at a low price?
>    你如何看待人们购买低价劣质商品时抱怨的行为？

A:

1.  **思路**: 承认低价可能意味着低质量，但仍认为抱怨是可以理解的。
    **That's an interesting question**. I think it's `understandable` for `people to complain` even if they buy `poor quality goods` at a `low price`. **Because** even if something is `cheap`, `customers` still expect it to `work` or be `good enough` for its purpose.
    **翻译**: 这是一个有趣的问题。我认为即使人们购买的是低价劣质商品，他们抱怨也是可以理解的。因为即使东西便宜，顾客仍然期望它能正常使用或达到其用途的基本要求。

2.  **思路**: 解释抱怨的原因，以及抱怨的积极作用，强调公平性。
    **Maybe** the `product` is very `cheap`, but if it `breaks right away` or doesn't `work at all`, then it's a `waste of money`, even if it's a small `amount`. `Complaining` in this case can `help sellers` know that their `cheap products` are `not acceptable` to `buyers`. This can encourage them to `improve` the `quality`, even for `low-price items`. It's about being `fair`.
    **翻译**: 也许产品很便宜，但如果它立刻就坏了或根本不能用，那么即使钱不多，也是一种浪费。在这种情况下，抱怨可以帮助卖家知道他们的廉价产品对买家来说是不可接受的。这可以鼓励他们提高质量，即使是低价商品。这关乎公平。
