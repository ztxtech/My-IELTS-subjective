# 糟糕购物

## 题目

### P2

Describe a problem you had while shopping online or in a store You should say: When it happened What you bought What problem you had And explain how you felt about the experience

### P3

1. What kind of customer service do you think is good?
2. What are the differences between shopping online and in-store?
3. What problems do customers often have while shopping?
4. What do you think customers should do when there are problems with products bought online?
5. Can customer complaints help improve product quality?
6. What do you think of people complaining when they buy poor quality goods at a low price?

## 回答

> Describe a problem you had while shopping online or in a store
> 描述你在网上或商店购物时遇到的一个问题

A:

1. 直接回答问题，说明购物时间和地点
**I'd like to talk about** a time I bought some snacks at a mall near my school last month.
我想谈谈上个月我在学校附近商场买零食的一次经历。

2. 描述购买的具体物品
I bought some chips and chocolate, which are my favorite snacks.
我买了一些薯片和巧克力，这些是我最喜欢的零食。

3. 说明遇到的问题
The problem was that when I opened the chips bag, it was almost empty! There were only a few chips inside.
问题是当我打开薯片袋时，里面几乎是空的！只有几片薯片。

4. 描述当时的感受
I felt quite disappointed because I was really looking forward to enjoying those chips.
我感到相当失望，因为我真的很期待享受那些薯片。

5. 说明如何处理这个问题
I went back to the store and showed them the problem. The staff was very nice and gave me a new bag of chips.
我回到商店并向他们展示了这个问题。工作人员非常友好，给了我一包新的薯片。

6. 补充更多感受细节
Although it was a small problem, it made me realize that we should always check products before buying them.
虽然是个小问题，但它让我意识到我们应该在购买前检查产品。

7. 总结整个经历
In the end, the store handled it well, and I was happy with their service.
最后，商店处理得很好，我对他们的服务感到满意。

> What kind of customer service do you think is good?
> 你认为什么样的客户服务是好的？

A:

1. 提出观点
**I think** good customer service means being helpful and patient with customers.
我认为好的客户服务意味着对客户有帮助和耐心。

2. 解释原因
This is important because customers want to feel respected and valued when they have problems.
这很重要，因为当客户遇到问题时，他们希望感到被尊重和重视。

3. 举例说明
For example, when I had the problem with my chips, the staff listened carefully and solved it quickly.
例如，当我的薯片出现问题时，工作人员认真倾听并迅速解决了问题。

> What are the differences between shopping online and in-store?
> 网上购物和店内购物有什么区别？

A:

1. 提出主要区别
The main difference is that when you shop in-store, you can see and touch the products before buying.
主要区别在于，当你在店内购物时，你可以在购买前看到和触摸产品。

2. 说明另一个区别
Also, online shopping is more convenient because you can shop from home at any time.
此外，网上购物更方便，因为你可以随时在家购物。

3. 补充对比
However, in-store shopping lets you get the product immediately, while online shopping requires waiting for delivery.
然而，店内购物可以立即获得产品，而网上购物需要等待送货。

> What problems do customers often have while shopping?
> 顾客在购物时经常遇到什么问题？

A:

1. 列举常见问题
Customers often find products that are damaged or don't work properly.
顾客经常发现产品损坏或无法正常工作。

2. 说明另一个问题
Sometimes the size or color is different from what they expected.
有时尺寸或颜色与他们的预期不同。

3. 补充更多问题
Also, they might receive the wrong item or find that something is missing from the package.
此外，他们可能会收到错误的物品，或者发现包装中缺少某些东西。

> What do you think customers should do when there are problems with products bought online?
> 当网上购买的产品出现问题时，你认为顾客应该怎么做？

A:

1. 提出建议
**I believe** customers should first contact the seller or the customer service team.
我认为顾客应该首先联系卖家或客服团队。

2. 解释原因
This is the best way because most companies want to solve problems and keep their customers happy.
这是最好的方式，因为大多数公司都希望解决问题并让客户满意。

3. 举例说明
For example, they can send an email or use the chat function on the shopping website to explain the problem.
例如，他们可以发送电子邮件或使用购物网站上的聊天功能来解释问题。

> Can customer complaints help improve product quality?
> 客户投诉能帮助提高产品质量吗？

A:

1. 直接回答
Yes, definitely. Customer complaints can definitely help improve product quality.
是的，当然。客户投诉肯定有助于提高产品质量。

2. 解释原因
When companies hear about problems, they can fix them and make better products in the future.
当公司听到问题时，他们可以修复这些问题并在未来制造更好的产品。

3. 举例说明
For instance, if many customers complain about a phone's battery life, the company might make batteries that last longer in their new models.
例如，如果许多客户抱怨手机的电池续航时间，公司可能会在新型号中制造续航时间更长的电池。

> What do you think of people complaining when they buy poor quality goods at a low price?
> 对于人们以低价购买劣质商品时抱怨，你怎么看？

A:

1. 表达理解
**I think** it's understandable for people to complain, even if they paid a low price.
我认为人们抱怨是可以理解的，即使他们支付了低价。

2. 解释原因
Because every product should work properly, no matter how much it costs.
因为每个产品都应该正常工作，无论它花费多少钱。

3. 提出平衡观点
However, if something is very cheap, we shouldn't expect it to be perfect, but it should still be usable.
然而，如果某样东西非常便宜，我们不应该期望它是完美的，但它仍然应该是可用的。

2. `chips` - 薯片
3. `empty bag` - 空袋子
4. `customer service` - 客户服务
5. `return to store` - 返回商店
6. `product quality` - 产品质量
7. `online shopping` - 网上购物
8. `in-store shopping` - 店内购物

1. **I'd like to talk about** - 我想谈谈（用于开始讲述）
2. **I was really looking forward to** - 我真的很期待（表达期待）

4. **I think** - 我认为（表达观点）
5. **This is important because** - 这很重要因为（解释原因）
6. **For example** - 例如（举例说明）
7. **The main difference is** - 主要区别是（进行比较）
8. **Also** - 此外（补充信息）
9. **However** - 然而（表示转折）
10. **I believe** - 我相信（表达信念）
11. **Yes, definitely** - 是的，当然（肯定回答）
12. **it's understandable for** - 对...来说是可以理解的（表示理解）
