# 想再去的旅行

## 题目

### P2

Describe a trip you would like to make again. You should say: Where and when you went, Who you made the trip with, What you did during the trip, And explain why you would like to make the trip again

### P3

1. Why do people like travelling?
2. What should we prepare before a trip?
3. What would happen if no preparation were made before the trip?
4. Why do some people prefer to travel in their own country rather than going abroad?

## 回答

> Describe a trip you would like to make again. You should say: Where and when you went, Who you made the trip with, What you did during the trip, And explain why you would like to make the trip again
> 描述一次你想再次去的旅行。你应该说：你去了哪里，什么时候去的，你和谁一起去的，旅行期间你做了什么，以及解释你为什么想再次去这次旅行

A:

1. **直接回应问题，说明旅行地点和时间**
I went to `Chengdu` last summer with my roommate `Ruijie Liu`.
我去年夏天和我的室友刘瑞杰一起去了成都。

2. **描述旅行伙伴和主要活动**
We walked around the city, ate lots of `spicy food`, and visited `wide and narrow alleys`.
我们在城市里散步，吃了很多辣的食物，还参观了宽窄巷子。

3. **补充旅行细节和体验**
The best part was trying different `street food` every day and taking photos with my `camera`.
最棒的部分是每天尝试不同的街头小吃，用我的相机拍照。

4. **解释想再次旅行的原因（体验方面）**
I want to go again because I really enjoyed the `local life` and atmosphere there.
我想再去一次是因为我非常喜欢那里的当地生活和氛围。

5. **解释想再次旅行的原因（个人成长方面）**
Also, I think I can take better `photos` and `videos` now to remember the trip.
而且，我觉得我现在可以拍更好的照片和视频来记住这次旅行。

6. **总结并表达期待**
It was a very happy memory, and I'm sure the next trip will be even better.
这是一个非常快乐的回忆，我相信下一次旅行会更好。

> 1. Why do people like travelling?
> 1. 为什么人们喜欢旅行？

A:

1. **提出主要观点**
People like travelling because it's `exciting` to see new places.
人们喜欢旅行是因为看到新地方很令人兴奋。

2. **解释原因**
When we travel, we can `experience` different `cultures` and try new foods.
当我们旅行时，我们可以体验不同的文化并尝试新的食物。

3. **举例说明**
For example, when I went to `Chengdu`, I tried `hotpot` for the first time and loved it.
例如，当我去成都时，我第一次尝试了火锅并且很喜欢它。

---

> 2. What should we prepare before a trip?
> 2. 在旅行前我们应该准备什么？

A:

1. **列出主要准备工作**
We should prepare our `tickets`, `money`, and `clothes`.
我们应该准备我们的票、钱和衣服。

2. **说明信息准备的重要性**
It's also important to `research` about the place we're visiting.
研究我们要去的地方也很重要。

3. **举例具体准备事项**
For example, we should check the `weather` and find good `hotels` to stay.
例如，我们应该查看天气并找好住的酒店。

---

> 3. What would happen if no preparation were made before the trip?
> 3. 如果旅行前没有做准备会发生什么？

A:

1. **说明可能遇到的问题**
If we don't prepare, we might `forget` important things or get `lost`.
如果我们不准备，我们可能会忘记重要的东西或者迷路。

2. **解释具体后果**
We could arrive at a place and find all `hotels` are full, with no place to sleep.
我们可能到达一个地方发现所有酒店都满了，没有地方睡觉。

3. **总结负面影响**
This would make the trip very `stressful` instead of enjoyable.
这会让旅行变得很有压力而不是享受。

---

> 4. Why do some people prefer to travel in their own country rather than going abroad?
> 4. 为什么有些人更喜欢在国内旅行而不是出国？

A:

1. **提出主要原因**
Some people prefer travelling in their own country because it's `easier` and `cheaper`.
有些人更喜欢在国内旅行因为这样更容易也更便宜。

2. **解释便利性因素**
They don't need a `passport` or to learn a new `language`.
他们不需要护照或者学习新的语言。

3. **举例说明文化熟悉度**
Also, they already know the `culture` and `food`, so they feel more `comfortable`.
而且，他们已经了解文化和食物，所以他们感觉更舒适。
