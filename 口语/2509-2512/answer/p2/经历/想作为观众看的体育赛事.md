# 想作为观众看的体育赛事

## 题目

### P2

Describe a sports event that you would like to attend as part of the audience You should say: What it is Who you want to watch with Why you want to be part of the audience And explain how you feel about it

### P3

1. What kinds of sports events are broadcast on TV or other media in your country?
2. Do people in your country enjoy watching football games?
3. Do encouragement and applause from the audience have an impact on athletes? How do these affect them?
4. Which do people in your country prefer to watch, team sports or individual sports?
5. What kinds of sports are the most popular in your country?
6. Why do so many children like to watch basketball games?

## 回答

> Describe a sports event that you would like to attend as part of the audience
> 描述一个你想作为观众参加的体育赛事

A:

1. (直接回答问题是什么赛事)
**I would love to watch a `basketball game` live in a stadium.** 
我想在体育馆现场看一场篮球比赛。

2. (说明想和谁一起观看)
**I want to go with my `roommate`, Ruijie Liu, because we both enjoy sports.** 
我想和我的室友刘瑞杰一起去，因为我们俩都喜欢运动。

3. (解释为什么想当观众的原因一)
**The main reason is that the atmosphere in a stadium is so exciting and full of energy.** 
主要原因是体育馆里的气氛非常激动人心，充满活力。

4. (解释原因二)
**Also, watching players make amazing shots in real life is much better than on TV.** 
而且，在现场看球员投出精彩的球比在电视上看要好得多。

5. (补充感受)
**I think it would make me feel very happy and excited to be part of the crowd.** 
我觉得成为人群中的一员会让我感到非常开心和兴奋。

6. (具体描述体验)
**Hearing everyone cheer and clap together would be a wonderful experience.** 
听到大家一起欢呼和鼓掌会是一次很棒的体验。

7. (总结感受)
**Overall, I believe attending a live sports event would be an unforgettable memory.** 
总的来说，我相信参加现场体育赛事会是一次难忘的回忆。

> What kinds of sports events are broadcast on TV or other media in your country?
> 在你们国家，电视或其他媒体上播放哪些类型的体育赛事？

A:

1. (列举常见的转播项目)
**In China, many sports events are shown on TV.** 
在中国，电视上会播放很多体育赛事。

2. (具体说明最受欢迎的项目)
**The most popular ones are `basketball games`, `football matches`, and table tennis competitions.** 
最受欢迎的是篮球比赛、足球比赛和乒乓球比赛。

3. (补充其他转播项目)
**Sometimes they also show swimming races and badminton games.** 
有时也会播放游泳比赛和羽毛球比赛。

> Do people in your country enjoy watching football games?
> 你们国家的人喜欢看足球比赛吗？

A:

1. (直接回答问题)
**Yes, many people in China really like watching football.** 
是的，中国很多人都很喜欢看足球。

2. (说明受欢迎程度)
**Especially when the national team plays, everyone gets very excited.** 
特别是国家队比赛时，大家都会很兴奋。

3. (举例说明观看方式)
**People often watch games together in sports bars or at home with friends.** 
人们经常在运动酒吧或和朋友在家里一起看比赛。

> Do encouragement and applause from the audience have an impact on athletes? How do these affect them?
> 观众的鼓励和掌声对运动员有影响吗？这些如何影响他们？

A:

1. (肯定回答)
**Yes, I think encouragement from the audience is very important for athletes.** 
是的，我认为观众的鼓励对运动员非常重要。

2. (解释影响方式)
**When people cheer for them, it gives athletes more energy and confidence.** 
当人们为他们加油时，这能给运动员更多能量和信心。

3. (举例说明)
**For example, in a close game, loud applause can help players try even harder.** 
例如，在比分接近的比赛中，响亮的掌声能帮助球员更加努力。

> Which do people in your country prefer to watch, team sports or individual sports?
> 你们国家的人更喜欢观看团队运动还是个人运动？

A:

1. (说明普遍偏好)
**I think most people in China prefer watching team sports.** 
我认为大多数中国人更喜欢观看团队运动。

2. (解释原因)
**This is because team games are more exciting to watch with friends and family.** 
这是因为团队比赛和朋友家人一起观看更有趣。

3. (举例说明)
**Sports like basketball and football are more popular than individual sports like tennis.** 
像篮球和足球这样的运动比像网球这样的个人运动更受欢迎。

> What kinds of sports are the most popular in your country?
> 在你们国家，哪些运动最受欢迎？

A:

1. (列举最受欢迎的运动)
**The most popular sports in China are `basketball`, `table tennis`, and `badminton`.** 
在中国最受欢迎的运动是篮球、乒乓球和羽毛球。

2. (说明原因)
**These sports are easy to play and many people have played them since childhood.** 
这些运动容易进行，很多人从小就开始玩。

3. (补充其他受欢迎运动)
**Swimming and running are also quite popular among young people.** 
游泳和跑步在年轻人中也很受欢迎。

> Why do so many children like to watch basketball games?
> 为什么这么多孩子喜欢看篮球比赛？

A:

1. (说明主要原因)
**I think children like basketball because it's fast and exciting.** 
我认为孩子们喜欢篮球是因为它快速且激动人心。

2. (解释吸引力)
**The players jump high and make amazing shots, which looks very cool.** 
球员跳得很高并且投出精彩的球，这看起来非常酷。

3. (补充其他原因)
**Also, many basketball stars are like heroes to young fans.** 
而且，很多篮球明星对年轻粉丝来说就像英雄一样。

## 分析

### 话题词汇/短语

1. `basketball game` - 篮球比赛
2. `live in a stadium` - 在体育馆现场观看
3. `roommate` - 室友
4. `atmosphere` - 气氛
5. `amazing shots` - 精彩的投篮
6. `cheer and clap` - 欢呼和鼓掌
7. `football matches` - 足球比赛
8. `table tennis` - 乒乓球
9. `national team` - 国家队
10. `energy and confidence` - 能量和信心

### 实用词汇/短语/句式

1. **I would love to...** - 我很想...（表达强烈愿望）
2. **The main reason is that...** - 主要原因是...（解释原因）
3. **much better than...** - 比...好得多（进行比较）
4. **I think it would make me feel...** - 我觉得这会让我感到...（表达感受）
5. **Overall, I believe...** - 总的来说，我相信...（总结观点）
6. **The most popular ones are...** - 最受欢迎的是...（列举项目）
7. **Especially when...** - 特别是当...的时候（强调特殊情况）
8. **Yes, I think...** - 是的，我认为...（肯定回答并补充观点）
9. **For example,...** - 例如...（举例说明）
10. **This is because...** - 这是因为...（解释原因）
