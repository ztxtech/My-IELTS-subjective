# 熊孩子

## 题目

### P2

Describe a time when you saw children behave badly in public You should say: Where it was What the children were doing How others reacted to it And explain how you felt about it

### P3

1. What bad behavior do children usually have?
2. How should parents stop their children from behaving badly in public? Are parents these days stricter than those in the past?
3. Whose influence on children is more important? Friends or parents?
4. Who is to blame when children have bad behaviors, the parents or the children themselves?
5. What effect does it have on children when their parents punish them for their bad behaviors?

## 回答

> Describe a time when you saw children behave badly in public
> 描述一次你在公共场合看到孩子行为不当的经历

A:
1. (介绍地点和场景) 我记得有一次在成都的一个大商场里的餐厅。
   I remember one time in a restaurant inside a big shopping mall in Chengdu.
2. (描述孩子的行为) 有两个小男孩在桌子之间跑来跑去，还大声喊叫，打扰了其他客人吃饭。
   Two little boys were running between tables and shouting loudly, which disturbed other guests who were eating.
3. (描述他人的反应) 其他客人看起来很不高兴，有些人皱起了眉头，服务员过来请孩子们的父母管管他们。
   Other guests looked unhappy, some frowned, and a waiter came over to ask the parents to control their children.
4. (表达个人感受) 我觉得这样的行为很不好，因为公共场合应该保持安静，尊重他人。
   I felt this behavior was not good because public places should be quiet and we should respect others.
5. (补充细节) 孩子们的父母似乎很尴尬，但也没有很严格地制止他们。
   The children's parents seemed embarrassed but didn't stop them very strictly.
6. (总结感受) 总之，我认为父母应该更好地教育孩子在公共场合如何表现。
   Overall, I think parents should teach their children better about how to behave in public places.

> What bad behavior do children usually have?
> 孩子们通常有哪些不良行为？

A:
1. (列举常见不良行为) 孩子们经常会在不该说话的时候大声说话，或者抢别人的玩具。
   Children often talk loudly when they shouldn't, or take other children's toys.
2. (举例说明) 例如，在安静的图书馆里跑来跑去，或者吃饭时把食物扔得到处都是。
   For example, running around in a quiet library, or throwing food everywhere during meals.
3. (总结观点) 这些行为都会影响周围的人，让人感到不舒服。
   These behaviors can affect people around them and make others feel uncomfortable.

> How should parents stop their children from behaving badly in public?
> 父母应该如何阻止孩子在公共场合行为不当？

A:
1. (提出建议) 父母应该提前告诉孩子在公共场合的规则，比如要保持安静。
   Parents should tell children the rules for public places in advance, like keeping quiet.
2. (解释方法) 当孩子开始行为不当时，父母应该立即温和但坚定地制止他们。
   When children start to misbehave, parents should stop them immediately in a gentle but firm way.
3. (举例说明) 比如说，可以带孩子暂时离开那个地方，让他们冷静下来。
   For example, they can take the child away from the place for a while to calm down.

> Are parents these days stricter than those in the past?
> 现在的父母比过去的父母更严格吗？

A:
1. (表达观点) 我不这么认为，我觉得现在的父母可能更宽松一些。
   I don't think so, I feel parents today might be more relaxed.
2. (解释原因) 因为现在人们更注重孩子的心理健康，不希望太严格伤害孩子。
   Because people now care more about children's mental health and don't want to be too strict and hurt them.
3. (补充说明) 但是有些父母还是很严格的，这取决于每个家庭的教育方式。
   But some parents are still strict, it depends on each family's way of education.

> Whose influence on children is more important? Friends or parents?
> 谁对孩子的影响更重要？朋友还是父母？

A:
1. (表达观点) 我认为父母的影响更重要。
   I think parents' influence is more important.
2. (解释原因) 因为父母是孩子最早的老师，他们教给孩子基本的价值观和行为规范。
   Because parents are children's first teachers, they teach basic values and behavior rules.
3. (补充说明) 当然，朋友也很重要，特别是当孩子长大后。
   Of course, friends are also important, especially when children grow older.

> Who is to blame when children have bad behaviors, the parents or the children themselves?
> 当孩子有不良行为时，应该责怪谁？父母还是孩子自己？

A:
1. (表达观点) 我认为主要责任在父母。
   I think the main responsibility is with the parents.
2. (解释原因) 因为父母有责任教育和引导孩子什么是对什么是错。
   Because parents have the responsibility to educate and guide children about what is right and wrong.
3. (补充说明) 但是当孩子长大一些后，他们也应该学会为自己的行为负责。
   But when children get older, they should also learn to take responsibility for their own behavior.

> What effect does it have on children when their parents punish them for their bad behaviors?
> 当父母因孩子的不良行为而惩罚他们时，会对孩子产生什么影响？

A:
1. (表达观点) 适当的惩罚可以帮助孩子学会规矩。
   Proper punishment can help children learn rules.
2. (解释原因) 如果惩罚是公平和解释清楚的，孩子会明白为什么他们的行为是错误的。
   If the punishment is fair and explained clearly, children will understand why their behavior was wrong.
3. (补充说明) 但是太严厉的惩罚可能会让孩子感到害怕或伤心，所以需要平衡。
   But too harsh punishment might make children feel scared or sad, so balance is needed.
