# 熊孩子 (Naughty Children)

## 题目

### P2

Describe a time when you saw children behave badly in public You should say: Where it was What the children were doing How others reacted to it And explain how you felt about it

### P3

1. What bad behavior do children usually have?
2. How should parents stop their children from behaving badly in public? Are parents these days stricter than those in the past?
3. Whose influence on children is more important? Friends or parents? Who is to blame when children have bad behaviors, the parents or the children themselves?
4. What effect does it have on children when their parents punish them for their bad behaviors?

## 回答

> Describe a time when you saw children behave badly in public. You should say: Where it was What the children were doing How others reacted to it And explain how you felt about it.
> 请描述一次你看到孩子在公共场合表现不好的经历。你应该说明：在哪里发生 孩子们在做什么 其他人对此有何反应 以及你对此有何感受。

A:

1.  **思路与结构**: 讲述一次在商场看到 `熊孩子` 的经历。首先说明地点，接着描述孩子们的具体行为，然后提及其他人的反应，最后表达自己的感受。
    **I remember one time** I was at a `shopping mall` in Chengdu. It was a `busy weekend` afternoon, and I was just `looking around` in a big store.
    **我记得有一次** 我在成都的一个`购物中心`。那是一个`繁忙的周末`下午，我正在一家大商店里`四处逛逛`。

2.  **思路与结构**: 详细描述孩子们的 `不礼貌行为`，强调其对周围人的影响。
    **Suddenly**, I saw two `young children`, maybe around `five or six years old`. They were `running around` very `fast` and `shouting loudly` near the clothes racks. They even `touched many things` on the shelves, almost making some `clothes fall down`. They were `not being polite` at all.
    **突然**，我看到两个`小孩子`，大概`五六岁`。他们在衣架附近`跑来跑去`，`大声喊叫`。他们甚至`碰了`货架上`很多东西`，差点让一些`衣服掉下来`。他们`一点也不礼貌`。

3.  **思路与结构**: 描述其他人的 `反应`，以及 `父母` 的 `不作为`。
    Many `other people` in the store looked at them with a `slightly annoyed` face. Some people even `moved away` from the children. Their `parents were right there`, but they were `busy looking at their phones` and `didn't seem to care` much about what their `children were doing`.
    店里许多`其他人`看着他们，脸上带着`一丝不悦`。有些人甚至`躲开了`那些孩子。他们的`父母`就在`旁边`，但他们`忙着看手机`，对`孩子们在做什么`似乎`不太在意`。

4.  **思路与结构**: 表达自己对 `熊孩子` 及其父母行为的 `感受` 和 `看法`。
    **I felt a bit uncomfortable** because it was quite `noisy` and `messy`. **I also felt a little sad** because I think `parents should teach their children` how to `behave well` in `public places`. It's `important` for `children` to `learn good manners` from a `young age`.
    **我感到有点不舒服**，因为它相当`吵闹`和`混乱`。**我还感到有点难过**，因为我认为`父母应该教他们的孩子`如何在` 公共场合``表现好 `。对`孩子们`来说，从`小`就`学习好礼仪`是`很重要`的。

> What bad behavior do children usually have?

A:

1.  **思路与结构**: 回答孩子们常见的 `不礼貌行为`，例如 `吵闹` 和 `乱跑`。
    **Well**, I think `children` often `run around` in places like `restaurants` or `supermarkets`. They might also `shout loudly` or `cry a lot` when they `don't get what they want`.
    **嗯**，我认为`孩子们`经常在`餐馆`或`超市`等地方`跑来跑去`。当他们`得不到想要的东西`时，也可能会`大声喊叫`或`大哭`。

2.  **思路与结构**: 补充一些 `其他常见行为`，例如 `挑食` 或 `乱扔东西`。
    **Sometimes**, they even `throw their food` or `toys` on the floor. These are some `common bad behaviors` that `children` show.
    **有时**，他们甚至会把`食物`或`玩具`扔到地板上。这些是`孩子们`表现出的一些`常见的不良行为`。

> 父母应该如何阻止他们的孩子在公共场合表现不好？现在的父母比过去的父母更严厉吗？

A:

1.  **思路与结构**: 首先说明 `父母` 应该 `如何教育孩子`，强调 `沟通` 和 `制定规则`。
    **I think parents should** `talk to their children` and `explain why something is wrong`. They should `teach them` what `good manners` are. **For example**, they can tell their `children`, "No `running` inside the `restaurant`." It's good to `set clear rules`.
    **我认为父母应该** `和孩子交谈`，并`解释为什么`某件事是`不对的`。他们应该`教他们`什么是`好礼仪`。**例如**，他们可以告诉`孩子`，“不可以在`餐馆里跑动`。” `制定明确的规则`是很好的。

2.  **思路与结构**: 对比 `现在` 和 `过去` `父母` 的 `严厉程度`，指出 `现在` 更注重 `沟通`。
    About if `parents these days` are `stricter`, **I would say no**. I think `parents now` are `less strict` than `parents in the past`. My `grandparents` told me that in `their time`, parents would `punish children` more often and `speak very loudly`. **But now**, `parents talk more` to their `children` and `try to understand` their `feelings`.
    至于`现在的父母`是否`更严厉`，**我会说不是**。我认为`现在的父母`比` 过去的父母``不那么严厉 `。我的`祖父母`告诉我，在`他们的时代`，父母会更经常`惩罚孩子`，并且`说话声音很大`。**但现在**，`父母`更`多地和孩子交谈`，并`努力理解`他们的`感受`。

> Whose influence on children is more important? Friends or parents? Who is to blame when children have bad behaviors, the parents or the children themselves?

    **For young children**, `parents' influence` is definitely `more important`. `Parents` are like the `first teachers`. They `teach children` what is `right and wrong`, and how to `behave` in `different places`. **When children get older**, `friends' influence` becomes stronger, but `parents' guidance` is still `very important`.
    **对于年幼的孩子来说**，`父母的影响`绝对`更重要`。`父母`就像`第一任老师`。他们`教孩子`什么是`对的`什么是`错的`，以及如何在` 不同场合``表现 `。**当孩子长大后**，`朋友的影响`变得`更强`，但`父母的指导`仍然`非常重要`。

    When `children have bad behaviors`, **I think it's mostly the parents' fault**. `Children learn` from their `parents`. If `parents don't teach them good manners` or `set good examples`, then `children might not know` how to `behave well`. `Young children` are still `learning` about the `world`, so `parents need to show them the right way`.

> What effect does it have on children when their parents punish them for their bad behaviors?
> 当父母因为孩子的不良行为而惩罚他们时，会对孩子产生什么影响？

A:

1.  **思路与结构**: 从 `积极` 的方面说明 `惩罚` 对 `孩子` 的 `教育作用`。
    When `parents punish children` for their `bad behaviors`, it can have `good effects`. `Children will learn` that `their actions have consequences`. They will `understand what they did wrong` and `try not to do it again` in the future. It helps them `know the rules`.
    当`父母`因为`孩子的不良行为`而`惩罚他们`时，可能会产生`好的影响`。`孩子们会明白`他们的`行为有后果`。他们会`理解自己做错了什么`，并`努力在将来不再这样做`。这有助于他们`了解规则`。

2.  **思路与结构**: 补充说明 `惩罚` 需要 `合理`，避免 `负面影响`。
    **However**, parents should `punish them in a good way`, not with `anger` or by `shouting too much`. If parents are `too harsh`, `children might feel scared` or `very sad`, and that's `not always helpful` for `learning`. A `fair punishment` can `help children grow`.
    **然而**，父母应该`以好的方式惩罚他们`，而不是`带着愤怒`或`大声吼叫`。如果父母`过于严厉`，`孩子可能会感到害怕`或`非常难过`，而这对于`学习`来说`并不总是有帮助的`。一个`公平的惩罚`可以`帮助孩子成长`。
