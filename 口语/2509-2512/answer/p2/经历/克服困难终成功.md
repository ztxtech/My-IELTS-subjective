# 克服困难终成功

## 题目

### P2

Describe a difficult thing you did and succeeded You should say: What it was How you overcame the difficulties Whether you got help And explain how you felt after you succeeded

### P3

1. Should people set goals for themselves?
2. How would you define success?
3. How can we judge whether young people are successful nowadays?
4. Are successful people often lonely?
5. What kinds of success can students achieve at school?
6. Should students be proud of their success?

## 回答

> Describe a difficult thing you did and succeeded
> 描述一件你做过并最终成功的困难事情

A:

1. (介绍困难的事情是什么)  
**I want to talk about a research project I did with my professor, Prof. Deng.**  
我想谈谈我和我的教授邓教授一起做的一个研究项目。

2. (说明困难的具体内容)  
**It was about time series analysis, and it was really hard because I had to learn many new things and the computer code often didn't work.**  
这是关于时间序列分析的，非常困难，因为我需要学习很多新东西，而且计算机代码经常出问题。

3. (描述如何克服困难 - 寻求帮助)  
**I overcame these difficulties by asking my professor for help and working with my roommate, Ruijie Liu, who is also good at computers.**  
我通过向教授寻求帮助以及与我的室友刘瑞杰合作来克服这些困难，他也擅长计算机。

4. (描述如何克服困难 - 坚持努力)  
**I also spent many extra hours in the library reading books and practicing coding until I understood everything better.**  
我还在图书馆花了很多额外的时间阅读书籍和练习编码，直到我更好地理解了一切。

5. (说明是否获得帮助)  
**Yes, I got a lot of help from my professor and my friend, which made the project much easier to complete.**  
是的，我从我的教授和朋友那里得到了很多帮助，这让项目更容易完成。

6. (解释成功后的感受 - 成就感)  
**After we succeeded and our paper was published, I felt very proud and happy because all my hard work paid off.**  
在我们成功并且论文发表后，我感到非常自豪和快乐，因为我所有的努力都得到了回报。

7. (总结感受和学习)  
**This experience taught me that with help and persistence, I can overcome any challenge.**  
这次经历教会我，借助帮助和坚持，我可以克服任何挑战。

> 1. Should people set goals for themselves?
> 1. 人们应该为自己设定目标吗？

A:

1. (直接回答观点)  
**Yes, I think people should set goals for themselves.**  
是的，我认为人们应该为自己设定目标。

2. (解释原因)  
**Because goals give us direction and motivation to work hard and improve ourselves.**  
因为目标给我们方向和动力，让我们努力工作和提升自己。

3. (举例说明)  
**For example, when I set a goal to publish a paper, it helped me focus and keep trying even when it was difficult.**  
例如，当我设定发表论文的目标时，它帮助我集中精力，即使在困难时也继续尝试。

> 2. How would you define success?
> 2. 你如何定义成功？

A:

1. (提出定义)  
**I define success as achieving something you worked hard for and feeling happy about it.**  
我将成功定义为通过努力实现某事并对此感到快乐。

2. (进一步解释)  
**It doesn't always mean being rich or famous; it can be small things like learning a new skill or helping someone.**  
它并不总是意味着富有或出名；它可以是小事，比如学习新技能或帮助别人。

3. (举例说明)  
**For instance, when I finally understood a difficult math problem, that felt like a success to me.**  
例如，当我最终理解了一个困难的数学问题时，那对我来说就像是一种成功。

> 3. How can we judge whether young people are successful nowadays?
> 3. 我们如何判断如今的年轻人是否成功？

A:

1. (提出判断标准)  
**We can judge by looking at their achievements in studies, work, or personal goals.**  
我们可以通过看他们在学习、工作或个人目标上的成就来判断。

2. (补充更多方面)  
**Also, if they are happy with their life and have good relationships with friends and family, that shows success too.**  
此外，如果他们对自己的生活感到满意，并且与朋友和家人关系良好，那也显示了成功。

3. (总结观点)  
**So, success is not just about money or grades, but about overall happiness and fulfillment.**  
所以，成功不仅仅是关于金钱或成绩，而是关于整体的快乐和满足感。

> 4. Are successful people often lonely?
> 4. 成功的人常常孤独吗？

A:

1. (直接回答观点)  
**Not necessarily; some successful people might be busy, but they can still have friends and family.**  
不一定；一些成功的人可能很忙，但他们仍然可以有朋友和家人。

2. (解释原因)  
**For example, my professor is successful in his career, but he spends time with his students and family, so he isn't lonely.**  
例如，我的教授在事业上很成功，但他花时间与学生和家人在一起，所以他并不孤独。

3. (补充对立面)  
**However, if someone focuses only on work and ignores relationships, they might feel lonely sometimes.**  
然而，如果某人只专注于工作而忽视关系，他们有时可能会感到孤独。

> 5. What kinds of success can students achieve at school?
> 5. 学生在学校可以取得哪些类型的成功？

A:

1. (列举学术成功)  
**Students can achieve academic success, like getting good grades or winning competitions.**  
学生可以取得学术成功，比如获得好成绩或赢得比赛。

2. (列举个人发展成功)  
**They can also succeed in personal growth, such as making new friends, learning a hobby, or overcoming fears.**  
他们也可以在个人成长上成功，比如交新朋友、学习爱好或克服恐惧。

3. (举例说明)  
**For instance, I felt successful when I joined the photography club and improved my skills.**  
例如，当我加入摄影俱乐部并提升我的技能时，我感到成功。

> 6. Should students be proud of their success?
> 6. 学生应该为自己的成功感到自豪吗？

A:

1. (直接回答观点)  
**Yes, students should be proud of their success, no matter how small it is.**  
是的，学生应该为自己的成功感到自豪，无论它有多小。

2. (解释原因)  
**Because being proud motivates them to keep trying and achieve more in the future.**  
因为感到自豪激励他们继续尝试并在未来取得更多成就。

3. (举例说明)  
**For example, when I felt proud of my research project, it encouraged me to take on new challenges.**  
例如，当我对我的研究项目感到自豪时，它鼓励我接受新的挑战。

## 分析

### 话题词汇/短语

1. **research project** - 研究项目
2. **time series analysis** - 时间序列分析
3. **overcome difficulties** - 克服困难
4. **hard work paid off** - 努力得到回报
5. **set goals** - 设定目标
6. **achieve success** - 取得成功
7. **academic achievements** - 学术成就
8. **personal growth** - 个人成长

### 实用词汇/短语/句式

1. **I want to talk about...** - 我想谈谈...（用于开头）
2. **It was really hard because...** - 这很难，因为...（解释困难）
3. **I overcame these difficulties by...** - 我通过...克服这些困难（描述方法）
4. **I felt very proud and happy because...** - 我感到非常自豪和快乐，因为...（表达感受）
5. **This experience taught me that...** - 这次经历教会我...（总结学习）
6. **Yes, I think...** - 是的，我认为...（直接回答）
7. **Because...** - 因为...（解释原因）
8. **For example,...** - 例如...（举例说明）
9. **Not necessarily;...** - 不一定；...（表达不确定性）
10. **They can also...** - 他们也可以...（补充观点）
