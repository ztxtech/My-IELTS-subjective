## 回答

> Describe a time when you saw a lot of plastic waste (e.g. in a park, on the beach, etc.) You should say: Where and when you saw the plastic waste Why there were a lot of plastic waste What you did after you saw them And explain what your thoughts were about this
> 描述一次你看到很多塑料废品的经历（例如在公园、海滩等）。你应该说：你在何时何地看到了这些塑料废品；为什么那里会有这么多塑料废品；你看到之后做了什么；并解释你对此的想法

A:

1. **直接回答问题，说明时间和地点**
   I remember seeing a huge amount of plastic waste last summer at a local park near my university.
   我记得去年夏天在我大学附近的一个本地公园看到了大量的塑料废品。

2. **解释原因，连接到个人经历**
   I think there was so much plastic because many students like me go there to have picnics and often use disposable plastic containers and bottles for convenience.
   我认为有这么多塑料是因为很多像我一样的学生去那里野餐，经常为了方便使用一次性塑料容器和瓶子。

3. **描述看到的具体物品，增加细节**
   I saw plastic bags, water bottles, and food packaging scattered everywhere, especially around the picnic areas and trash cans.
   我看到塑料袋、水瓶和食品包装散落得到处都是，尤其是在野餐区和垃圾桶周围。

4. **说明采取的行动**
   After I saw this, I felt really bad, so I picked up some of the plastic waste and put it in the recycling bin, even though it wasn't mine.
   我看到之后感觉很难过，所以我捡起了一些塑料废品放进了回收箱，尽管那不是我的。

5. **表达个人感受和想法**
   I thought about how this plastic could harm the environment and animals, and it made me realize that we all need to be more careful.
   我想到这些塑料会如何伤害环境和动物，这让我意识到我们都需要更加小心。

6. **连接回个人习惯**
   This experience actually made me start using my own water bottle and food containers when I go out, instead of disposable ones.
   这次经历实际上让我开始在外出时使用自己的水瓶和食物容器，而不是一次性的。

7. **总结反思**
   So, overall, it was a sad sight but also an important lesson for me about being more environmentally friendly.
   所以，总的来说，这是一个令人难过的景象，但也是关于更加环保的重要一课。

> Do you think we should use plastic products?
> 你认为我们应该使用塑料产品吗？

A:

1. **给出平衡的观点**
   I think we should use less plastic, but it's hard to stop completely because it's very useful in some situations, like for keeping food fresh.
   我认为我们应该少用塑料，但很难完全停止，因为它在某些情况下非常有用，比如保持食物新鲜。

2. **解释原因**
   The problem is that people often use plastic for things they don't really need, and then throw it away too quickly.
   问题是人们经常为他们并不真正需要的东西使用塑料，然后太快扔掉它。

3. **提出解决方案**
   So, we should try to use other materials when we can, like glass or metal, especially for things we use every day.
   所以，我们应该尽可能尝试使用其他材料，比如玻璃或金属，特别是对于我们每天使用的东西。

> How can we reduce our use of plastic?
> 我们如何减少塑料的使用？

A:

1. **提出简单实用的建议**
   One easy way is to always carry a reusable water bottle and shopping bag, so we don't need to use plastic ones.
   一个简单的方法是总是携带可重复使用的水瓶和购物袋，这样我们就不需要使用塑料的。

2. **给出另一个建议**
   Also, when we buy things, we can choose products with less plastic packaging, or no packaging at all if possible.
   而且，当我们买东西时，我们可以选择塑料包装更少的产品，或者如果可能的话完全没有包装的。

3. **鼓励行为改变**
   Small changes like these can make a big difference if many people do them together.
   如果很多人一起做，像这样的小改变可以产生很大的不同。

> What kinds of plastic waste are often seen in your country?
> 在你的国家经常看到哪些种类的塑料废品？

A:

1. **列举常见类型**
   The most common plastic waste in China is probably plastic bottles, shopping bags, and food packaging from takeout meals.
   在中国最常见的塑料废品可能是塑料瓶、购物袋和外卖食品的包装。

2. **提供具体例子**
   You can see these everywhere - on streets, in parks, and especially around schools and offices where people often eat takeout food.
   你到处都能看到这些——在街上、公园里，尤其是在人们经常吃外卖的学校和办公室周围。

3. **说明问题范围**
   Single-use plastic items like straws and disposable cutlery are also very common and create a lot of waste.
   像吸管和一次性餐具这样的一次性塑料物品也非常常见，并产生大量废品。

> Why do people like to use plastic products?
> 为什么人们喜欢使用塑料产品？

A:

1. **说明主要优势**
   People like plastic because it's very convenient, lightweight, and cheap to produce.
   人们喜欢塑料是因为它非常方便、轻便且生产成本低廉。

2. **解释实际好处**
   It's also waterproof and good for protecting food and other items, which makes it useful for everyday life.
   它还能防水，有利于保护食物和其他物品，这使得它在日常生活中很有用。

3. **承认习惯因素**
   Many people are just used to using plastic and don't think about the environmental problems it causes.
   很多人只是习惯使用塑料，并没有考虑它造成的环境问题。

> What can the government do to reduce plastic pollution?
> 政府可以做些什么来减少塑料污染？

A:

1. **提出政策建议**
   The government could make laws to limit single-use plastics, like banning plastic bags in stores or charging extra for them.
   政府可以制定法律来限制一次性塑料，比如禁止商店使用塑料袋或对它们收取额外费用。

2. **建议教育措施**
   They could also teach people about recycling and provide more recycling bins in public places.
   他们还可以教人们关于回收的知识，并在公共场所提供更多的回收箱。

3. **鼓励替代方案**
   Supporting companies that make eco-friendly alternatives to plastic would also help reduce plastic use.
   支持生产环保塑料替代品的公司也有助于减少塑料使用。

> Do you think we can do without plastic altogether in the future?
> 你认为我们未来能完全不用塑料吗？

A:

1. **表达现实观点**
   I don't think we can completely stop using plastic because it's too useful in many areas like medicine and technology.
   我认为我们不能完全停止使用塑料，因为它在医学和技术等许多领域太有用了。

2. **提出折中方案**
   But we can definitely use much less plastic in our daily lives, especially for things we use only once.
   但我们绝对可以在日常生活中少用很多塑料，特别是对于那些我们只使用一次的东西。

3. **展望未来可能性**
   Maybe in the future, scientists will develop new materials that work like plastic but don't harm the environment.
   也许在未来，科学家会开发出像塑料一样工作但不伤害环境的新材料。
