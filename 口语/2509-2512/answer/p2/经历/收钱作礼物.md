> Describe a time when you received money as a gift.
> 描述一次你收到钱作为礼物的时候。

A:

1.  这部分是引出话题，说明这是一次特别的经历，而且收到的钱是意外之喜。

    **I remember** a very special time when I received some money, and **it felt like** a `gift` from luck. **It happened** just a few `months ago` at a `grocery store` near my university.

    我记得有一次非常特别的经历，我收到了一些钱，感觉就像是好运送来的`礼物`。`几个月前`，这件事发生在我大学附近的`一家杂货店`里。

2.  这部分是具体描述收钱的经过，说明是买东西抽奖赢来的，并且强调这是一个惊喜。

    **I was buying** some `snacks` and other `daily things`, and the `store` had a `promotion`. **If you bought** enough `items`, you could `draw a prize` from a box. **I thought**, "Why not give it a try?" And `to my surprise`, I `won a big prize`! **It was** a `cash prize`, which **was completely unexpected**.

    我当时在买一些`零食`和`日常用品`，`商店`里有一个`促销活动`。`如果你买`的东西足够多，就可以从一个盒子里`抽奖`。`我想`，“为什么不试试呢？”`令我惊讶的是`，我`中了大奖`！`那是一笔现金`，这`完全是出乎意料的`。

3.  这部分是解释为什么说它像一份礼物，强调那种惊喜和开心的感觉。

    **Even though** it **was a prize** and not directly `given by` a `person`, **I still saw it as a gift** because **it was such a nice surprise** and **made me feel very happy**. **It was like** the `store` was giving me a `special treat` for being a `lucky customer`.

    `尽管`那`是一个奖品`，不是直接由`一个人`给的，`我仍然把它看作是一份礼物`，因为`那是一个非常棒的惊喜`，`让我感到非常开心`。`这就像`是`商店`在给我这个`幸运顾客`一份`特别的奖励`。

    **I was super excited** when I received the money. **I used** a small part of it to buy more `snacks` to share with my `friends` in the `dormitory`. **We had a little celebration** together. **The rest of the money**, **I decided to save** it for `future needs`, like maybe a `trip` or buying a new `book`.

> Why do people rarely use cash now?
> 为什么人们现在很少使用现金？

    **I think** one big reason is that using `digital payments` on `phones` **is super easy and fast**. **You just need to** `scan a code` or `tap your phone`, and the payment is done. **It's much quicker** than finding `cash` and `coins`.

    `我认为`一个重要的原因是，在`手机`上使用` 数字支付``超级方便快捷 `。` 你只需要``扫一个码 `或者`点一下手机`，支付就完成了。`这比找现金和硬币快得多`。

2.  这部分强调了数字支付的安全性和追踪消费的好处。

    **Also, it's safer**. **You don't need to carry** a lot of `cash` with you, **which means** `thieves` cannot easily `steal it`. **Plus, you can always check** your `spending records` on your `phone` or `bank app`, **which helps you** `manage your money` better.

    `此外，它也更安全`。`你不需要随身携带`很多`现金`，`这意味着小偷`不容易`偷走它`。`另外，你总是可以在手机`或`银行应用`上`查看你的消费记录`，`这有助于你`更好地`管理你的钱`。

2.  这部分说明了信用卡的缺点，主要是过度消费和债务问题。

    **However, there are disadvantages**. **The main problem is that** **it's very easy to spend too much money** **if you are not careful**. `Credit cards` let you buy things you `don't really have the money for yet`, so **you can easily get into `debt`**. **If you don't pay back** the `money` `on time`, `you have to pay extra interest`, **which makes the debt even bigger**.

    **Regarding `digital payments`**, **yes, I definitely think it's a good thing** that more `people` are using them. **Like I said before**, **they are super convenient and safe**. **You don't need to worry about losing** your `wallet` or `cash`. **It also helps with tracking** your `spending`, **which is great** for `personal finance management`.
