# 收钱作礼物

## 题目

### P2

Describe a time when you received money as a gift You should say: When it happened Who gave you money Why he/she gave you money And explain how you used the money

### P3

1. Why do people rarely use cash now?
2. When do children begin to comprehend the value of money?
3. Should parents reward children with money?
4. Is it good and necessary to teach children to save money?
5. What are the advantages and disadvantages of using credit cards?
6. Do you think it's a good thing that more people are using digital payment?

## 回答

> Describe a time when you received money as a gift
> 描述一次你收到钱作为礼物的经历

A:

1. (直接回应问题，说明时间和送礼人)
**I remember this happened last year during the Spring Festival.** My uncle gave me some money as a gift.
我记得这是在去年春节期间发生的。我叔叔给了我一些钱作为礼物。

2. (解释送礼原因)
**He gave me the money because** it's a tradition in our culture to give money to younger family members during festivals. It's called 'lucky money' and it's meant to bring good fortune.
他给我钱是因为在我们文化中，节日期间给年轻家庭成员钱是一种传统。这叫做"压岁钱"，意味着带来好运。

3. (说明金额和使用计划)
**It was about 500 yuan, and I decided to use it** to buy a new lens for my camera. I love photography, and this was a perfect chance to upgrade my equipment.
大约是500元，我决定用这些钱为我的相机买一个新镜头。我热爱摄影，这是个升级设备的好机会。

4. (详细说明购买过程)
**I went to a camera store near my university** and found a nice portrait lens. The salesperson was very helpful and showed me how to use it.
我去了大学附近的一家相机店，找到了一个不错的人像镜头。销售人员非常帮忙，还教我如何使用。

5. (说明使用效果和感受)
**Now I use this lens all the time** for taking photos of my friends and campus life. Every time I use it, I remember my uncle's kindness.
现在我经常用这个镜头为朋友和校园生活拍照。每次使用它时，我都会想起叔叔的好意。

> Why do people rarely use cash now?
> 为什么现在人们很少使用现金？

A:

1. (直接回答原因)
**Because digital payments are much more convenient.** You just need your phone to pay anywhere.
因为数字支付方便得多。你只需要手机就可以在任何地方支付。

2. (补充另一个原因)
**Also, it's safer than carrying cash** because you won't lose physical money if your phone is protected.
而且，这比携带现金更安全，因为如果手机有保护，你就不会丢失实体钱。

3. (举例说明)
**For example, when I go shopping,** I always use my phone to pay. It's quick and I don't need to worry about having enough change.
例如，当我购物时，我总是用手机支付。这很快，我也不用担心没有足够的零钱。

> When do children begin to comprehend the value of money?
> 孩子们什么时候开始理解钱的价值？

A:

1. (给出大致年龄)
**I think around 6-8 years old,** when they start going to school and buying things by themselves.
我认为大约在6-8岁时，当他们开始上学并自己买东西时。

2. (解释原因)
**This is because** they begin to understand that money can be exchanged for things they want, like toys or snacks.
这是因为他们开始理解钱可以换取他们想要的东西，比如玩具或零食。

3. (举例说明)
**For instance, my little cousin** started understanding money when she began buying her own stationery at school.
例如，我的小表妹在学校开始自己买文具时就理解了钱的价值。

> Should parents reward children with money?
> 父母应该用钱奖励孩子吗？

A:

1. (表达观点)
**I think it's okay sometimes,** but not for everything.
我认为有时候可以，但不能什么事都给钱。

2. (解释原因)
**Because if children get money for every small thing,** they might only do things for money, not because it's the right thing to do.
因为如果孩子每件小事都能得到钱，他们可能只会为了钱做事，而不是因为这是正确的事。

3. (提出更好的方式)
**Instead, parents can give praise** or spend quality time with their children as rewards.
相反，父母可以给予表扬或花时间陪伴孩子作为奖励。

> Is it good and necessary to teach children to save money?
> 教孩子存钱是好事并且有必要吗？

A:

1. (肯定回答)
**Yes, absolutely.** It's a very important life skill.
是的，绝对有必要。这是非常重要的生活技能。

2. (解释好处)
**It teaches children** to plan for the future and understand that some things are worth waiting for.
这教会孩子为未来做计划，并理解有些东西值得等待。

3. (举例说明)
**For example, if a child wants an expensive toy,** saving money teaches them patience and the value of working towards a goal.
例如，如果一个孩子想要一个昂贵的玩具，存钱教会他们耐心和为目标努力的价值。

> What are the advantages and disadvantages of using credit cards?
> 使用信用卡的优点和缺点是什么？

A:

1. (说明优点)
**The main advantage is convenience** - you can buy things now and pay later. It's also good for emergencies.
主要优点是方便 - 你可以现在买东西，以后付款。这对紧急情况也很好。

2. (说明缺点)
**However, the disadvantage is** that people might spend more than they can afford and get into debt.
然而，缺点是人们可能花费超过他们能负担的，并陷入债务。

3. (提出建议)
**So it's important to** use credit cards carefully and only spend what you can pay back.
所以重要的是要谨慎使用信用卡，只花费你能偿还的金额。

> Do you think it's a good thing that more people are using digital payment?
> 你认为越来越多人使用数字支付是好事吗？

A:

1. (表达肯定观点)
**Yes, I think it's generally a good thing** because it makes life more convenient.
是的，我认为这通常是好事，因为它让生活更方便。

2. (承认潜在问题)
**But we need to be careful about** security and make sure our personal information is protected.
但我们需要小心安全问题，确保我们的个人信息得到保护。

3. (总结观点)
**Overall, the benefits outweigh the risks** if we use digital payments responsibly.
总的来说，如果我们负责任地使用数字支付，好处大于风险。
