# 天空中所见

## 题目

### P2

Describe a time when you saw something in the sky (e.g. flying kites, birds, sunset, etc.) You should say: What you saw Where/when you saw it/them How long you saw it/them And explain how you felt about the experience

### P3

1. Would people be willing to get up early to watch and enjoy the sunrise? When would people watch the sky?
2. Do many people pay attention to the shapes of stars?
3. What do people usually see in the sky in the daytime?
4. What are the differences between things people see in the sky in the daytime and at night?
5. Why do some people like to watch stars at night?

## 回答

> Describe a time when you saw something in the sky (e.g. flying kites, birds, sunset, etc.) You should say: What you saw Where/when you saw it/them How long you saw it/them And explain how you felt about the experience
> 描述一次你在天空中看到某物的经历（例如：放风筝、鸟、日落等）。你应该说：你看到了什么；你在哪里/何时看到它/它们；你看了它/它们多久；并解释你对这次经历的感受

A:

1. **直接回应问题，介绍事件**
I want to talk about a beautiful sunset I saw last month from my dormitory window.
我想谈谈上个月从我宿舍窗户看到的一次美丽日落。

2. **说明具体时间和地点**
It was around 6 p.m. after finishing my classes, and I was just looking out of the window on the 15th floor.
大约是下午6点，我上完课后，正从15楼的窗户往外看。

3. **描述所见景象**
The sky was turning orange and pink, and the clouds looked like they were on fire.
天空变成了橙色和粉色，云朵看起来像着火了一样。

4. **说明观看时长**
I watched it for about 20 minutes until the sun completely disappeared.
我看了大约20分钟，直到太阳完全消失。

5. **表达感受和情绪**
I felt really peaceful and relaxed because the view was so beautiful.
我感到非常平静和放松，因为景色太美了。

6. **补充细节，增强描述**
The colors kept changing every minute, which made it even more special.
颜色每分钟都在变化，这让它变得更加特别。

7. **总结体验价值**
This experience reminded me to appreciate the simple beauties in life.
这次经历提醒我要欣赏生活中简单的美好。

> 1. Would people be willing to get up early to watch and enjoy the sunrise? When would people watch the sky?
> 1. 人们愿意早起观看和欣赏日出吗？人们什么时候会看天空？

A:

1. **提出观点**
I think some people would be willing to get up early for a sunrise, especially if they are on vacation or really love nature.
我认为有些人愿意早起看日出，特别是当他们度假或真的很热爱自然时。

2. **说明观看时间**
People usually watch the sky during sunrise, sunset, or at night when they have free time.
人们通常在日出、日落或有空闲时间的夜晚观看天空。

3. **举例说明**
For example, many tourists wake up early to see the sunrise at famous places.
例如，许多游客早起在著名的地方看日出。

> 2. Do many people pay attention to the shapes of stars?
> 2. 很多人会注意星星的形状吗？

A:

1. **直接回答**
Not really, I think most people don't pay much attention to star shapes.
不太会，我认为大多数人不太注意星星的形状。

2. **解释原因**
This is because stars look very small and similar from far away.
这是因为星星从远处看很小而且很相似。

3. **补充说明**
Only astronomers or people very interested in stars might notice their shapes.
只有天文学家或对星星非常感兴趣的人可能会注意到它们的形状。

> 3. What do people usually see in the sky in the daytime?
> 3. 人们在白天通常能在天空中看到什么？

A:

1. **列举常见景象**
During daytime, people usually see the sun, clouds, and sometimes airplanes or birds.
白天，人们通常能看到太阳、云朵，有时还能看到飞机或鸟。

2. **说明不同天气情况**
On clear days, the sky is blue with white clouds, while on cloudy days, the sky is gray.
晴朗的日子里，天空是蓝色的，有白云，而多云的日子里，天空是灰色的。

3. **补充特殊现象**
Occasionally, people might see rainbows after it rains.
偶尔，人们可能会在雨后看到彩虹。

> 4. What are the differences between things people see in the sky in the daytime and at night?
> 4. 人们在白天和夜晚在天空中看到的东西有什么不同？

A:

1. **说明主要区别**
The main difference is that we see the sun and clouds during the day, but the moon and stars at night.
主要区别是我们在白天看到太阳和云朵，但在夜晚看到月亮和星星。

2. **解释原因**
This happens because the sunlight is too bright to see stars during the day.
这是因为白天阳光太亮，看不到星星。

3. **补充观察**
Also, the sky appears blue in daytime but black or dark blue at night.
而且，天空在白天是蓝色的，但在夜晚是黑色或深蓝色的。

> 5. Why do some people like to watch stars at night?
> 5. 为什么有些人喜欢在晚上看星星？

A:

1. **提出主要原因**
I think people enjoy stargazing because it helps them feel calm and connected to nature.
我认为人们喜欢观星是因为这能帮助他们感到平静并与自然连接。

2. **说明心理益处**
Looking at stars can make people forget their daily worries and feel peaceful.
看星星可以让人们忘记日常的烦恼，感到平静。

3. **举例说明**
For example, many people find it romantic to watch stars with loved ones.
例如，许多人发现与爱人一起看星星很浪漫。
