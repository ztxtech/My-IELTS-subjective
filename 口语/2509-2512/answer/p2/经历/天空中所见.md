> 4. What are the differences between things people see in the sky in the daytime and at night?

A:

    **There are big differences** between what people see `in the sky in the daytime` and `at night`. `In the daytime`, the `sky` is usually `blue`, and the `sun` is the `main thing` we see. We also see `white clouds` and `birds` flying. It feels `bright` and `full of life`.
    **有很大的不同**，在`白天`和`晚上`人们`在天空中`看到的东西。`白天`，`天空`通常是`蓝色`的，`太阳`是我们看到的`主要东西`。我们也看到`白色的云`和`鸟`在飞。它感觉`明亮`而`充满生机`。

2.  比较夜晚的景象和感受
    **However**, `at night`, the `sky` turns `dark`, and the `main things` we see are the `moon` and many `stars`. There are no `clouds` like in the `day`, and we don't usually see `birds` flying. It feels much `calmer` and more `mysterious`, very `different` from the `day`.
    **然而**，`晚上`，`天空`变得`黑暗`，我们看到的`主要东西`是`月亮`和许多`星星`。不像`白天`那样有`云`，我们通常也看不到`鸟`飞。它感觉更`平静`和更`神秘`，与`白天`非常`不同`。
