# 印象深刻的英语课

## 题目

### P2

Describe an impressive English lesson you had and enjoyed You should say: What it was about When you had it What the teacher did And why you enjoyed the lesson

### P3

1. Why do people learn foreign languages?
2. What makes a good foreign language teacher?
3. Do you think grammar is important when learning foreign languages? Is it interesting to be a foreign language teacher? Why?
4. What is the impact of information technology on learning foreign languages?
5. What effect will it have on the students if the teacher is impatient with them?

## 回答

> Describe an impressive English lesson you had and enjoyed You should say: What it was about When you had it What the teacher did And why you enjoyed the lesson
> 描述一堂你上过且喜欢的令人印象深刻的英语课。你应该说：它是关于什么的，你什么时候上的，老师做了什么，以及你为什么喜欢这堂课

A:

1. (直接回答问题，介绍课程主题)
   **I want to talk about** a special English lesson about `scientific research`. 
   It was about how to write a good research paper in English.
   
   我想谈谈一堂特别的关于科学研究的英语课。它是关于如何用英语写一篇好的研究论文的。

2. (说明上课时间)
   **I had this lesson** last year when I was working on my first international paper with my `professor, Prof. Deng`.
   
   我去年上了这堂课，当时我正在和我的邓教授一起写我的第一篇国际论文。

3. (描述老师的具体行为)
   **The teacher didn't just teach us grammar rules**. Instead, he showed us real examples from published papers. 
   He explained how to organize ideas clearly and use the right academic words.
   
   老师不只是教我们语法规则。相反，他给我们看了已发表论文的真实例子。他解释了如何清晰地组织想法和使用正确的学术词汇。

4. (解释喜欢的第一个原因)
   **I enjoyed this lesson because** it was very practical. I could use what I learned immediately in my own research work with Prof. Deng.
   
   我喜欢这堂课是因为它非常实用。我可以立即将学到的知识用在我和邓教授的研究工作中。

5. (解释喜欢的第二个原因)
   **Also, the teacher was very patient**. When I didn't understand something, he would explain it in different ways until I got it.
   
   而且，老师非常有耐心。当我不理解某些内容时，他会用不同的方式解释，直到我明白为止。

6. (说明课程的长期价值)
   **This lesson helped me a lot**. Later, my paper was accepted by an international journal, and I think this English lesson played a big role.
   
   这堂课对我帮助很大。后来，我的论文被国际期刊接受了，我认为这堂英语课起了很大作用。

7. (总结感受)
   **That's why** this English lesson was so impressive and enjoyable for me.
   
   这就是为什么这堂英语课让我如此印象深刻和愉快。

> 1. Why do people learn foreign languages?
> 1. 为什么人们学习外语？

A:

1. (给出主要观点)
   **People learn foreign languages mainly for** better opportunities. 
   
   人们学习外语主要是为了更好的机会。

2. (解释原因)
   **For example**, knowing English can help people get good jobs in international companies or study abroad.
   
   例如，懂英语可以帮助人们在跨国公司找到好工作或出国留学。

3. (补充另一个原因)
   **Also**, learning languages helps people understand different cultures and make friends from other countries.
   
   而且，学习语言帮助人们理解不同的文化，并结交来自其他国家的朋友。

> 2. What makes a good foreign language teacher?
> 2. 什么造就了一名好的外语老师？

A:

1. (提出核心品质)
   **A good language teacher should be** patient and encouraging.
   
   一个好的语言老师应该有耐心且能鼓励人。

2. (解释原因)
   **Because** learning a new language is difficult, and students need someone who won't get angry when they make mistakes.
   
   因为学习一门新语言很难，学生需要一个在他们犯错时不会生气的人。

3. (举例说明)
   **For instance**, my English teacher always smiled and said "good try" even when my pronunciation was wrong.
   
   例如，我的英语老师总是微笑，即使我的发音错了也会说"试得好"。

> 3. Do you think grammar is important when learning foreign languages? Is it interesting to be a foreign language teacher? Why?
> 3. 你认为语法在学习外语时重要吗？当外语老师有趣吗？为什么？

A:

1. (回答语法重要性)
   **Yes, grammar is important because** it helps people speak correctly and avoid misunderstandings.
   
   是的，语法很重要，因为它帮助人们正确说话并避免误解。

2. (回答是否有趣)
   **Being a language teacher can be interesting** when you see students improve and become confident.
   
   当你看到学生进步并变得自信时，当语言老师可以很有趣。

3. (解释原因)
   **It must feel great** to help someone learn a new skill and open doors to new opportunities.
   
   帮助某人学习新技能并为新机会打开大门，这种感觉一定很棒。

> 4. What is the impact of information technology on learning foreign languages?
> 4. 信息技术对学习外语有什么影响？

A:

1. (指出主要影响)
   **Information technology has made** language learning much easier and more convenient.
   
   信息技术使语言学习变得更容易、更方便。

2. (举例说明)
   **For example**, now we can use apps on our phones to learn vocabulary anytime, anywhere.
   
   例如，现在我们可以用手机上的应用程序随时随地学习词汇。

3. (说明另一个好处)
   **Also**, we can watch videos or talk to native speakers online, which helps with listening and speaking skills.
   
   而且，我们可以在线观看视频或与母语者交谈，这有助于听力和口语技能。

> 5. What effect will it have on the students if the teacher is impatient with them?
> 5. 如果老师对学生不耐烦，会对学生产生什么影响？

A:

1. (指出主要影响)
   **If a teacher is impatient**, students might become afraid to speak or ask questions.
   
   如果老师不耐烦，学生可能会害怕说话或提问。

2. (解释后果)
   **This can make** students lose confidence and interest in learning the language.
   
   这会使学生失去学习语言的信心和兴趣。

3. (说明长期影响)
   **In the long run**, they might give up learning altogether because they feel stressed and discouraged.
   
   从长远来看，他们可能会完全放弃学习，因为他们感到压力大和沮丧。

## 分析

### 话题词汇/短语

1. `scientific research` - 科学研究
2. `research paper` - 研究论文
3. `international journal` - 国际期刊
4. `professor` - 教授
5. `academic words` - 学术词汇

### 实用词汇/短语/句式

1. **I want to talk about** - 我想谈谈...(用于开始描述)
2. **I had this lesson** - 我上了这堂课...(用于说明时间)
3. **didn't just... Instead** - 不只是...而是...(用于对比)
4. **I enjoyed this lesson because** - 我喜欢这堂课因为...(用于解释原因)
5. **Also** - 而且(用于补充观点)
6. **This helped me a lot** - 这对我帮助很大(用于说明价值)
7. **That's why** - 这就是为什么(用于总结)
8. **People learn... mainly for** - 人们学习...主要是为了(用于给出原因)
9. **For example** - 例如(用于举例)
10. **A good... should be** - 一个好的...应该是(用于提出品质)
11. **Because** - 因为(用于解释原因)
12. **Yes,... is important because** - 是的，...很重要因为(用于肯定回答并解释)
13. **can be interesting when** - 当...时可能很有趣(用于条件性回答)
14. **has made... much easier** - 使...变得容易得多(用于说明影响)
15. **If..., ... might** - 如果...，...可能(用于假设情况)
16. **In the long run** - 从长远来看(用于说明长期影响)
