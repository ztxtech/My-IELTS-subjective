# 劝你的人

## 题目

### P2

Describe a time when a person persuaded you to do something and you were happy about that You should say: Who he/she was When/where it happened What he/she persuaded you to do And explain why you were happy

### P3

1. What impact does advertising have on children and their parents? What do parents often persuade their children to do?
2. Who do children listen to more, their parents or their teachers? Why? What are some good ways to persuade children?
3. What advice should young people follow?
4. How do advertisements persuade people?

## 回答

> Describe a time when a person persuaded you to do something and you were happy about that You should say: Who he/she was When/where it happened What he/she persuaded you to do And explain why you were happy
> 描述一个别人劝你做某事而你对此感到高兴的经历。你应该说：这个人是谁；什么时候/在哪里发生的；他/她劝你做什么；并解释你为什么感到高兴。

A:

1. **直接回答问题，介绍人物**
   I want to talk about my `professor`, `Prof. Deng`. He is my `advisor` at the university.
   我想谈谈我的教授，邓教授。他是我在大学里的导师。

2. **说明时间和地点**
   This happened last year in his `office` on `campus`. We were talking about my `studies`.
   这件事发生在去年他校园的办公室里。我们当时在讨论我的学业。

3. **说明劝说的内容**
   He `persuaded` me to `join` his `research team` and `write` a `paper` about `time series`.
   他劝我加入他的研究团队，写一篇关于时间序列的论文。

4. **解释高兴的原因 - 机会难得**
   I was really `happy` because it was a great `opportunity` to `learn` new things and `improve` my `skills`.
   我很高兴，因为这是一个学习新东西和提高技能的好机会。

5. **解释高兴的原因 - 获得成就感**
   Also, after many `months` of hard work, our `paper` got `published` in an `international journal`. That made me feel very `proud`.
   而且，经过几个月的努力，我们的论文在国际期刊上发表了。这让我感到非常自豪。

6. **解释高兴的原因 - 个人成长**
   This `experience` taught me to be more `confident` and not `afraid` of `challenges`.
   这次经历教会我更加自信，不害怕挑战。

7. **总结感受**
   So, I'm very `thankful` to `Prof. Deng` for `persuading` me to do this.
   所以，我非常感谢邓教授劝我做这件事。

> What impact does advertising have on children and their parents? What do parents often persuade their children to do?
> 广告对儿童和他们的父母有什么影响？父母经常劝孩子做什么？

A:

1. **观点**
   `Advertising` can make `children` want to `buy` many `things`, like `toys` and `snacks`.
   广告会让孩子们想买很多东西，比如玩具和零食。

2. **原因**
   This sometimes causes `arguments` between `parents` and `children` because `parents` need to say `no`.
   这有时会导致父母和孩子之间的争吵，因为父母需要说不。

3. **例子**
   For example, after seeing a `commercial` for a new `toy`, a `child` might `beg` their `parents` to buy it.
   例如，看完一个新玩具的广告后，孩子可能会恳求父母买它。

4. **回答第二部分**
   `Parents` often `persuade` their `children` to do `homework`, eat `healthy food`, and go to `bed` early.
   父母经常劝孩子做作业、吃健康食品和早睡。

> Who do children listen to more, their parents or their teachers? Why? What are some good ways to persuade children?
> 孩子们更听父母的话还是老师的话？为什么？有哪些好的方法可以说服孩子？

A:

1. **观点**
   I think `children` `listen` to their `teachers` more in `school`, but at `home`, they `listen` more to their `parents`.
   我认为孩子们在学校更听老师的话，但在家里更听父母的话。

2. **原因**
   This is because `teachers` have `authority` in the `classroom`, and `parents` know their `children` better.
   这是因为老师在教室里有权威，而父母更了解他们的孩子。

3. **回答第三部分**
   Good ways to `persuade` `children` include `explaining` `reasons`, giving small `rewards`, and being a good `example`.
   说服孩子的好方法包括解释原因、给予小奖励和树立好榜样。

> What advice should young people follow?
> 年轻人应该遵循什么建议？

A:

1. **观点**
   `Young people` should `follow` `advice` about `studying` hard and `staying` `healthy`.
   年轻人应该遵循关于努力学习和保持健康的建议。

2. **原因**
   This `helps` them build a good `future` and `avoid` `problems`.
   这有助于他们建设美好的未来并避免问题。

3. **例子**
   For example, listening to `teachers` and `parents` about `career` `choices` can lead to better `opportunities`.
   例如，听取老师和父母关于职业选择的建议可以带来更好的机会。

---

> How do advertisements persuade people?
> 广告是如何说服人们的？

A:

1. **观点**
   `Advertisements` `persuade` `people` by showing `happy` and `attractive` `images`.
   广告通过展示快乐和有吸引力的图像来说服人们。

2. **原因**
   They make `products` look `fun` and `necessary`, so `people` feel they need to `buy` them.
   它们让产品看起来有趣且必要，所以人们觉得需要购买它们。

3. **例子**
   For instance, a `commercial` for a new `phone` will show `people` having a great `time` using it.
   例如，一个新手机的广告会展示人们使用它时的快乐时光。

## 分析

### 话题词汇/短语

1. `professor` - 教授
2. `advisor` - 导师
3. `persuade` - 劝说
4. `research team` - 研究团队
5. `paper` - 论文
6. `time series` - 时间序列
7. `published` - 发表
8. `international journal` - 国际期刊
9. `opportunity` - 机会
10. `confident` - 自信的
11. `challenges` - 挑战
12. `thankful` - 感激的

### 实用词汇/短语/句式

1. **I want to talk about...** - 我想谈谈... (用于开头引入话题)
2. **This happened...** - 这件事发生在... (用于说明时间地点)
3. **He persuaded me to...** - 他劝我... (用于说明劝说内容)
4. **I was really happy because...** - 我很高兴因为... (用于解释原因)
5. **This experience taught me to...** - 这次经历教会我... (用于说明个人成长)
6. **I'm very thankful to...** - 我很感谢... (用于表达感激之情)
7. **For example,...** - 例如... (用于举例说明)
8. **This is because...** - 这是因为... (用于解释原因)
9. **I think...** - 我认为... (用于表达观点)
10. **They make... look...** - 它们让...看起来... (用于描述广告效果)
