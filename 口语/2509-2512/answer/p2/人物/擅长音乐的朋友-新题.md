# 擅长音乐的朋友

## 题目

### P2

Describe a friend of yours who is good at music/singing. You should say: Who he/she is, When/where you listen to his/her music/singing, What kind of music/songs he/she is good at, And explain how you feel when listening to his music/singing

### P3

1. What kind of music is popular in your country?
2. What kind of music do young people like?
3. Do you think music is beneficial for children at school?
4. What are the benefits of children learning a musical instrument?
5. Do you know what kind of music children like today?
6. Do you think the government should invest more money on concerts?

## 回答

> Describe a friend of yours who is good at music/singing.
> 描述一位擅长音乐/唱歌的朋友。

A:
1.  **思路**: 介绍这位朋友是谁，以及她什么时候、在哪里唱歌。

    Let me tell you about my `friend`, Lily. She is my `classmate` at university, and she is `really good at` `singing`. Sometimes, when we are `relaxing` in our `dorm` after a `long day` of `studying`, she `loves to sing` `popular songs`.
    让我来介绍一下我的朋友Lily。她是我大学的同学，她唱歌真的很好听。有时，当我们学习了一整天，在宿舍里放松的时候，她喜欢唱流行歌曲。

2.  **思路**: 描述她擅长唱什么类型的歌，并具体说出她如何唱歌。

    Lily is `best at` `pop music`. She has a `very sweet voice`, and she can `sing many famous songs` that `everyone knows`. She doesn't just `sing the words`; she also `sings with a lot of feeling`, so it `sounds very nice`.
    Lily最擅长流行音乐。她的声音很甜美，能唱很多大家耳熟能详的著名歌曲。她不只是唱歌词；她还带着很多感情去唱，所以听起来非常棒。

3.  **思路**: 解释听她唱歌时我的感受。

    When I `listen to` her `singing`, I `feel very happy` and `relaxed`. If I am `feeling tired` or `a bit sad`, her `music` can `make me feel better`. It's `like magic`! Her `voice` can `cheer me up` and `make me forget` my `problems` for a while. `Listening to her` is always a `good time`.
    当我听她唱歌时，我感到非常开心和放松。如果我感到疲倦或有点难过，她的音乐能让我感觉好起来。这就像魔法一样！她的声音能让我振作起来，让我暂时忘记烦恼。听她唱歌总是很愉快的时光。

> Do you think music is beneficial for children at school?
> 你认为音乐对学校里的孩子有益吗？

A:
1.  **思路**: 肯定音乐的益处，并从创造力和记忆力两方面说明。

    `Yes`, I `think music` is `very good for children` at `school`. `Firstly`, it `helps them be creative`. When they `sing songs` or `make music`, they can `use their imagination`. `Also`, `music` can `help them remember things better`, `like learning new words` or `counting numbers` in a `fun way`.
    是的，我认为音乐对学校里的孩子非常好。首先，它能帮助他们发挥创造力。当他们唱歌或制作音乐时，他们可以使用他们的想象力。此外，音乐可以帮助他们更好地记住东西，比如用有趣的方式学习新单词或数数。

2.  **思路**: 进一步说明音乐在团队合作和放松方面的作用。

    `Secondly`, if `children sing together` in a `choir` or `play instruments` in a `band`, it `teaches them to work with friends`. This is `very important`. `Moreover`, `listening to music` can be a `good way for children to relax` when they are `feeling stressed` from `schoolwork`.
    其次，如果孩子们在合唱团里一起唱歌，或者在乐队里演奏乐器，这能教会他们如何与朋友合作。这非常重要。此外，听音乐对孩子们来说是一个很好的放松方式，当他们因为学业感到压力时。

> What are the benefits of children learning a musical instrument?
> 孩子学习乐器有什么好处？

A:
1.  **思路**: 从耐心和大脑发展两个角度阐述好处。

    `Learning a musical instrument` has `many benefits` for `children`. `First`, it `teaches them patience`. They `need to practice a lot` to `get better`, and this `helps them understand` that `good things take time`. `Second`, it `makes their brains smarter`. When they `play an instrument`, they `use many parts of their brain`, `like memory` and `hand-eye coordination`.
    学习乐器对孩子有很多好处。首先，它教会他们耐心。他们需要大量练习才能变得更好，这有助于他们明白好事情需要时间。其次，它让他们的大脑更聪明。当他们演奏乐器时，他们会用到大脑的许多部分，比如记忆力和手眼协调能力。

2.  **思路**: 补充乐器学习在情感表达和纪律性方面的益处。

    `Also`, `children` can `show how they feel` through `music`. If they are `happy` or `sad`, they can `play a song` to `express themselves`. `And finally`, `learning an instrument` can `teach them discipline`, because they `need to follow rules` and a `schedule to practice`.
    此外，孩子们可以通过音乐表达他们的感受。如果他们开心或难过，他们可以弹奏一首歌曲来表达自己。最后，学习乐器可以教会他们纪律，因为他们需要遵循规则和练习时间表。

> Do you think the government should invest more money on concerts?
> 你认为政府应该在音乐会上投入更多资金吗？

A:
1.  **思路**: 肯定政府投资音乐会的好处，从文化生活和经济方面说明。

    `I think the government` `could invest more money` on `concerts`. `Firstly`, `concerts` are a `good way for people to have fun` and `enjoy music together`. This `makes our cultural life richer`. `Also`, `concerts` can `help the economy`, because they `bring people to cities`, and these `people spend money` on `food` and `hotels`.
    我认为政府可以投入更多资金举办音乐会。首先，音乐会是人们一起娱乐和欣赏音乐的好方式。这让我们的文化生活更丰富。此外，音乐会可以帮助经济，因为它们吸引人们来到城市，这些人会在食物和酒店上花钱。

2.  **思路**: 承认政府可能面临的其他优先事项，但强调平衡的重要性。

    `However`, I `also understand` that the `government has many other important things` to `spend money on`, `like schools` and `hospitals`. So, it's `important to find a balance`. Maybe they can `support some concerts` but `also make sure` that `money is used for other important services` too.
    然而，我也明白政府还有许多其他重要的事情需要花钱，比如学校和医院。所以，找到一个平衡很重要。也许他们可以支持一些音乐会，但也要确保资金也用于其他重要的服务。
