# 擅长音乐的朋友

## 题目

### P2

Describe a friend of yours who is good at music/singing. You should say: Who he/she is, When/where you listen to his/her music/singing, What kind of music/songs he/she is good at, And explain how you feel when listening to his music/singing

### P3

1. What kind of music is popular in your country?
2. What kind of music do young people like?
3. Do you think music is beneficial for children at school?
4. What are the benefits of children learning a musical instrument?
5. Do you know what kind of music children like today?
6. Do you think the government should invest more money on concerts?

## 回答

> Describe a friend of yours who is good at music/singing. You should say: Who he/she is, When/where you listen to his/her music/singing, What kind of music/songs he/she is good at, And explain how you feel when listening to his music/singing
> 描述一个你擅长音乐/唱歌的朋友。你应该说：他/她是谁，你何时/何地听他/她的音乐/唱歌，他/她擅长什么类型的音乐/歌曲，并解释你听他/她的音乐/唱歌时的感受

A:

1. 介绍朋友身份和基本情况
**I want to talk about my roommate, Ruijie Liu.** He's a computer science student like me, and he's really good at playing the guitar.
我想谈谈我的室友刘瑞杰。他和我一样是计算机专业的学生，他非常擅长弹吉他。

2. 说明听音乐的具体场景和时间
**I often listen to him play in our dorm room.** Usually in the evenings or on weekends when we're both free from studying.
我经常在宿舍听他弹奏。通常是在晚上或者周末我们学习空闲的时候。

3. 描述他擅长的音乐类型
**He's especially good at pop music and folk songs.** He can play many popular songs that everyone knows.
他特别擅长流行音乐和民歌。他能弹奏很多大家都知道的流行歌曲。

4. 表达听他演奏时的感受
**When I listen to him play, I feel very relaxed and happy.** His music makes our dorm feel more lively and comfortable.
当我听他演奏时，我感到非常放松和开心。他的音乐让我们的宿舍感觉更有活力和舒适。

5. 补充音乐带来的积极影响
**Sometimes when I'm stressed from studying, his music helps me feel better.** It's like a nice break from all the schoolwork.
有时候当我学习压力大时，他的音乐帮助我感觉更好。就像是课业中的一个美好休息。

6. 说明音乐在我们友谊中的作用
**We often talk about music together and share our favorite songs.** This has made us become closer friends.
我们经常一起讨论音乐，分享各自喜欢的歌曲。这让我们成为了更亲密的朋友。

7. 总结感受和重要性
**Overall, having a musical friend like him makes college life more enjoyable.** I'm really glad we can share this interest.
总的来说，有一个像他这样懂音乐的朋友让大学生活更加愉快。我很高兴我们能分享这个兴趣。

> What kind of music is popular in your country?
> 在你的国家，什么类型的音乐很受欢迎？

A:

1. 直接回答流行音乐类型
**In China, pop music is very popular among most people.** Many people enjoy listening to both Chinese and international pop songs.
在中国，流行音乐在大多数人中很受欢迎。很多人喜欢听中文和国际流行歌曲。

2. 补充其他受欢迎的类型
**Also, traditional Chinese music and folk songs are quite popular,** especially among older generations.
另外，中国传统音乐和民歌也相当受欢迎，特别是在老一辈人中。

3. 举例说明流行程度
**You can hear pop music everywhere** - in shopping malls, restaurants, and on the radio.
你到处都能听到流行音乐 - 在商场、餐厅和广播中。

---

> What kind of music do young people like?
> 年轻人喜欢什么类型的音乐？

A:

1. 说明年轻人主要喜欢的类型
**Most young people in China prefer pop music and hip-hop.** These styles are modern and energetic.
中国的大多数年轻人更喜欢流行音乐和嘻哈音乐。这些风格现代且充满活力。

2. 解释喜欢的原因
**They like these types because the beats are strong** and the lyrics often talk about things young people care about.
他们喜欢这些类型是因为节奏感强，而且歌词经常谈论年轻人关心的事情。

3. 举例说明流行程度
**Many young people follow popular singers** and go to concerts when they can.
很多年轻人关注流行歌手，并在有机会时去听演唱会。

---

> Do you think music is beneficial for children at school?
> 你认为音乐对学校里的孩子有益吗？

A:

1. 明确表达肯定观点
**Yes, I definitely think music is very beneficial for children at school.** It helps them in many ways.
是的，我绝对认为音乐对学校里的孩子非常有益。它在很多方面帮助他们。

2. 说明具体益处
**Music can help children relax** after studying and make school more enjoyable.
音乐可以帮助孩子们在学习后放松，让学校生活更加愉快。

3. 补充其他好处
**It also helps develop their creativity** and can improve their memory skills.
它还有助于发展他们的创造力，并能提高记忆能力。

---

> What are the benefits of children learning a musical instrument?
> 孩子们学习乐器有什么好处？

A:

1. 列举主要好处
**Learning an instrument helps children develop patience and discipline.** They need to practice regularly to improve.
学习乐器帮助孩子们培养耐心和纪律性。他们需要定期练习来提高。

2. 说明认知发展好处
**It also improves their concentration** and helps with brain development.
它还能提高他们的注意力，并有助于大脑发育。

3. 提到社交和情感益处
**Plus, it gives them confidence** when they can play well for others.
另外，当他们能为他人演奏得很好时，这会给他们带来自信。

---

> Do you know what kind of music children like today?
> 你知道现在的孩子们喜欢什么类型的音乐吗？

A:

1. 说明观察到的偏好
**From what I've seen, children today like simple, happy songs** with catchy melodies.
根据我的观察，现在的孩子们喜欢简单、快乐的歌曲，旋律朗朗上口。

2. 提及流行文化的影响
**They often enjoy songs from cartoons and movies** that they watch.
他们经常喜欢来自他们观看的卡通和电影中的歌曲。

3. 说明年龄差异
**Younger children prefer nursery rhymes,** while older kids start liking pop music like teenagers.
年幼的孩子更喜欢童谣，而大一点的孩子开始喜欢像青少年那样的流行音乐。

---

> Do you think the government should invest more money on concerts?
> 你认为政府应该在音乐会上投入更多资金吗？

A:

1. 表达支持观点
**I think it would be good if the government invested more in concerts,** especially free ones for the public.
我认为如果政府在音乐会上投入更多资金会很好，特别是为公众提供免费音乐会。

2. 说明文化益处
**This would help make music more accessible to everyone** and support local artists.
这将有助于让音乐对每个人都更容易接触，并支持本地艺术家。

3. 提出平衡建议
**However, they should also balance this with funding for other important areas** like education and healthcare.
然而，他们也应该平衡这一点，为其他重要领域如教育和医疗保健提供
