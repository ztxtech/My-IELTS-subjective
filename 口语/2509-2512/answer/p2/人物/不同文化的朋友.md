# 不同文化的朋友

## 题目

### P2

Describe a person from a different cultural background with whom you enjoy spending time You should say: Who he/she is Where he/she is from Where/how you knew him/her And explain how you feel about him/her

### P3

1. Is it easy to meet people from different cultural backgrounds in your country?
2. What do people from different cultural backgrounds usually talk about with each other?
3. Is it good to live in a multicultural society?
4. What are the advantages of being friends with people from different cultural backgrounds?
5. Why do people choose to travel or live abroad?
6. What are the benefits of living in another country?

## 回答

> Describe a person from a different cultural background with whom you enjoy spending time You should say: Who he/she is Where he/she is from Where/how you knew him/her And explain how you feel about him/her
> 描述一个你喜欢的来自不同文化背景的人。你应该说：他/她是谁；他/她来自哪里；你在哪里/如何认识他/她的；并解释你对他/她的感觉

A:

1. 直接回答问题，介绍朋友身份
   
   I'd like to talk about my roommate, `Ruijie Liu`. He comes from a different province in China, which has its own unique culture and traditions.

   我想谈谈我的室友刘瑞杰。他来自中国的一个不同省份，那里有自己独特的文化和传统。

2. 说明认识的方式和地点
   
   We met at university when we became roommates in our dormitory. We both study computer science and share the same room.

   我们在大学认识，当时我们成了宿舍室友。我们都学习计算机科学，住在同一个房间。

3. 描述文化差异的具体表现
   
   Although we're both Chinese, his hometown has different food and customs. For example, he loves spicy food more than I do, and he celebrates some local festivals that are new to me.

   虽然我们都是中国人，但他的家乡有不同的食物和习俗。比如，他比我更爱吃辣，他庆祝的一些当地节日对我来说是新的。

4. 表达积极的感受和体验
   
   I really enjoy spending time with him because he teaches me about his culture, and we learn from each other. It makes our friendship more interesting and fun.

   我真的很喜欢和他在一起，因为他教我了解他的文化，我们互相学习。这让我们的友谊更有趣、更好玩。

5. 总结感受
   
   Overall, I feel happy and lucky to have such a friend from a different background. He helps me see things from different perspectives.

   总的来说，我很高兴也很幸运有这样一个来自不同背景的朋友。他帮助我从不同的角度看问题。

> 1. Is it easy to meet people from different cultural backgrounds in your country?
> 1. 在你的国家容易遇到来自不同文化背景的人吗？

A:

1. 给出观点
   
   **Yes, it's quite easy** to meet people from different cultures in China, especially in big cities like Chengdu where I live.

   是的，在中国遇到来自不同文化的人相当容易，特别是我居住的成都这样的大城市。

2. 解释原因
   
   **This is because** many people from different parts of China come to study and work in big cities, bringing their unique cultures with them.

   这是因为许多来自中国不同地区的人来大城市学习和工作，带来了他们独特的文化。

3. 举例说明
   
   **For example**, in my university, there are students from all over the country, each with their own local traditions and ways of life.

   例如，在我的大学里，有来自全国各地的学生，每个人都有自己当地的传统和生活方式。

> 2. What do people from different cultural backgrounds usually talk about with each other?
> 2. 来自不同文化背景的人通常彼此谈论什么？

A:

1. 给出观点
   
   **I think** they usually talk about their daily lives and cultural differences.

   我认为他们通常谈论他们的日常生活和文化差异。

2. 解释原因
   
   **The main reason is that** these topics help them understand each other better and find common interests.

   主要原因是这些话题帮助他们更好地理解彼此，找到共同的兴趣。

3. 举例说明
   
   **For instance**, my roommate and I often discuss our hometown foods and local festivals, which is always interesting.

   例如，我的室友和我经常讨论我们家乡的食物和当地节日，这总是很有趣。

> 3. Is it good to live in a multicultural society?
> 3. 生活在多元文化社会好吗？

A:

1. 给出观点
   
   **Yes, I believe** living in a multicultural society is very good.

   是的，我相信生活在多元文化社会非常好。

2. 解释原因
   
   **This is because** it allows people to learn about different cultures and become more open-minded.

   这是因为它让人们了解不同的文化，变得更加开放。

3. 举例说明
   
   **For example**, when people experience different traditions and foods, they become more understanding and tolerant of others.

   例如，当人们体验不同的传统和食物时，他们会变得更加理解和宽容他人。

> 4. What are the advantages of being friends with people from different cultural backgrounds?
> 4. 与来自不同文化背景的人交朋友有什么优势？

A:

1. 给出观点
   
   **There are several advantages** to having friends from different cultures.

   与来自不同文化的人交朋友有几个优势。

2. 解释原因
   
   **The main benefit is that** you can learn new things and see the world from different perspectives.

   主要好处是你可以学习新事物，从不同的角度看世界。

3. 举例说明
   
   **For instance**, my friend from another province taught me about his local festivals, which made me appreciate cultural diversity more.

   例如，我来自另一个省份的朋友教我了解他当地的节日，这让我更欣赏文化多样性。

> 5. Why do people choose to travel or live abroad?
> 5. 为什么人们选择出国旅行或居住？

A:

1. 给出观点
   
   **People usually** travel or live abroad to experience new cultures and opportunities.

   人们通常出国旅行或居住是为了体验新的文化和机会。

2. 解释原因
   
   **This is because** living in another country allows them to learn new languages, meet different people, and gain new experiences.

   这是因为在另一个国家生活让他们学习新语言，认识不同的人，获得新的体验。

3. 举例说明
   
   **For example**, many students go abroad to study because they want to get a better education and experience life in a different culture.

   例如，许多学生出国学习是因为他们想要获得更好的教育，体验不同文化的生活。

> 6. What are the benefits of living in another country?
> 6. 在另一个国家生活有什么好处？

A:

1. 给出观点
   
   **Living in another country** has many benefits, such as personal growth and cultural understanding.

   在另一个国家生活有很多好处，比如个人成长和文化理解。

2. 解释原因
   
   **The main advantage is that** it helps people become more independent and broadens their worldview.

   主要优势是它帮助人们变得更加独立，拓宽他们的世界观。

3. 举例说明
   
   **For instance**, when people live abroad, they learn to adapt to new environments and understand different ways of life, which makes them more confident and open-minded.

   例如，当人们在国外生活时，他们学会适应新环境，理解不同的生活方式，这让他们更加自信和开放。
