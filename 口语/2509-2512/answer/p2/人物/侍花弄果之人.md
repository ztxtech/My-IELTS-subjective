# 侍花弄果之人

## 题目

### P2

Describe a person you know who loves to grow plants (e.g. vegetables/fruits/flowers etc.) You should say: Who this person is What he/she grows Where he/she grows them And explain why he/she enjoys growing plants

### P3

1. Are there many people growing their own vegetables now?
2. Do you think it's good to let kids learn how to plant?
3. What do you think of the job of a farmer?
4. What are the differences between traditional and modern agriculture? What happened to the farmers' income during the pandemic?
5. How do people grow plants in cities?

## 回答

> Describe a person you know who loves to grow plants
> 描述一个你喜欢种植物的人

A:
1. **直接回答问题，介绍人物**
I'd like to talk about my roommate, Ruijie Liu. He's really into growing plants in our dorm room.
我想谈谈我的室友刘瑞杰。他真的很喜欢在我们的宿舍里种植物。

2. **说明他种植的内容**
He mainly grows small herbs like mint and basil, and some pretty flowers. He keeps them in small pots on our windowsill.
他主要种一些小草药，比如薄荷和罗勒，还有一些漂亮的花。他把它们放在我们窗台上的小盆里。

3. **解释他喜欢的原因**
I think he enjoys it because it's very relaxing. After studying computer science all day, taking care of plants helps him feel calm. Also, he says it's nice to watch something grow because of his care.
我认为他喜欢这个是因为这很放松。在整天学习计算机科学之后，照顾植物帮助他感到平静。而且，他说看着东西因为他的照顾而成长是很好的。

4. **补充说明**
Sometimes he even uses the mint in his tea. It's really fresh and tastes much better than store-bought.
有时他甚至把薄荷用在茶里。真的很新鲜，味道比商店买的好多了。

## 分析

### 话题词汇/短语

1. `grow plants` - 种植植物
2. `herbs` - 草药
3. `mint and basil` - 薄荷和罗勒
4. `pots` - 花盆
5. `windowsill` - 窗台
6. `relaxing` - 放松的
7. `take care of plants` - 照顾植物
8. `feel calm` - 感到平静

### 实用词汇/短语/句式

1. **I'd like to talk about...** - 我想谈谈...
2. **He's really into...** - 他真的很喜欢...
3. **I think he enjoys it because...** - 我认为他喜欢这个是因为...
4. **Also, he says...** - 而且，他说...
5. **Sometimes he even...** - 有时他甚至...

> Are there many people growing their own vegetables now?
> 现在有很多人自己种蔬菜吗？

A:
1. **表达观点**
Actually, I think more and more people are starting to grow their own vegetables now.
实际上，我认为现在越来越多人开始自己种蔬菜了。

2. **说明原因**
This is probably because people want to eat healthier food and know exactly where it comes from.
这可能是因为人们想吃更健康的食物，并且确切知道食物从哪里来。

3. **举例说明**
For example, some of my friends' parents have small gardens where they grow tomatoes and carrots.
例如，我一些朋友的父母有小花园，他们在那里种西红柿和胡萝卜。

> Do you think it's good to let kids learn how to plant?
> 你认为让孩子们学习种植好吗？

A:
1. **表达观点**
Yes, definitely. I think it's very good for children to learn how to plant.
是的，当然。我认为让孩子们学习种植非常好。

2. **说明好处**
It teaches them about nature and where food comes from. Also, it can be a fun outdoor activity.
这教他们了解自然和食物从哪里来。而且，这可以是有趣的户外活动。

3. **补充说明**
They can feel proud when they see the plants they planted growing.
当他们看到他们种的植物生长时，他们会感到自豪。

---

> What do you think of the job of a farmer?
> 你对农民的工作有什么看法？

A:
1. **表达观点**
I think being a farmer is very important but also very hard work.
我认为当农民非常重要但也是非常辛苦的工作。

2. **说明原因**
Farmers work long hours outdoors to grow food for everyone. Without them, we wouldn't have food to eat.
农民长时间在户外工作，为每个人种植食物。没有他们，我们就没食物吃。

3. **补充说明**
They need to know a lot about weather, soil, and plants to be successful.
他们需要了解很多关于天气、土壤和植物的知识才能成功。

> What are the differences between traditional and modern agriculture? What happened to the farmers' income during the pandemic?
> 传统农业和现代农业有什么区别？疫情期间农民的收入发生了什么变化？

A:
1. **回答第一部分**
Traditional farming uses more manual work, while modern farming uses machines and technology.
传统农业使用更多的人工劳动，而现代农业使用机器和技术。

2. **回答第二部分**
During the pandemic, I think many farmers had problems because they couldn't sell their products easily.
疫情期间，我认为许多农民遇到了问题，因为他们不能轻易销售他们的产品。

3. **补充说明**
Some farmers' income probably went down because markets were closed or people couldn't go out to buy food.
一些农民的收入可能下降了，因为市场关闭或者人们不能外出购买食物。

> How do people grow plants in cities?
> 人们在城市里如何种植植物？

A:
1. **表达观点**
People in cities find creative ways to grow plants.
城市里的人们找到有创意的方式种植植物。

2. **说明方法**
They use balconies, rooftops, or even small spaces near windows. Some people use special pots that don't need much space.
他们使用阳台、屋顶，甚至窗户附近的小空间。一些人使用不需要太多空间的特殊花盆。

3. **举例说明**
I've seen some restaurants growing herbs in small boxes outside their kitchens.
我看到一些餐厅在厨房外的小盒子里种植
