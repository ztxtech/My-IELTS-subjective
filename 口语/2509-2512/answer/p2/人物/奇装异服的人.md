# 奇装异服的人

## 题目

### P2

Describe a person who you think wears unusual clothes You should say: Who this person is How you knew this person What his/her clothes are like And explain why you think his/her clothes are unusual

### P3

1. What are the differences between clothes worn by old people and those by young people?
2. What kind of clothes do people wear in the workplace?
3. Do you think it is a good idea to buy clothes online?
4. Do you think young people wear unusual clothes more than other age groups? Do you think that the style of the clothing is more important than its
5. comfort?
6. What factors affect people s decisions when buying clothes?

## 回答

> Describe a person who you think wears unusual clothes You should say: Who this person is How you knew this person What his/her clothes are like And explain why you think his/her clothes are unusual
> 描述一个你认为穿着奇装异服的人 你应该说：这个人是谁 你是怎么认识这个人的 他/她的衣服是什么样的 并解释为什么你认为他/她的衣服很特别

A:

1. 直接介绍人物身份
   
   I'd like to talk about my roommate, `Ruijie Liu`. He studies computer science with me at university.

   我想谈谈我的室友刘瑞杰。他和我在大学一起学习计算机科学。

2. 说明认识方式和人物特点
   
   We live in the same dormitory, so I see him every day. He's really into music and often plays guitar in our room.

   我们住在同一个宿舍，所以我每天都能见到他。他非常喜欢音乐，经常在房间里弹吉他。

3. 描述服装特点
   
   His clothes are quite unusual. He often wears bright `colorful shirts` with interesting patterns, and sometimes he wears `hats` that look very artistic.

   他的衣服相当特别。他经常穿颜色鲜艳、图案有趣的衬衫，有时还戴看起来很有艺术感的帽子。

4. 进一步描述服装细节
   
   What makes his style special is that he mixes traditional Chinese elements with modern fashion. For example, he might wear a `modern jacket` with some traditional embroidery.

   让他的风格特别的是，他把中国传统元素和现代时尚混搭在一起。比如，他可能会穿一件带有传统刺绣的现代夹克。

5. 解释为什么觉得特别
   
   I think his clothes are unusual because most students in our university wear simple `T-shirts` and `jeans`, but he always stands out with his unique style.

   我觉得他的衣服很特别，因为我们大学里大多数学生都穿简单的T恤和牛仔裤，但他总是以独特的风格脱颖而出。

6. 补充说明服装背后的意义
   
   Actually, his unusual clothing reflects his personality - he's creative and doesn't like to follow the crowd. His clothes are like his way of expressing himself.

   实际上，他特别的着装反映了他的个性——他有创造力，不喜欢随大流。他的衣服就像他表达自我的方式。

7. 总结感受
   
   Although his clothes might seem unusual to some people, I think they suit him perfectly and show who he really is.

   虽然他的衣服对某些人来说可能显得特别，但我觉得它们非常适合他，并且展现了他真实的样子。

> What are the differences between clothes worn by old people and those by young people?
> 老年人和年轻人穿的衣服有什么不同？

A:

1. 提出主要差异观点
   
   **Well, I think** the main difference is in `colors` and `styles`. Old people usually prefer darker colors and more traditional styles, while young people like brighter colors and more fashionable designs.

   嗯，我认为主要区别在于颜色和款式。老年人通常更喜欢深色和更传统的款式，而年轻人喜欢更鲜艳的颜色和更时尚的设计。

2. 举例说明差异
   
   **For example**, my grandfather always wears dark `suits` and formal `shoes`, but my younger cousin wears colorful `sneakers` and trendy `hoodies`.

   例如，我祖父总是穿深色西装和正式鞋子，但我表弟穿彩色运动鞋和时尚连帽衫。

3. 总结差异原因
   
   **I believe** this difference comes from different generations having different tastes and lifestyles.

   我相信这种差异来自于不同代际有不同的品味和生活方式。

> What kind of clothes do people wear in the workplace?
> 人们在职场穿什么样的衣服？

A:

1. 说明职场着装的一般要求
   
   **It depends on** the type of job, but **in most cases** people wear formal clothes like `suits`, `dresses`, or `business casual` outfits in the workplace.

   这取决于工作的类型，但在大多数情况下，人们在职场穿正式服装，比如西装、连衣裙或商务休闲装。

2. 举例说明不同场合的着装
   
   **For instance**, in `offices`, people usually wear `button-down shirts` and `dress pants`, while in more creative jobs like `design companies`, the dress code might be more relaxed.

   例如，在办公室里，人们通常穿纽扣衬衫和西裤，而在设计公司等更有创意的工作中，着装要求可能更宽松。

3. 总结着装目的
   
   The main purpose of workplace clothing is to look professional and make a good impression on clients and colleagues.

   职场着装的主要目的是看起来专业，给客户和同事留下好印象。

> Do you think it is a good idea to buy clothes online?
> 你认为在网上买衣服是个好主意吗？

A:

1. 提出观点
   
   **Yes, I think** buying clothes online can be a good idea because it's very convenient and offers more choices.

   是的，我认为在网上买衣服可能是个好主意，因为它非常方便并且提供更多选择。

2. 说明优点
   
   **The main advantage is that** you can shop from home and compare prices from different stores easily. Also, online shopping often has better sales and discounts.

   主要优势是你可以从家里购物，并且轻松比较不同商店的价格。而且，网上购物通常有更好的促销和折扣。

3. 提到注意事项
   
   **However**, you need to be careful about sizes and quality since you can't try on the clothes before buying them.

   然而，你需要小心尺寸和质量，因为你在购买前不能试穿衣服。

> Do you think young people wear unusual clothes more than other age groups?
> 你认为年轻人比其他年龄段的人更常穿奇装异服吗？

A:

1. 直接回答
   
   **Yes, definitely**. Young people tend to wear more unusual clothes than other age groups.

   是的，当然。年轻人往往比其他年龄段的人穿更特别的衣服。

2. 解释原因
   
   **This is because** young people are more experimental with fashion and want to express their individuality. They're not afraid to try new styles and stand out from the crowd.

   这是因为年轻人对时尚更爱尝试，并且想要表达他们的个性。他们不害怕尝试新风格并从人群中脱颖而出。

3. 举例说明
   
   **For example**, you often see teenagers wearing `unique accessories` or `dyed hair` that older generations might not feel comfortable wearing.

   例如，你经常看到青少年戴独特的配饰或染发，这些是老一辈可能不太愿意尝试的。

> Do you think that the style of the clothing is more important than its comfort?
> 你认为服装的款式比它的舒适度更重要吗？

A:

1. 提出平衡观点
   
   **Well, it's a balance between both**. I think both style and comfort are important, but it depends on the occasion.

   嗯，这是两者之间的平衡。我认为款式和舒适度都很重要，但这取决于场合。

2. 说明不同场合的需求
   
   **For example**, for special events like parties or meetings, style might be more important. But for everyday wear, comfort should be the priority.

   例如，对于派对或会议等特殊场合，款式可能更重要。但对于日常穿着，舒适度应该是优先考虑的。

3. 总结个人看法
   
   **Personally**, I prefer clothes that are both stylish and comfortable because I want to look good but also feel good in what I'm wearing.

   就个人而言，我更喜欢既时尚又舒适的衣服，因为我想要看起来好看，同时穿起来感觉也好。

> What factors affect people s decisions when buying clothes?
> 人们买衣服时哪些因素会影响他们的决定？

A:

1. 列举主要因素
   
   **There are several factors** that affect clothing choices, including `price`, `quality`, `style`, and `comfort`.

   有几个因素会影响服装选择，包括价格、质量、款式和舒适度。

2. 详细说明影响因素
   
   **Also**, people consider the `occasion` - whether they need clothes for work, casual wear, or special events. `Brand reputation` and `personal preferences` also play important roles.

   而且，人们会考虑场合——他们需要工作服装、休闲装还是特殊场合的服装。品牌声誉和个人偏好也扮演重要角色。

3. 总结决策过程
   
   **Ultimately**, most people try to find clothes that match their personal style while staying within their budget and meeting their practical needs.

   最终，大多数人试图找到符合他们个人风格、在预算内并满足实际需求的衣服。
