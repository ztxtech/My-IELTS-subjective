# 童年朋友

## 题目

### P2

Describe a friend from your childhood You should say: Who he/she is Where and how you met each other What you often did together And explain what made you like him/her

### P3

1. Do you still keep in touch with your friends from childhood? Why or why not?
2. How important is childhood friendship to children?
3. What do you think of communicating via social media?
4. Do you think online communication through social media will replace face-to-face communication?
5. What's the difference between having younger friends and older friends? Has technology changed people's friendships? How?

## 回答

> Describe a friend from your childhood You should say: Who he/she is Where and how you met each other What you often did together And explain what made you like him/her
> 描述一个你童年时期的朋友。你应该说：他/她是谁；你们在哪里以及如何相遇；你们经常一起做什么；并解释你喜欢他/她的原因

A:

1. **直接回答问题并介绍人物**
   My childhood friend is `Ruijie Liu`. We met in primary school in my hometown, `Chizhou`.
   我童年的朋友是刘瑞杰。我们在我的家乡池州的小学相遇。
   
2. **说明相遇方式和地点**
   We were in the same class and sat next to each other. That's how we became friends.
   我们在同一个班级，座位相邻。就是这样我们成为了朋友。
   
3. **描述经常一起做的事情**
   We often played basketball after school and did homework together. On weekends, we went to the park or played video games.
   我们经常放学后打篮球，一起做作业。周末我们去公园或者玩电子游戏。
   
4. **解释喜欢的原因**
   I liked him because he was funny and kind. He always helped me with schoolwork and made me laugh.
   我喜欢他因为他很有趣而且善良。他总是帮助我学习，逗我笑。
   
5. **补充说明友谊的持续性**
   Even though we now live in different cities for university, we still talk sometimes.
   尽管我们现在因为上大学住在不同的城市，我们仍然有时会联系。
   
6. **总结友谊的价值**
   Childhood friendship is special because it's based on simple, happy times.
   童年友谊很特别，因为它是建立在简单快乐的时光基础上的。

> 3. What do you think of communicating via social media?
> 3. 你如何看待通过社交媒体进行交流？

A:

1. **表达观点**
   Communicating via `social media` has both good and bad points.
   通过社交媒体交流有好处也有坏处。
   
2. **说明优点**
   The good thing is that it's convenient. We can talk to friends anytime, anywhere.
   好处是很方便。我们可以随时随地和朋友聊天。
   
3. **说明缺点**
   But sometimes it feels less personal than face-to-face talking. We miss seeing real smiles and feelings.
   但有时感觉不如面对面交流那么亲切。我们错过了看到真实笑容和情感的机会。

3. `face-to-face` - 面对面
4. `personal` - 亲切的、个人的

### 实用词汇/短语/句式

2. **anytime, anywhere** - 随时随地
3. **less...than...** - 不如...那么...

> 4. Do you think online communication through social media will replace face-to-face communication?
> 4. 你认为通过社交媒体的在线交流将取代面对面交流吗？

A:

1. **直接回答**
   No, I don't think online communication will replace face-to-face communication.
   不，我认为在线交流不会取代面对面交流。
   
2. **解释原因**
   People still need real human connection. Meeting in person allows us to feel emotions better.
   人们仍然需要真实的人际联系。面对面见面让我们能更好地感受情感。
   
3. **补充说明**
   Online communication is good for keeping in touch, but it can't replace the warmth of real meetings.
   在线交流适合保持联系，但不能取代真实见面的温暖。

### 话题词汇/短语

3. `in person` - 亲自、面对面
4. `feel emotions` - 感受情感

2. **allows us to...** - 让我们能够...

> 5. What's the difference between having younger friends and older friends? Has technology changed people's friendships? How?
> 5. 拥有年轻朋友和年长朋友有什么区别？技术改变了人们的友谊吗？如何改变的？

A:

1. **回答第一部分问题**
   Younger friends are usually more energetic and like new things. Older friends often have more experience and can give good advice.
   年轻朋友通常更有活力，喜欢新事物。年长朋友往往更有经验，能给出好建议。
   
2. **回答第二部分问题**
   Yes, `technology` has definitely changed friendships.
   是的，技术确实改变了友谊。
   
3. **解释如何改变**
   Now we can make friends from all over the world through social media. But sometimes we spend so much time online that we forget to meet real friends.
   现在我们可以通过社交媒体交到世界各地的朋友。但有时我们花太多时间在网上，忘记了去见真实的朋友。

### 话题词汇/短语

1. `younger friends` - 年轻朋友
2. `older friends` - 年长朋友
3. `energetic` - 有活力的
4. `technology` - 技术
5. `social media` - 社交媒体

### 实用词汇/短语/句式

1. **usually more...** - 通常更...
2. **often have more...** - 往往有更多...
3. **definitely changed** - 确实改变了
4. **all over the world** - 世界各地
5. **spend so much time...that...** - 花太多时间...以至于
