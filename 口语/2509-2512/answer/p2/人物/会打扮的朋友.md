# 会打扮的朋友

## 题目

### P2

Describe a friend of yours who is well-dressed and is good at dressing up You should say: Who he/she is How you knew him/her What his/her dressing style is And explain why he/she dresses this way

### P3

1. Do most people in your country prefer to buy clothes online or at the street market? Why?
2. Do you think young people know more about fashion and are better at dressing up than elderly people?
3. Do you think people would use clothing to show their identity?
4. Why do many people prefer to buy rather expensive clothes?
5. What are the differences between cheap and expensive clothes?
6. Why do some people care so much about their clothing?

## 回答

> Describe a friend of yours who is well-dressed and is good at dressing up You should say: Who he/she is How you knew him/her What his/her dressing style is And explain why he/she dresses this way
> 描述一个你穿着得体、善于打扮的朋友。你应该说：他/她是谁，你是怎么认识他/她的，他/她的穿衣风格是什么，并解释他/她为什么这样穿

A:

1. **直接介绍朋友** (先明确回答题目要求)

I want to talk about my roommate, `Ruijie Liu`. We study computer science together at university and live in the same dorm room.

我想谈谈我的室友`刘瑞杰`。我们在大学一起学习计算机科学，住在同一个宿舍。

2. **说明相识过程** (回答how you knew him/her)

I met him on the first day of university when we moved into our dorm. We became friends quickly because we share the same major and interests.

我在大学第一天搬进宿舍时认识了他。我们很快成为了朋友，因为我们有相同的专业和兴趣。

3. **描述穿衣风格** (回答what his dressing style is)

He always wears `casual but stylish clothes`. He likes wearing `comfortable jeans` with `nice t-shirts` and `cool sneakers`. Sometimes he wears `smart jackets` that make him look really good.

他总是穿`休闲但时尚的衣服`。他喜欢穿`舒适的牛仔裤`配`好看的T恤`和`酷炫的运动鞋`。有时他会穿`帅气的外套`，这让他看起来很好看。

4. **解释原因1** (开始解释why he dresses this way)

He dresses this way because he wants to feel `comfortable and confident`. He says that when he wears clothes he likes, it makes him feel better about himself.

他这样穿是因为他想感到`舒适和自信`。他说当他穿自己喜欢的衣服时，会让他自我感觉更好。

5. **解释原因2** (进一步解释原因)

Also, he loves `music and art`, so he uses his clothes to `express his personality`. His style shows that he is `creative and fun`.

而且，他热爱`音乐和艺术`，所以他用衣服来`表达自己的个性`。他的风格显示他是`有创造力和有趣的人`。

6. **举例说明** (用具体例子支持)

For example, he has a `guitar-themed t-shirt` that he wears when he practices music. This shows how his clothes match his hobbies.

例如，他有一件`吉他主题的T恤`，他在练习音乐时会穿。这显示了他的衣服如何与他的爱好相匹配。

7. **总结观点** (结束回答)

So, I think his dressing style is not just about looking good, but also about showing who he really is as a person.

所以，我认为他的穿衣风格不仅仅是看起来好看，更是展示他真实自我的方式。

> 1. Do most people in your country prefer to buy clothes online or at the street market? Why?
> 1. 在你们国家，大多数人更喜欢在网上还是在街市买衣服？为什么？

A:

1. **给出观点** (直接回答问题)

I think most young people in China prefer to `buy clothes online` these days.

我认为现在中国大多数年轻人更喜欢`在网上买衣服`。

2. **解释原因** (说明为什么)

The main reason is that `online shopping is more convenient`. You can browse many different styles and compare prices without leaving your home.

主要原因是`网上购物更方便`。你可以在不出门的情况下浏览许多不同款式并比较价格。

3. **举例说明** (用例子支持观点)

For example, my friends and I often use shopping apps like Taobao because we can find more choices and better deals there.

例如，我和我的朋友经常使用淘宝这样的购物应用，因为我们可以在那里找到更多选择和更好的优惠。

> 2. Do you think young people know more about fashion and are better at dressing up than elderly people?
> 2. 你认为年轻人比老年人更懂时尚、更会打扮吗？

A:

1. **给出观点** (表达基本看法)

Yes, I generally think `young people` know more about current fashion trends.

是的，我普遍认为`年轻人`更了解当前的时尚趋势。

2. **解释原因** (说明原因)

This is because they spend more time on `social media and the internet`, where they can see the latest styles from around the world.

这是因为他们花更多时间在`社交媒体和互联网上`，在那里他们可以看到来自世界各地的最新款式。

3. **补充说明** (提供平衡的观点)

However, some elderly people also have their own `classic and elegant` dressing style that is timeless.

然而，一些老年人也有自己`经典优雅`的穿衣风格，这种风格是永恒的。

> 3. Do you think people would use clothing to show their identity?
> 3. 你认为人们会用服装来展示自己的身份吗？

A:

1. **明确回答** (直接表明立场)

Yes, definitely. I believe `clothing is a powerful way` to show who you are.

是的，当然。我相信`服装是一种强有力的方式`来展示你是谁。

2. **解释原因** (说明为什么)

People often choose clothes that reflect their `personality, interests, and values`. For example, someone who loves sports might wear athletic clothes.

人们经常选择反映他们`个性、兴趣和价值观`的衣服。例如，热爱运动的人可能会穿运动服。

3. **举例说明** (用具体例子支持)

My friend Ruijie wears music-themed clothes because he wants people to know about his passion for music.

我的朋友瑞杰穿音乐主题的衣服，因为他想让人们知道他对音乐的热情。

> 4. Why do many people prefer to buy rather expensive clothes?
> 4. 为什么很多人更喜欢买相当贵的衣服？

A:

1. **给出原因1** (第一个主要原因)

Many people believe that `expensive clothes` are of `better quality` and will last longer.

许多人认为`昂贵的衣服``质量更好`，而且会更耐用。

2. **给出原因2** (第二个原因)

Also, some people buy expensive brands because they want to `show their status` or feel more confident.

而且，有些人购买昂贵的品牌是因为他们想`展示自己的地位`或者感到更自信。

3. **总结观点** (简要总结)

So it's often about both practical reasons and personal feelings.

所以这通常既是出于实际原因，也是出于个人感受。

> 5. What are the differences between cheap and expensive clothes?
> 5. 便宜和贵的衣服有什么区别？

A:

1. **质量差异** (第一个主要区别)

The main difference is in `quality`. Expensive clothes usually use `better materials` and have `better craftsmanship`.

主要区别在于`质量`。昂贵的衣服通常使用`更好的材料`并且有`更好的工艺`。

2. **品牌价值** (第二个区别)

Another difference is the `brand value`. Expensive clothes often come from well-known brands that people trust.

另一个区别是`品牌价值`。昂贵的衣服通常来自人们信任的知名品牌。

3. **设计细节** (第三个区别)

Also, expensive clothes often have more `unique designs` and attention to detail compared to cheaper ones.

而且，与便宜的衣服相比，昂贵的衣服通常有更`独特的设计`和对细节的关注。

> 6. Why do some people care so much about their clothing?
> 6. 为什么有些人如此在意他们的衣着？

A:

1. **个人形象** (第一个原因)

Some people care about clothing because it affects how `others see them`. They want to make a good impression.

有些人在意衣着是因为它影响`别人如何看待他们`。他们想留下好印象。

2. **自我表达** (第二个原因)

For others, clothing is a way to `express themselves` and show their unique personality.

对其他人来说，服装是一种`表达自己`和展示独特个性的方式。

3. **自信心** (第三个原因)

Also, wearing nice clothes can make people feel more `confident and happy` in their daily lives.

而且，穿漂亮的衣服可以让人们在日常生活中感到更`自信和快乐`。
