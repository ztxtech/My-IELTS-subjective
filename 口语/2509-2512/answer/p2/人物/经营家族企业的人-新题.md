# 经营家族企业的人

## 题目

### P2

Describe a person you know who runs a family business. You should say: Who he/she is, What the business is, What products it sells, And explain what you have learned from him/her

### P3

1. Would you like to start a family business?
2. What are the advantages and disadvantages of family business?

## 回答

> Describe a person you know who runs a family business. You should say: Who he/she is, What the business is, What products it sells, And explain what you have learned from him/her
> 描述一个你认识的经营家族企业的人。你应该说：他/她是谁，企业是什么，销售什么产品，以及你从他/她身上学到了什么

A:

1. **直接回应并介绍人物** (介绍我认识的经营家族企业的人)

I'd like to talk about my friend Ruijie Liu's father. He runs a small `bakery` in their hometown.

我想谈谈我朋友刘瑞杰的父亲。他在家乡经营一家小型面包店。

2. **描述企业性质** (说明企业类型和产品)

It's a family business that sells fresh bread, cakes, and cookies. They make everything themselves every morning.

这是一家家族企业，销售新鲜面包、蛋糕和饼干。他们每天早上自己制作所有产品。

3. **说明产品特色** (具体描述产品特点)

Their `specialty` is birthday cakes with beautiful designs. They also make traditional Chinese pastries during festivals.

他们的特色是带有漂亮设计的生日蛋糕。节日期间他们还制作传统中式糕点。

4. **解释学习内容1** (从中学到的第一点)

From him, I learned that running a business requires a lot of `hard work`. He wakes up very early to prepare everything.

从他身上，我了解到经营企业需要付出很多努力。他每天很早就起床准备一切。

5. **解释学习内容2** (从中学到的第二点)

I also learned about `customer service`. He always remembers what his regular customers like and makes them feel special.

我还学到了关于客户服务的知识。他总是记得老顾客喜欢什么，让他们感到特别。

6. **解释学习内容3** (从中学到的第三点)

Most importantly, he showed me how to `balance` work and family life. Even though he's busy, he always has time for his family.

最重要的是，他向我展示了如何平衡工作和家庭生活。尽管他很忙，但总是有时间陪伴家人。

7. **总结感受** (总结所学内容)

Watching him run his business has taught me valuable lessons about `dedication` and taking care of people.

观察他经营企业让我学到了关于奉献和关心他人的宝贵经验。

> Would you like to start a family business?
> 你想创办家族企业吗？

A:

1. **表达观点** (直接回答问题)

**Actually, I would love to start a family business someday.** 

实际上，我很想有一天创办家族企业。

2. **说明原因** (解释原因)

**The main reason is that** I enjoy baking and photography, so maybe I could open a cafe that also sells photos.

主要原因是，我喜欢烘焙和摄影，所以也许我可以开一家同时销售照片的咖啡馆。

3. **补充说明** (进一步阐述想法)

**For example**, I could display my photos on the walls and sell them to customers who like them.

例如，我可以在墙上展示我的照片，并将它们卖给喜欢的顾客。

> What are the advantages and disadvantages of family business?
> 家族企业的优点和缺点是什么？

A:

1. **陈述优点** (说明主要优势)

**One big advantage is** that family members trust each other more, so they can work together better.

一个很大的优点是，家庭成员之间更信任彼此，因此他们可以更好地合作。

2. **陈述缺点** (说明主要劣势)

**However, the main disadvantage is** that sometimes family arguments can affect the business.

然而，主要的缺点是，有时候家庭争吵会影响生意。

3. **举例说明** (用例子支持观点)

**For instance**, if family members disagree about money or decisions, it might cause problems in both home and work life.

例如，如果家庭成员在金钱或决策上意见不合，可能会在家庭和工作生活中都造成问题。
