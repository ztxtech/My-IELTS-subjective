## 回答

> Describe a singer whose music/songs you like You should say: Who he/she is What genre his/her music belongs to When/where you listen to his/her music/songs And explain why you like him/her and his/her music
> 描述一个你喜欢的歌手。你应该说：他/她是谁，他/她的音乐属于什么类型，你何时何地听他/她的音乐/歌曲，并解释你为什么喜欢他/她和他/她的音乐

A:

1. **直接介绍歌手**
   I really like the singer `Jay Chou`. 
   我真的很喜欢歌手周杰伦。

2. **说明音乐类型**
   His music is `pop music` with some `Chinese style`. 
   他的音乐是带有中国风的流行音乐。

3. **说明听歌场景**
   I often listen to his songs when I `study` or `relax` in my dorm. 
   我经常在宿舍学习或放松时听他的歌。

4. **说明听歌时间**
   Sometimes I also listen to his music while `walking` to class. 
   有时我走路去上课时也会听他的音乐。

5. **解释喜欢的原因（旋律）**
   I like his music because the `melodies` are very `beautiful` and easy to remember. 
   我喜欢他的音乐因为旋律很优美而且容易记住。

6. **解释喜欢的原因（歌词）**
   Also, his `lyrics` are like poems, they make me feel `relaxed` and happy. 
   而且，他的歌词像诗一样，让我感到放松和快乐。

7. **总结感受**
   That's why Jay Chou is my favorite singer and I always enjoy his songs.
   这就是为什么周杰伦是我最喜欢的歌手，我总是很享受他的歌曲。

> 1. What kind of music do people like at different ages?
> 1. 不同年龄段的人喜欢什么类型的音乐？

A:

1. **观点**
   I think `young people` like `pop music` and `rock music` because it's exciting.
   我认为年轻人喜欢流行音乐和摇滚音乐，因为很刺激。

2. **原因**
   But `older people` often prefer `soft music` like `classical music` because it's more peaceful.
   但年长的人通常更喜欢柔和的音乐，比如古典音乐，因为更平静。

3. **例子**
   For example, my roommate loves pop songs, but my parents enjoy listening to classical music.
   比如，我的室友喜欢流行歌曲，但我父母喜欢听古典音乐。

> 2. What kind of music is popular in China now and what kind will be in the future?
> 2. 现在在中国什么音乐流行？未来会流行什么？

A:

1. **现在流行**
   Now, `pop music` and `hip-hop` are very popular in China, especially among young people.
   现在，流行音乐和嘻哈音乐在中国很流行，特别是在年轻人中。

2. **未来预测**
   In the future, I think `electronic music` might become more popular because it's modern and fun.
   未来，我认为电子音乐可能会更流行，因为它很现代和有趣。

3. **原因**
   Also, with new technology, people can create more interesting music styles.
   而且，随着新技术，人们可以创造更多有趣的音乐风格。

> 3. Do Chinese parents require their children to learn to play musical instruments?
> 3. 中国父母会要求他们的孩子学习乐器吗？

A:

1. **普遍现象**
   Yes, many Chinese parents want their children to learn instruments like `piano` or `violin`.
   是的，很多中国父母希望他们的孩子学习钢琴或小提琴等乐器。

2. **原因**
   They think it's good for children's `development` and can help them in the future.
   他们认为这对孩子的发展有好处，并且未来能帮助他们。

3. **例子**
   For example, my friend's parents asked him to learn piano when he was young.
   比如，我朋友的父母在他小时候要求他学钢琴。

> 4. Why do some people like to listen to live music while others prefer CDs? Can anyone learn how to sing?
> 4. 为什么有些人喜欢听现场音乐，而其他人更喜欢CD？任何人都能学会唱歌吗？

A:

1. **现场音乐偏好**
   Some people like `live music` because it feels more `real` and exciting.
   有些人喜欢现场音乐因为它感觉更真实和刺激。

2. **CD偏好**
   Others prefer `CDs` because the sound is perfect and they can listen anytime.
   其他人更喜欢CD因为声音很完美而且他们可以随时听。

3. **学习唱歌**
   Yes, I think anyone can learn to sing with practice and good teaching.
   是的，我认为通过练习和好的教学，任何人都能学会唱歌。

> 5. What should the government do to help people with musical talent?
> 5. 政府应该做什么来帮助有音乐才华的人？

A:

1. **建议1**
   The government can provide `free music classes` in schools.
   政府可以在学校提供免费音乐课程。

2. **建议2**
   They can also organize `music competitions` to find talented people.
   他们也可以组织音乐比赛来发现有才华的人。

3. **建议3**
   Plus, giving `scholarships` to good music students would be very helpful.
   另外，给优秀的音乐学生提供奖学金会很有帮助。
