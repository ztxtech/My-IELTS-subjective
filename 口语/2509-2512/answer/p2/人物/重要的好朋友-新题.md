# 重要的好朋友

## 题目

### P2

Describe a good friend who is important to you. You should say: Who he/she is, How/where you got to know him/her, How long you have known each other, And explain why he/she is important to you

### P3

1. Do you think it is better for children to have a few close friends or many casual friends?
2. Do you think a child's relationship with friends can be replaced by that with other people, like parents or other family members?

## 回答

> Describe a good friend who is important to you. You should say: Who he/she is, How/where you got to know him/her, How long you have known each other, And explain why he/she is important to you
> 描述一个对你很重要的好朋友。你应该说：他/她是谁，你如何/在哪里认识他/她的，你们认识多久了，并解释为什么他/她对很重要

A:

1. 介绍我的朋友是谁

My important friend is `Ruijie Liu`. He is my `university roommate`.

我重要的朋友是刘瑞杰。他是我的大学室友。

2. 说明我们如何认识的

We met in our `dorm room` on the first day of university. We were both `computer science students`.

我们在大学第一天的宿舍里认识的。我们都是计算机专业的学生。

3. 说明认识的时间长度

We have known each other for about `three years` now.

我们现在已经认识大约三年了。

4. 解释他为什么重要 - 第一点

He is important to me because he always `helps me with my studies`. When I have problems with my `computer projects`, he explains things to me.

他对我来说很重要，因为他总是在学习上帮助我。当我在计算机项目上遇到问题时，他会给我解释。

5. 解释他为什么重要 - 第二点

Also, he is very `good at music` and plays the `guitar`. He teaches me how to play sometimes, which is really fun.

而且，他非常擅长音乐，会弹吉他。他有时教我弹吉他，这真的很有趣。

6. 补充说明我们的友谊

Even though we sometimes have small `arguments` about things like cleaning, we always `make up quickly`.

尽管我们有时会因为打扫卫生之类的事情有小争执，但我们总是很快和好。

7. 总结重要性

That's why he is such an `important friend` to me - he helps me, teaches me, and we have a good `friendship`.

这就是为什么他对我来说是如此重要的朋友 - 他帮助我，教我，我们有着很好的友谊。

> Do you think it is better for children to have a few close friends or many casual friends?
> 你认为孩子有几个亲密朋友更好，还是有很多普通朋友更好？

A:

1. 表达观点

I think it's better for children to have a `few close friends`.

我认为孩子有几个亲密朋友更好。

2. 解释原因

The reason is that `close friends` can provide `real support` and `deep connections`. With many casual friends, the relationships are not as strong.

原因是亲密朋友可以提供真正的支持和深厚的联系。有很多普通朋友的话，关系没有那么牢固。

3. 举例说明

For example, when a child has problems, a close friend will really `listen and help`, while casual friends might not care as much.

例如，当孩子遇到问题时，亲密朋友会真正倾听和帮助，而普通朋友可能不那么在意。

> Do you think a child's relationship with friends can be replaced by that with other people, like parents or other family members?
> 你认为孩子与朋友的关系可以被与其他人的关系取代吗，比如父母或其他家庭成员？

A:

1. 表达观点

No, I don't think `friendship` can be replaced by `family relationships`.

不，我认为友谊不能被家庭关系取代。

2. 解释原因

This is because friends are at the same age and have similar experiences. They can understand each other in ways that `parents cannot`.

因为朋友年龄相仿，有相似的经历。他们可以用父母无法做到的方式相互理解。

3. 举例说明

For instance, children can talk to friends about `school problems` or `hobbies` that parents might not understand as well.

例如，孩子们可以和朋友谈论学校问题或爱好，这些父母可能不太理解。
