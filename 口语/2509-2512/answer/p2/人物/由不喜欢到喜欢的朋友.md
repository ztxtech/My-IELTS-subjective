# 由不喜欢到喜欢的朋友

## 题目

### P2

Describe a person you disliked at first but ended up being friends with You should say: Who he/she is How you knew him/her Why you disliked him/her at first but changed your mind And explain how you feel about the experience

### P3

1. What do you think of making friends online?
2. Is it a good idea to make friends with people who are much older than you? Do you enjoy meeting new people?
3. Do you think friendship is important?
4. Why is it difficult for some people to make friends with others?
5. Do we benefit from having friends with different personalities?

## 回答

> Describe a person you disliked at first but ended up being friends with
> 描述一个你起初不喜欢但最终成为朋友的人

A:
1. **直接介绍人物**
   I want to talk about my roommate, `Ruijie Liu`. We study computer science together at university.
   我想谈谈我的室友刘瑞杰。我们在大学一起学习计算机科学。

2. **说明认识方式**
   We became roommates when we started university. At first, I thought he was too noisy because he always played guitar in our room.
   我们上大学时成为了室友。起初，我觉得他太吵了，因为他总是在房间里弹吉他。

3. **解释态度转变的原因**
   But later, I found out he's actually very kind and helpful. He helped me with my homework many times.
   但后来，我发现他其实很善良而且乐于助人。他很多次帮我做作业。

4. **描述成为朋友的过程**
   We started talking more and found we both like music and photography. Now we often go to the gym together.
   我们开始更多地交谈，发现我们都喜欢音乐和摄影。现在我们经常一起去健身房。

5. **表达对这段经历的感受**
   I feel happy that we became friends. This experience taught me not to judge people too quickly.
   我很高兴我们成为了朋友。这段经历教会我不要太快评判别人。

> What do you think of making friends online?
> 你对网上交朋友有什么看法？

A:
1. **表达观点**
   I think making friends online can be good because you can meet people from different places.
   我认为网上交朋友可以很好，因为你可以认识来自不同地方的人。

2. **说明原因**
   It's convenient and you can find people who share your interests easily.
   这很方便，而且你可以轻松找到和你有共同兴趣的人。

3. **提出注意事项**
   But you need to be careful and not share personal information too quickly.
   但你需要小心，不要太快分享个人信息。

> Is it a good idea to make friends with people who are much older than you? Do you enjoy meeting new people?
> 和比你年长很多的人交朋友是个好主意吗？你喜欢认识新朋友吗？

A:
1. **回答第一个问题**
   Yes, I think it's a good idea because older friends can give good advice based on their experience.
   是的，我认为这是个好主意，因为年长的朋友可以根据他们的经验给出好建议。

2. **回答第二个问题**
   I really enjoy meeting new people because it helps me learn about different ways of thinking.
   我真的很喜欢认识新朋友，因为这帮助我了解不同的思维方式。

3. **补充说明**
   Meeting people from different backgrounds makes life more interesting.
   认识来自不同背景的人让生活更有趣。

> Do you think friendship is important?
> 你认为友谊重要吗？

A:
1. **直接回答**
   Yes, I think friendship is very important for everyone.
   是的，我认为友谊对每个人都很重要。

2. **解释原因**
   Good friends can help you when you have problems and make happy times even better.
   好朋友可以在你有困难时帮助你，让快乐时光更加美好。

3. **举例说明**
   For example, when I feel stressed about schoolwork, talking to friends always makes me feel better.
   例如，当我为学业感到压力时，和朋友交谈总是让我感觉更好。

> Why is it difficult for some people to make friends with others?
> 为什么有些人很难和别人交朋友？

A:
1. **指出主要原因**
   Some people are shy and find it hard to start conversations with strangers.
   有些人很害羞，觉得很难和陌生人开始交谈。

2. **说明另一个原因**
   Others might have had bad experiences in the past that make them careful about making new friends.
   其他人可能过去有过不好的经历，让他们对交新朋友很谨慎。

3. **提出解决方法**
   I think joining clubs or groups with similar interests can help people make friends more easily.
   我认为加入有相似兴趣的俱乐部或团体可以帮助人们更容易交朋友。

> Do we benefit from having friends with different personalities?
> 拥有不同性格的朋友对我们有好处吗？

A:
1. **肯定回答**
   Definitely yes, we can benefit a lot from having friends with different personalities.
   当然是的，拥有不同性格的朋友我们可以受益很多。

2. **解释好处**
   They can help us see things from different perspectives and teach us new ways of thinking.
   他们可以帮助我们从不同的角度看问题，教我们新的思维方式。

3. **举例说明**
   For example, my more organized friends help me be better at planning my time.
   例如，我更有条理的朋友帮助我更好地规划时间。
