## 回答

> Describe a person who inspired you to do something interesting
> 描述一个激励你做有趣事情的人

A:

1. **介绍人物**
   **I'd like to talk about my university professor, Prof. Deng.** He teaches computer science at my university.
   我想谈谈我的大学教授，邓教授。他在我的大学教计算机科学。

2. **如何认识**
   **I first met him when I started my research project on time series.** He was my supervisor and we worked together in the lab.
   我第一次见到他是在我开始时间序列研究项目时。他是我的导师，我们在实验室一起工作。

3. **有趣的事情**
   **He inspired me to publish my first research paper in an international journal.** This was something I never thought I could do before.
   他激励我在国际期刊上发表了我的第一篇研究论文。这是我以前从未想过能做到的事情。

4. **如何激励**
   **He always believed in me even when I failed many times.** He would say "Let's try again" and show me how to improve my work.
   即使我失败了很多次，他也一直相信我。他会说"让我们再试一次"，并告诉我如何改进我的工作。

5. **具体例子**
   **For example, when my first paper was rejected, he helped me understand the reviewers' comments.** We worked late in the lab to make it better.
   例如，当我的第一篇论文被拒绝时，他帮助我理解审稿人的意见。我们在实验室工作到很晚来改进它。

6. **最终成果**
   **After three months of hard work, our paper was finally accepted.** I felt so proud and happy that I could achieve this.
   经过三个月的努力，我们的论文终于被接受了。我感到非常自豪和高兴能够实现这个目标。

7. **总结影响**
   **Prof. Deng showed me that with patience and hard work, I can do interesting and important things.** He made me love research and learning new things.
   邓教授向我展示了只要有耐心和努力工作，我就能做有趣且重要的事情。他让我爱上了研究和学习新事物。

> What qualities make someone a role model?
> 什么品质能让人成为榜样？

A:

1. **观点**
   **I think a good role model should be kind and patient.** They should always want to help others.
   我认为一个好的榜样应该善良且有耐心。他们应该总是想要帮助别人。

2. **原因**
   **This is important because people need someone who can guide them without getting angry.** A kind person makes others feel safe to learn.
   这很重要，因为人们需要一个不会生气就能指导他们的人。一个善良的人让别人感到学习是安全的。

3. **例子**
   **For example, my professor never shouted when I made mistakes.** He always explained things calmly, which made me want to learn more.
   例如，我的教授在我犯错时从不喊叫。他总是平静地解释事情，这让我想学得更多。

> Why should children learn from role models?
> 为什么孩子应该向榜样学习？

A:

1. **观点**
   **Children should learn from role models because they show good examples to follow.** It helps children know what is right and wrong.
   孩子们应该向榜样学习，因为他们展示了好的例子可以遵循。这帮助孩子们知道什么是对错。

2. **原因**
   **Role models can teach children important values like honesty and hard work.** These values help children become better people when they grow up.
   榜样可以教给孩子们重要的价值观，如诚实和努力工作。这些价值观帮助孩子们长大后成为更好的人。

3. **例子**
   **For instance, when children see their role models helping others, they will also want to help people.** This makes the world a better place.
   例如，当孩子们看到他们的榜样帮助别人时，他们也会想要帮助别人。这让世界变得更美好。

> Who can influence children more, teachers or parents?
> 谁更能影响孩子，老师还是父母？

A:

1. **观点**
   **I think both are important, but parents usually have more influence.** Children spend more time with their parents from a young age.
   我认为两者都很重要，但父母通常更有影响力。孩子们从小就和父母在一起的时间更多。

2. **原因**
   **Parents teach children their first words and basic behaviors.** These early lessons stay with children for their whole life.
   父母教孩子们说第一个词和基本行为。这些早期的课程会伴随孩子们一生。

3. **例子**
   **However, teachers also have big influence at school.** They teach children new subjects and how to work with other children.
   然而，老师在学校也有很大的影响力。他们教孩子们新科目以及如何与其他孩子合作。

> What kind of international news inspires people?
> 什么样的国际新闻能激励人们？

A:

1. **观点**
   **News about scientific discoveries and new inventions can inspire people.** These stories show what humans can achieve.
   关于科学发现和新发明的新闻可以激励人们。这些故事展示了人类能够实现什么。

2. **原因**
   **People feel hopeful when they read about new technologies that can help solve world problems.** It makes them believe in progress.
   当人们读到可以帮助解决世界问题的新技术时，他们会感到充满希望。这让他们相信进步。

3. **例子**
   **For example, news about new medical treatments or clean energy solutions inspires many people.** It shows we are making the world better.
   例如，关于新医疗方法或清洁能源解决方案的新闻激励着许多人。这表明我们正在让世界变得更好。

> Besides parents and teachers, who else can motivate children?
> 除了父母和老师，还有谁能激励孩子？

A:

1. **观点**
   **Friends and older siblings can also motivate children a lot.** Children often want to be like their friends or older brothers and sisters.
   朋友和哥哥姐姐也能很好地激励孩子。孩子们经常想要像他们的朋友或哥哥姐姐一样。

2. **原因**
   **Children spend much time with their friends and learn from each other.** They see what their friends can do and want to do the same.
   孩子们花很多时间和朋友在一起并互相学习。他们看到朋友能做什么，并想要做同样的事情。

3. **例子**
   **For instance, if a child's friend is good at sports, the child might also want to play sports.** This kind of motivation comes from friendship.
   例如，如果一个孩子的朋友擅长运动，这个孩子可能也会想要运动。这种激励来自友谊。

> Can online teaching motivate students to learn? How?
> 在线教学能激励学生学习吗？如何激励？

A:

1. **观点**
   **Yes, online teaching can motivate students if it is done well.** Good online classes can be interesting and fun.
   是的，如果做得好，在线教学可以激励学生。好的在线课程可以有趣且好玩。

2. **原因**
   **Online teaching uses videos, games, and interactive activities that make learning enjoyable.** Students can learn at their own pace.
   在线教学使用视频、游戏和互动活动，使学习变得愉快。学生可以按照自己的节奏学习。

3. **例子**
   **For example, some online platforms use points and rewards to motivate students.** When students complete tasks, they get stars or badges, which makes them want to learn more.
   例如，一些在线平台使用积分和奖励来激励学生。当学生完成任务时，他们会获得星星或徽章，这让他们想学得更多。
