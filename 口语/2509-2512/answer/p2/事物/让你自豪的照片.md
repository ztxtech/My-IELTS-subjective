# 让你自豪的照片

## 题目

### P2
Describe a photo you took that you are proud of You should say:When you took it Where you took it What is in this photo And explain why you are proud of it

### P3
1. Why do some people like to record important things with photos? What can people learn from historical photographs?
2. Is taking photos the best way to remember something? Which is better, taking photos or keeping a diary?
3. When do people take photos?
4. Why do some people like to keep old photos?

## 回答

> Describe a photo you took that you are proud of
> 描述一张你拍过的让你感到自豪的照片

A:
1. **直接回答问题，介绍照片基本信息**
I took a photo of my university campus at sunset last month. I used my `Nikon Z30` camera to take it. The photo shows the beautiful red sky and our school buildings. It looks very peaceful and pretty.

我上个月用我的尼康Z30相机拍了一张校园日落照片。照片里有漂亮的红色天空和学校建筑。看起来非常宁静美丽。

2. **解释自豪的原因**
I'm proud of this photo because I learned how to use my camera better. Before, my photos were not very good. But this one turned out really well. It reminds me of my progress in `photography`.

我为这张照片感到自豪，因为我学会了更好地使用相机。以前我的照片拍得不好，但这张拍得很好。它让我想起我在摄影上的进步。

3. **补充细节并总结**
Also, this photo helps me remember my happy school life. Every time I look at it, I feel good. That's why this photo is special to me.

而且，这张照片帮助我记住快乐的校园生活。每次看到它，我都感觉很好。这就是为什么这张照片对我很特别。

> Why do some people like to record important things with photos? What can people learn from historical photographs?
> 为什么有些人喜欢用照片记录重要的事情？人们能从历史照片中学到什么？

A:
1. **回答问题第一部分**
People like taking photos because photos help them remember special moments. Photos can show exactly how things looked at that time. It's like keeping a memory in a picture.

人们喜欢拍照是因为照片帮助他们记住特殊时刻。照片能准确显示当时的样子。就像把记忆保存在图片里。

2. **回答问题第二部分**
From historical photos, we can learn about how people lived in the past. We can see what clothes they wore and what their cities looked like. These photos teach us about history.

从历史照片中，我们可以了解过去人们的生活。我们可以看到他们穿什么衣服，城市是什么样子。这些照片教我们历史知识。

> Is taking photos the best way to remember something? Which is better, taking photos or keeping a diary?
> 拍照是记住某事的最佳方式吗？拍照和写日记哪个更好？

A:
1. **表达观点**
I think both ways are good for remembering things. Photos are better for remembering how things looked. But diaries are better for remembering feelings and thoughts.

我认为两种方式都对记忆有帮助。照片更适合记住事物的样子。但日记更适合记住感受和想法。

2. **举例说明**
For example, a photo can show your birthday cake. But a diary can tell how happy you felt that day. So they are both useful in different ways.

例如，照片可以显示你的生日蛋糕。但日记可以告诉你那天有多开心。所以它们在不同方面都有用。

> When do people take photos?
> 人们什么时候拍照？

A:
1. **列举常见情况**
People usually take photos during special events. Like at weddings, birthdays, or when traveling. They also take photos of beautiful scenery or with friends.

人们通常在特殊场合拍照。比如在婚礼、生日或旅行时。他们也会拍美丽的风景或和朋友合影。

> Why do some people like to keep old photos?
> 为什么有些人喜欢保存旧照片？

A:
1. **解释原因**
People keep old photos because they bring back good memories. Looking at old photos makes them feel happy and nostalgic. It's like visiting the past again.

人们保存旧照片是因为它们能带回美好回忆。看旧照片让人感到快乐和怀念。就像再次访问过去一样。
