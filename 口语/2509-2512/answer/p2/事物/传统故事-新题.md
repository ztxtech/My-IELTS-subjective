# 传统故事

## 题目

### P2

Describe an interesting traditional story
You should say:
What the story is about
When/how you knew it
Who told you the story
And explain how you felt when you first heard it

### P3

1. What kind of stories do children like?
2. What are the benefits of bedtime stories for children?
3. Why do most children like listening to stories before bedtime?
4. What can children learn from stories?
5. Do all stories for children have happy endings?
6. Is a good storyline important for a movie?

## 回答

> Describe an interesting traditional story
> 描述一个有趣的传统故事

A:

1. **直接回答并介绍故事内容** (介绍故事内容)

**I'd like to talk about** the story of 'The Monkey King'. **It's about** a magical monkey who becomes very powerful and goes on many adventures. He fights demons and protects a monk on a long journey to get holy books.

我想谈谈"孙悟空"的故事。它是关于一只神奇的猴子变得非常强大并进行许多冒险的故事。他打妖怪并保护一位和尚长途跋涉去取经书。

2. **说明了解故事的时间和方式** (说明了解故事的方式)

**I first heard this story** when I was a child, maybe around 7 or 8 years old. **My grandfather used to tell me** this story before bedtime.

我第一次听到这个故事是在我小时候，大概七八岁的时候。我爷爷过去常常在睡前给我讲这个故事。

3. **描述讲故事的人** (描述讲故事的人)

**It was my grandfather who told me** this wonderful story. He was a great storyteller and always made the characters come alive with different voices.

是我的爷爷给我讲了这个精彩的故事。他是个很会讲故事的人，总是用不同的声音让角色活灵活现。

4. **表达初次听故事的感受** (表达初次感受)

**When I first heard it**, I felt really excited and amazed. **I was completely fascinated by** the Monkey King's magical powers and brave adventures.

当我第一次听到时，我感到非常兴奋和惊奇。我完全被孙悟空的魔法力量和勇敢冒险迷住了。

5. **补充细节和情感反应** (补充情感细节)

**What made it even more special was** that my grandfather would act out some parts. **This made me feel** like I was right there in the story with the characters.

让它更特别的是我爷爷会表演一些部分。这让我感觉就像和角色一起在故事里。

6. **总结故事的意义** (总结意义)

**Even now, this story remains** one of my favorite childhood memories. **It's not just entertaining but also** teaches about bravery and friendship.

即使现在，这个故事仍然是我最喜爱的童年记忆之一。它不仅有趣，还教导了勇敢和友谊。

> What kind of stories do children like?
> 孩子们喜欢什么样的故事？

A:

1. **直接回答类型** (说明孩子喜欢的类型)

**Most children tend to prefer** stories with magical elements and exciting adventures. **They especially enjoy** tales with talking animals or superhero characters.

大多数孩子往往更喜欢有魔法元素和刺激冒险的故事。他们特别喜欢有会说话的动物或超级英雄角色的故事。

> What are the benefits of bedtime stories for children?
> 睡前故事对儿童有什么好处？

A:

1. **列举主要好处** (说明好处)

**Bedtime stories offer several benefits for** children's development. **They help** improve language skills and stimulate imagination. **Additionally**, they create special bonding time between parents and children.

睡前故事对儿童发展有几个好处。它们帮助提高语言能力和激发想象力。此外，它们创造了父母和孩子之间的特殊亲密时光。

> Why do most children like listening to stories before bedtime?
> 为什么大多数孩子喜欢在睡前听故事？

A:

1. **解释原因** (说明原因)

**The main reason children enjoy** bedtime stories **is that** it helps them relax and feel secure. **It's also a comforting routine** that signals it's time to sleep, making them feel calm and ready for bed.

孩子们喜欢睡前故事的主要原因是它帮助他们放松和感到安全。这也是一个令人安心的惯例，标志着该睡觉了，让他们感到平静并准备好入睡。

> What can children learn from stories?
> 孩子们能从故事中学到什么？

A:

1. **说明学习内容** (列举学习内容)

**Children can learn many valuable lessons from** stories. **They learn about** good values like honesty and kindness. **Stories also teach them** about different cultures and how to solve problems.

孩子们可以从故事中学到许多宝贵的教训。他们学习关于诚实和善良等良好价值观。故事还教他们了解不同的文化以及如何解决问题。

> Do all stories for children have happy endings?
> 所有的儿童故事都有快乐结局吗？

A:

1. **回答问题并解释** (说明实际情况)

**Not all children's stories have** happy endings. **While many do end happily**, some stories have sad or open endings to teach important life lessons. **However**, most stories for young children tend to have positive conclusions.

并非所有的儿童故事都有快乐结局。虽然很多确实以快乐结束，但有些故事有悲伤或开放式结局来教授重要的人生教训。然而，大多数幼儿故事往往有积极的结局。

> Is a good storyline important for a movie?
> 好的故事情节对电影重要吗？

A:

1. **强调重要性** (说明重要性)

**Absolutely, a good storyline is crucial for** any movie. **It's the foundation that** keeps viewers engaged and emotionally invested. **Without a strong plot**, even beautiful visuals and great acting can't save a movie.

绝对地，好的故事情节对任何电影都至关重要。它是让观众保持投入和情感投入的基础。没有强大的情节，即使有美丽的视觉效果和出色的表演也无法拯救一部电影。
