# 传统故事

## 题目

### P2  
Describe an interesting traditional story  
You should say:  
- What the story is about  
- When/how you knew it  
- Who told you the story  
- And explain how you felt when you first heard it  

### P3  
1. What kind of stories do children like?  
2. What are the benefits of bedtime stories for children?  
3. Why do most children like listening to stories before bedtime?  
4. What can children learn from stories?  
5. Do all stories for children have happy endings?  
6. Is a good storyline important for a movie?  

---

## 回答

> Describe an interesting traditional story  
> 描述一个有趣的传统故事  

A:

1. 思路：先回应题目，点出故事名字  
**One** interesting **traditional story** is **"The Cowherd and the Weaver Girl"**.  
一个有趣的传统故事是《牛郎织女》。

2. 思路：一句话说故事内容  
It is about **a poor boy and a sky girl** who fall in love but can only meet once a year on a bridge of birds.  
它讲的是一个穷男孩和天上的女孩相爱，但一年只能在一座鹊桥上见一次。

3. 思路：说何时何地知道  
I first heard it **when I was six**, during the **Qixi Festival**, when my **grandma** told me.  
我六岁时第一次听到，是在七夕节，奶奶讲给我听的。

4. 思路：描述当时感受  
I felt **so sad** for them, but also **happy** because their love was strong.  
我当时为他们感到难过，但也开心，因为他们的爱很坚定。

5. 思路：总结为什么难忘  
That story made me believe **true love can wait**, and I still remember it today.  
那个故事让我相信真爱可以等待，我到现在还记得。

---

### 分析

#### 话题词汇/短语  
1. **traditional story** 传统故事  
2. **Qixi Festival** 七夕节  
3. **bridge of birds** 鹊桥  

#### 实用词汇/短语/句式  
1. **It is about...** 它讲的是……（简单介绍故事）  
2. **I first heard it when...** 我第一次听到是在……（时间状语从句）  
3. **I felt so... but also...** 我当时很……但也……（表达复杂感受）

> What kind of stories do children like?  
> 孩子们喜欢什么样的故事？  

A:  
Most kids love **stories with animals** because they are cute and easy to understand.  
大多数孩子喜欢有动物的故事，因为它们可爱又容易懂。

They also enjoy **stories with magic**, like a flying carpet or a talking tree.  
他们也喜欢有魔法的故事，比如飞毯或会说话的树。

---

> What are the benefits of bedtime stories for children?  
> 睡前故事对孩子有什么好处？  

A:  
Bedtime stories help kids **relax** and **fall asleep faster**.  
睡前故事帮助孩子放松，更快入睡。

They also **grow vocabulary** because children hear new words in a fun way.  
还能扩大词汇量，因为孩子能在有趣的方式中听到新词。

---

> Why do most children like listening to stories before bedtime?  
> 为什么大多数孩子喜欢睡前听故事？  

A:  
It feels **safe and warm** when a parent reads in a soft voice.  
当父母用温柔的声音读故事时，孩子感到安全又温暖。

Also, the dark room and the story together make a **little adventure** before sleep.  
而且，黑暗的房间和故事一起，在入睡前带来一次小冒险。

---

> What can children learn from stories?  
> 孩子能从故事中学到什么？  

A:  
They learn **right and wrong**, like helping friends is good.  
他们学会对错，比如帮助朋友是好事。

Stories also teach **new words** and **different places**, so kids see a bigger world.  
故事还教新词和不同的地方，让孩子看到更大的世界。

---

> Do all stories for children have happy endings?  
> 所有儿童故事都有快乐结局吗？  

A:  
No, some end sadly, like the **Little Match Girl**, to teach kindness.  
不是，有些结局悲伤，比如《卖火柴的小女孩》，用来教人善良。

But most have happy endings so kids feel **hopeful** before sleep.  
但大多数是快乐结局，让孩子在睡前感到有希望。

---

> Is a good storyline important for a movie?  
> 一部好电影需要好故事吗？  

A:  
Yes, a strong story keeps people watching till the end.  
是的，好故事能让人一直看到最后。

Without it, even big stars or cool pictures feel **empty**.  
没有好故事，即使有大明星或酷炫画面也会显得空洞。
