> 1. Where do we often see advertisements?
> 1. 我们经常在哪里看到广告？

A:

1. **给出常见地点** (中文思路)

We often see advertisements in many places like on `TV`, `YouTube`, and `social media`. Also, we see them on buses, in subways, and on big boards in the city.

我们经常在很多地方看到广告，比如在`电视`、`YouTube`和`社交媒体`上。此外，我们在公交车、地铁和城市的广告牌上也能看到它们。

2. **补充更多地点** (中文思路)

Another common place is inside shopping malls and on the packaging of products we buy. Sometimes we even get advertisements on our phones as messages.

另一个常见的地方是购物中心内部和我们购买产品的包装上。有时我们甚至在手机上收到广告信息。

3. **总结普遍性** (中文思路)

Basically, advertisements are everywhere in our daily life, especially in cities where many people can see them.

基本上，广告在我们日常生活中无处不在，特别是在很多人能看到它们的城市里。

> 2. What are the benefits of advertising?
> 2. 广告有什么好处？

A:

1. **主要好处** (中文思路)

The main benefit is that it helps people learn about new products and services. Without advertisements, we might not know about useful things we can buy.

主要的好处是它帮助人们了解新产品和服务。没有广告，我们可能不知道可以购买的有用东西。

2. **商业好处** (中文思路)

For companies, advertising helps them sell more products and become more successful. It also creates competition which can lead to better products and lower prices.

对公司来说，广告帮助它们销售更多产品并变得更成功。它还创造了竞争，这可以带来更好的产品和更低的价格。

3. **消费者好处** (中文思路)

For consumers, advertisements can help us make better choices by showing us different options and sometimes offering special discounts.

对消费者来说，广告通过向我们展示不同的选择有时还提供特别折扣，帮助我们做出更好的选择。

> 3. Are advertisements good or bad for children?
> 3. 广告对儿童是好是坏？

A:

1. **潜在好处** (中文思路)

Advertisements can be good for children when they show educational products like books or learning toys. These can help children learn new things in a fun way.

当广告展示教育产品如书籍或学习玩具时，它们对儿童有好处。这些可以帮助孩子们以有趣的方式学习新东西。

2. **主要问题** (中文思路)

However, advertisements are often bad for children because they make kids want things they don't need. Many advertisements show unhealthy food or expensive toys that can cause problems for parents.

然而，广告通常对儿童不好，因为它们让孩子们想要他们不需要的东西。许多广告展示不健康的食物或昂贵的玩具，这可能给父母带来问题。

3. **需要监管** (中文思路)

I think there should be rules about what can be advertised to children. Advertisements for children should be helpful and not just trying to make them buy things.

我认为应该对可以向儿童广告的内容制定规则。针对儿童的广告应该是有帮助的，而不仅仅是试图让他们买东西。

> 4. How does advertising affect people?
> 4. 广告如何影响人们？

A:

1. **信息影响** (中文思路)

Advertising affects people by giving them information about products they might need or want. It helps people discover new things that can make their lives better or easier.

广告通过向人们提供他们可能需要或想要的产品信息来影响人们。它帮助人们发现可以让生活更好或更轻松的新东西。

2. **欲望影响** (中文思路)

It also affects people by creating desires for things they didn't know they wanted. Sometimes this is good, but sometimes it makes people spend money on things they don't really need.

它还通过创造人们对他们不知道想要的东西的欲望来影响人们。有时这是好的，但有时它让人们把钱花在他们并不真正需要的东西上。

3. **行为影响** (中文思路)

Finally, advertising can change how people think about certain products or brands. Good advertisements can make people feel positive about a company and more likely to buy from them.

最后，广告可以改变人们对某些产品或品牌的看法。好的广告可以让人们对公司产生积极感受，更可能从他们那里购买。

> 5. Do people pay attention to advertisements on public transport?
> 5. 人们会注意公共交通工具上的广告吗？

A:

1. **有时会注意** (中文思路)

Some people do pay attention to advertisements on public transport, especially when they are bored or have nothing else to do. Colorful or interesting ads can catch their eye.

有些人确实会注意公共交通工具上的广告，特别是当他们无聊或没有其他事情可做时。色彩鲜艳或有趣的广告可以吸引他们的注意。

2. **经常忽略** (中文思路)

However, many people ignore these advertisements because they are too busy looking at their phones or thinking about other things. They see the same ads every day, so they stop noticing them.

然而，许多人忽略这些广告，因为他们太忙于看手机或思考其他事情。他们每天看到相同的广告，所以他们停止注意它们。

3. **有效性有限** (中文思路)

I think these advertisements work best for people who are new to the city or don't travel often. For regular commuters, they become just part of the background.

我认为这些广告对刚来到城市或不经常旅行的人最有效。对常规通勤者来说，它们变成了背景的一部分。

> 6. Do you think it is good to see the same advertisements everywhere?
> 6. 你认为到处看到相同的广告好吗？

A:

1. **品牌识别好处** (中文思路)

Seeing the same advertisements everywhere can be good for companies because it helps people remember their brand. When people see an ad many times, they are more likely to remember the product.

到处看到相同的广告对公司来说可能是好的，因为它帮助人们记住他们的品牌。当人们多次看到一个广告时，他们更可能记住这个产品。

2. **对消费者的问题** (中文思路)

For consumers, it can become annoying to see the same ads repeatedly. It might make people tired of the brand instead of interested in it.

对消费者来说，重复看到相同的广告可能会变得烦人。它可能让人们对这个品牌感到厌倦而不是感兴趣。

3. **需要平衡** (中文思路)

I think it's better to have some variety in advertisements. Companies should create different versions of their ads to keep people interested without making them feel bored.

我认为在广告中有一些多样性更好。公司应该创建他们广告的不同版本，以保持人们的兴趣而不让他们感到无聊
