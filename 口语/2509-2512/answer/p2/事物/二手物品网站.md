# 二手物品网站

## 题目

### P2

Describe a website that sells second-hand items You should say: What it is How you found out about it What people can buy from it And explain whether you like it

### P3

1. Some people think it s a waste to buy too many clothes for children. What do you think?
2. How do people usually handle the clothes they don t want?
3. Why do people buy second-hand clothes?
4. What problems will occur if people don t recycle?
5. Should the government encourage people to recycle items?
6. How do people in your country recycle various items they don t want?

## 回答

> Describe a website that sells second-hand items You should say: What it is How you found out about it What people can buy from it And explain whether you like it
> 描述一个卖二手物品的网站。你应该说：它是什么；你是怎么发现它的；人们可以从上面买什么；并解释你是否喜欢它

A:

1. (直接回答网站名称和基本介绍)  
**I want to talk about Xianyu, which is a very popular second-hand website in China.**  
我想谈谈闲鱼，这是中国一个非常受欢迎的二手网站。

2. (说明如何发现这个网站)  
**I found out about it from my roommate, Ruijie, because he often sells his old clothes and books there.**  
我是从我的室友Ruijie那里知道的，因为他经常在上面卖他的旧衣服和旧书。

3. (介绍网站上可购买的物品范围)  
**On this website, people can buy almost anything second-hand, like clothes, electronics, books, and even cameras.**  
在这个网站上，人们几乎可以买到任何二手物品，比如衣服、电子产品、书，甚至相机。

4. (举例说明具体商品，与个人经历关联)  
**For example, I bought my Nikon Z30 camera from there at a good price, and it works perfectly.**  
比如，我在那里以不错的价格买到了我的尼康Z30相机，它用起来很好。

5. (表达个人喜好和原因)  
**I really like using Xianyu because it's easy to use and helps me save money.**  
我真的很喜欢用闲鱼，因为它很容易用，还能帮我省钱。

6. (进一步说明优点)  
**Also, it's good for the environment because it gives old things a new life instead of throwing them away.**  
而且，这对环境有好处，因为它让旧东西有了新生命，而不是被扔掉。

7. (总结观点)  
**So, I think it's a useful and smart way to shop.**  
所以，我觉得这是一种有用又聪明的购物方式。

1. Some people think it s a waste to buy too many clothes for children. What do you think?
有些人认为给孩子买太多衣服是浪费。你怎么看？

A:

**I agree with this idea.**  
我同意这个观点。

**Children grow up very fast, so they quickly outgrow their clothes.**  
孩子们长得非常快，所以他们很快就不合身了。

**Buying too many is a waste of money and not good for the environment.**  
买太多是浪费钱，而且对环境也不好。

---

2. How do people usually handle the clothes they don t want?
人们通常如何处理他们不想要的衣服？

A:

**Many people give them to friends or family members.**  
很多人把它们送给朋友或家人。

**Some people sell them online on websites like Xianyu.**  
有些人在网上像闲鱼这样的网站上卖掉它们。

**Others donate them to charity so people in need can use them.**  
其他人把它们捐给慈善机构，这样有需要的人可以用。

---

3. Why do people buy second-hand clothes?
人们为什么买二手衣服？

A:

**The main reason is to save money because they are much cheaper.**  
主要原因是为了省钱，因为它们便宜得多。

**Also, some people think it's a good way to help the environment by reusing things.**  
而且，有些人认为通过重复使用东西来帮助环境是一个好方法。

**For example, I bought a nice jacket second-hand for half the original price.**  
例如，我以原价一半的价格买了一件不错的二手夹克。

---

4. What problems will occur if people don t recycle?
如果人们不回收利用，会出现什么问题？

A:

**There will be too much trash, which is bad for our planet.**  
会有太多的垃圾，这对我们的星球不好。

**It can pollute the air and water, making people sick.**  
它会污染空气和水，让人们生病。

**Also, we will waste many useful materials that could be used again.**  
而且，我们会浪费许多可以再次使用的有用材料。

---

5. Should the government encourage people to recycle items?
政府应该鼓励人们回收物品吗？

A:

**Yes, definitely.**  
是的，当然应该。

**The government can teach people why recycling is important through ads and schools.**  
政府可以通过广告和学校教人们为什么回收是重要的。

**They can also make rules to make recycling easier for everyone.**  
他们还可以制定规则让每个人回收更容易。

---

6. How do people in your country recycle various items they don t want?
在你们国家，人们如何回收他们不想要的各种物品？

A:

**In my city, we have different bins for paper, plastic, and glass.**  
在我的城市，我们有不同的垃圾桶用于纸、塑料和玻璃。

**People put their recyclable items in these bins, and a truck comes to collect them.**  
人们把可回收物品放在这些桶里，有卡车来收集它们。

**Many also use apps to sell or give away things they don't need anymore.**  
很多人也用应用程序来卖掉或送掉他们不再需要的东西。
