# 喜欢的节目

## 题目

### P2

Describe a program you like to watch You should say: What it is What it is about Who you watch it with And explain why you like to watch it

### P3

1. What programs do people like to watch in your country?
2. Do people in my country like to watch foreign TV programs?
3. What's the benefit of letting kids watch animal videos than visiting zoos?
4. Do teachers play videos in class in my country?
5. Do you think watching talk shows is a waste of time?
6. Do you think we can acquire knowledge from watching TV programs?

## 回答

> Describe a program you like to watch You should say: What it is What it is about Who you watch it with And explain why you like to watch it
> 描述一个你喜欢看的节目。你应该说：它是什么，它是关于什么的，你和谁一起看，并解释你为什么喜欢看它

A:

1. **直接介绍节目** (简单说明喜欢的节目类型)

I really like to watch a `cooking show` called "MasterChef". It's a program about `home cooks` trying to become the best chef.

我真的很喜欢看一个叫"厨艺大师"的烹饪节目。这是一个关于家庭厨师努力成为最佳厨师的节目。

2. **描述节目内容** (说明节目的具体内容)

The show has different `challenges` every week. The `contestants` need to cook amazing dishes, and the `judges` decide who is the best.

这个节目每周都有不同的挑战。参赛者需要烹饪美味的菜肴，评委决定谁是最好的。

3. **说明观看伙伴** (提到和谁一起观看)

I usually watch it with my `roommate` Ruijie. We enjoy it together and sometimes talk about the food.

我通常和我的室友瑞杰一起看。我们一起欣赏，有时会讨论食物。

4. **解释喜欢原因1** (说明第一个喜欢的原因)

I like it because it teaches me about different `cooking techniques`. I can learn how to make new dishes.

我喜欢它是因为它教我不同的烹饪技巧。我可以学习如何制作新菜肴。

5. **解释喜欢原因2** (说明第二个喜欢的原因)

It's also very `entertaining` to watch. The `competition` is exciting, and I like seeing the creative dishes.

观看它也非常有趣。比赛很刺激，我喜欢看创意菜肴。

6. **解释喜欢原因3** (说明第三个喜欢的原因)

Sometimes it gives me ideas for my own `baking hobby`. I get inspiration from the show.

有时它给我的烘焙爱好提供了想法。我从节目中获得了灵感。

7. **总结感受** (简单总结对节目的整体感受)

So, this is why I enjoy watching this program every week. It's both fun and educational.

所以，这就是为什么我喜欢每周看这个节目。它既有趣又有教育意义。

For example, `singing competitions` and `soccer games` get many viewers. People like the excitement.

此外，烹饪节目和电视剧也很常见。不同的人有不同的偏好。

A:

1. **直接回答** (说明对外国节目的态度)

Yes, many people in my country enjoy watching `foreign programs`. They are quite popular.

是的，我们国家很多人喜欢看外国节目。它们相当受欢迎。

> What's the benefit of letting kids watch animal videos than visiting zoos?
> 让孩子看动物视频相比参观动物园有什么好处？

A:

1. **说明主要好处** (指出最重要的优势)

The main benefit is that it's much `safer` for children. They can learn about animals without any risk.

主要的好处是对孩子来说更安全。他们可以在没有任何风险的情况下了解动物。

2. **解释便利性** (说明方便之处)

Also, it's more `convenient` and `cheaper`. Parents don't need to travel or pay for tickets.

此外，它更方便且更便宜。父母不需要旅行或支付门票。

3. **补充教育价值** (提到教育方面的优势)

Children can see many different animals from around the world. This helps them learn about `nature` and `conservation`.

孩子们可以看到来自世界各地的许多不同动物。这帮助他们了解自然和保护。

> Do teachers play videos in class in your country?
> 在你们国家，老师会在课堂上播放视频吗？

A:

1. **直接回答** (说明课堂使用视频的情况)

Yes, teachers often use `videos` in class. It's a common teaching method.

是的，老师经常在课堂上使用视频。这是一种常见的教学方法。

2. **解释用途** (说明视频的教学用途)

They show `educational videos` to help explain difficult topics. This makes learning more interesting.

他们播放教育视频来帮助解释困难的话题。这使学习更有趣。

3. **举例说明** (给出具体的例子)

For example, in `science class`, teachers might show videos about animals or space. This helps students understand better.

例如，在科学课上，老师可能会播放关于动物或太空的视频。这帮助学生更好地理解。

> Do you think watching talk shows is a waste of time?
> 你认为看脱口秀是浪费时间吗？

A:

1. **表达观点** (说明个人看法)

I don't think it's a complete `waste of time`. It depends on how you watch them.

我不认为这完全是浪费时间。这取决于你如何观看它们。

2. **说明好处** (指出积极方面)

Talk shows can be `educational`. You can learn about current events and different opinions.

脱口秀可以具有教育意义。你可以了解时事和不同的观点。

3. **提出建议** (给出合理的观看建议)

However, people should not watch too much. Like everything, `moderation` is important.

然而，人们不应该看太多。像所有事情一样，适度很重要。
