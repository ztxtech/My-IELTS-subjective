# 搞笑电影

## 题目

### P2

Describe a film that made you laugh You should say: What it is When you watched it Who you watched it with And explain why it made you laugh

### P3

1. Do people like comedy?
2. Why do people of all ages like cartoons?
3. Why do some people like to make others laugh?
4. Should teachers tell jokes in class?
5. What kind of people like comedy?
6. Is it good to tell jokes in business activities?

## 回答

> Describe a film that made you laugh You should say: What it is When you watched it Who you watched it with And explain why it made you laugh
> 描述一部让你发笑的电影。你应该说：它是什么，你什么时候看的，你和谁一起看的，并解释为什么它让你发笑

A:

1. **直接回答问题并介绍电影** (使用简单词汇介绍电影)

I want to talk about a funny movie called `The Lego Movie`. I watched it last month with my roommate `Ruijie Liu` in our dorm. We found it online and decided to watch it together.

我想谈谈一部叫做《乐高大电影》的搞笑电影。我上个月和我的室友刘瑞杰在宿舍里一起看的。我们在网上找到它并决定一起看。

2. **描述观影场景和感受** (使用简单句子描述具体场景)

The movie is about a normal Lego man who becomes a hero. The story is very silly but in a good way. There are many funny characters and silly jokes that made us laugh a lot.

这部电影是关于一个普通的乐高小人如何成为英雄的故事。故事很傻但很有趣。有很多搞笑角色和傻傻的笑话让我们笑个不停。

3. **解释为什么觉得好笑** (使用具体例子说明)

My favorite part was when the main character tried to build something but it kept falling apart. We laughed so hard that we had to pause the movie. It was a great way to relax after studying.

我最喜欢的部分是当主角试图建造东西时，东西一直倒塌。我们笑得那么厉害，不得不暂停电影。这是在学习后放松的好方法。

4. **总结感受** (简单总结)

I think this movie is perfect when you need a good laugh. It's simple, funny, and great for watching with friends.

我认为当你需要好好笑一笑时，这部电影是完美的。它简单、有趣，非常适合和朋友一起看。

5. `great for watching with friends` - 非常适合和朋友一起看

> 1. Do people like comedy?
> 1. 人们喜欢喜剧吗？

A:

**大多数人都喜欢喜剧** (直接回答观点)

Yes, I think most people enjoy comedy. It helps them forget their problems and feel happy. For example, after a long day at work, watching a funny show can make people relax.

是的，我认为大多数人都喜欢喜剧。它帮助人们忘记问题并感到快乐。例如，在长时间工作后，看搞笑节目可以让人们放松。

> 2. Why do people of all ages like cartoons?
> 2. 为什么所有年龄段的人都喜欢卡通片？

A:

**卡通片简单有趣** (解释原因)

Cartoons are simple and fun for everyone. They have bright colors and funny characters that appeal to all ages. Also, they often tell good stories that are easy to understand.

卡通片对每个人来说都简单有趣。它们有鲜艳的颜色和有趣的角色，吸引所有年龄段的人。而且，它们通常讲述容易理解的好故事。

> 3. Why do some people like to make others laugh?
> 3. 为什么有些人喜欢让别人笑？

A:

**让人快乐的感觉很好** (说明动机)

Some people enjoy making others laugh because it feels good to see people happy. When you tell a joke and people laugh, it creates a friendly atmosphere. It's also a way to make friends and connect with people.

有些人喜欢让别人笑，因为看到别人快乐感觉很好。当你讲笑话而人们笑时，会创造友好的氛围。这也是交朋友和与人联系的方式。

> 4. Should teachers tell jokes in class?
> 4. 老师应该在课堂上讲笑话吗？

A:

**适度的笑话有帮助** (表达观点)

I think teachers can tell some jokes, but not too many. A little humor can make lessons more interesting and help students remember things. However, the main focus should still be on learning.

我认为老师可以讲一些笑话，但不要太多。一点幽默可以让课程更有趣，帮助学生记住东西。然而，主要重点仍应该是学习。

> 5. What kind of people like comedy?
> 5. 什么样的人喜欢喜剧？

A:

**各种性格的人都可能喜欢** (描述不同类型)

Many different people enjoy comedy. Some are outgoing and like to laugh with friends, while others are quiet but still enjoy funny movies alone. Basically, anyone who wants to feel happy can like comedy.

许多不同的人喜欢喜剧。有些是外向的，喜欢和朋友一起笑，而其他安静的人仍然喜欢独自看搞笑电影。基本上，任何想要感到快乐的人都可能喜欢喜剧。

> 6. Is it good to tell jokes in business activities?
> 6. 在商业活动中讲笑话好吗？

A:

**需要谨慎使用** (说明利弊)

It can be good if the jokes are appropriate. A little humor can make business meetings less stressful and help people relax. But if the jokes are not suitable, they might offend people or seem unprofessional.

如果笑话合适的话是好的。一点幽默可以让商务会议压力减小，帮助人们放松。但如果笑话不合适，可能会冒犯别人或显得不专业
