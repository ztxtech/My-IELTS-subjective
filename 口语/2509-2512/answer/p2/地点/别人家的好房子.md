# 别人家的好房子

## 题目

### P2

Describe a person's house/apartment that you think is very good You should say: Who this person is What the house/apartment is like How often you visit there And explain why you think it's very good

### P3

1. Why is it so expensive to buy a house or an apartment now?
2. Do many people spend a lot of money renovating and decorating their houses or apartments?
3. Is natural light important for a house or apartment?
4. What kind of houses or apartments do people prefer to buy?

## 回答

> Describe a person's house/apartment that you think is very good You should say: Who this person is What the house/apartment is like How often you visit there And explain why you think it's very good
> 描述一个你认为非常好的房子或公寓。你应该说：这个人是谁，房子或公寓是什么样的，你多久去一次，并解释你为什么认为它非常好

A:

1. 介绍人物和基本情况
I'd like to talk about my friend Ruijie Liu's apartment. He's my university roommate and we study computer science together. He lives in a nice apartment near our campus.

我想谈谈我的朋友刘瑞杰的公寓。他是我的大学室友，我们一起学习计算机科学。他住在我们校园附近的一个不错的公寓里。

2. 描述公寓特点
His apartment is not very big but it's very comfortable. It has two bedrooms, a living room, and a small kitchen. What I like most is that it gets lots of natural light during the day, which makes the whole place feel warm and bright.

他的公寓不是很大但非常舒适。有两个卧室、一个客厅和一个小厨房。我最喜欢的是白天有很多自然光，这让整个地方感觉温暖明亮。

3. 说明访问频率
I visit his place quite often, usually once or twice a month. Sometimes we study together there, and other times we just hang out and listen to music. He has a guitar and often plays it when I visit.

我经常去他的地方，通常每月一两次。有时我们在那里一起学习，有时我们只是闲逛听音乐。他有一把吉他，我拜访时他经常弹。

4. 解释为什么喜欢
I think his apartment is very good because it feels like a home. It's always clean and tidy, and he has some nice decorations on the walls. Also, the location is perfect - it's close to school and there are many shops and restaurants nearby.

我认为他的公寓非常好，因为它感觉像家一样。总是干净整洁，墙上有些漂亮的装饰。而且位置完美 - 离学校近，附近有很多商店和餐馆。

> 1. Why is it so expensive to buy a house or an apartment now?
> 1. 为什么现在买房或公寓这么贵？

A:

1. 说明主要原因
I think houses are expensive now because many people want to live in cities where there are good jobs and schools. When many people want something but there isn't enough for everyone, the price goes up.

我认为现在房子贵是因为很多人都想住在有好工作和好学校的城市。当很多人都想要某样东西但不够每个人分时，价格就会上涨。

> 2. Do many people spend a lot of money renovating and decorating their houses or apartments?
> 2. 很多人花很多钱装修和装饰他们的房子或公寓吗？

A:

1. 说明普遍现象
Yes, many people do spend money on making their homes look nice. They want their house to be comfortable and to show their personal style. For example, my friend spent some money to paint his walls and buy new furniture.

是的，很多人确实花钱让他们的家看起来漂亮。他们希望房子舒适并能展示个人风格。例如，我的朋友花了一些钱粉刷墙壁和买新家具。

> 3. Is natural light important for a house or apartment?
> 3. 自然光对房子或公寓重要吗？

A:

1. 强调重要性
Yes, natural light is very important. It makes rooms feel bigger and more cheerful. It's also good for health because sunlight gives us vitamin D. My friend's apartment has big windows and it always feels happy there.

是的，自然光非常重要。它让房间感觉更大更愉快。对健康也有好处，因为阳光给我们维生素D。我朋友的公寓有大窗户，那里总是感觉很愉快。

> 4. What kind of houses or apartments do people prefer to buy?
> 4. 人们更喜欢买什么样的房子或公寓？

A:

1. 说明偏好原因
Most people prefer apartments that are in good locations, close to their work or school. They also like places that are modern, clean, and have good facilities like gyms or parks nearby. Size is important too - not too big but not too small.

大多数人更喜欢位置好的公寓，靠近工作或学校的地方。他们也喜欢现代化、干净且有健身房或公园等好设施的地方。大小也很重要 - 不太大但也不太小。

## 分析

### 话题词汇/短语

1. `apartment` - 公寓
2. `natural light` - 自然光
3. `comfortable` - 舒适的
4. `renovating` - 装修
5. `decorating` - 装饰
6. `location` - 位置
7. `facilities` - 设施

4. **For example,...** - 例如...
5. **Most people prefer...** - 大多数人更喜欢...
6. **It's also good for...** - 这对...也有好处
