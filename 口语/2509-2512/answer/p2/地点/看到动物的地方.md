> Describe a place where you saw many animals.
> 描述一个你看到很多动物的地方。

A:

1.  **介绍地点和时间** (Introduce the place and time)

    **Okay, I'd like to talk about a place** I visited where I saw many `animals`. It was the `Chengdu Zoo`, and I went there last `summer holiday` with my `family`.
    好的，我想说说我看到很多动物的一个地方。那是`成都动物园`，去年`暑假`我和`家人`一起去的。

2.  **描述所见动物** (Describe the animals seen)

    **When we got there, I was so excited because there were so many different kinds of `animals`. For example**, we saw big `lions` and `tigers` sleeping in the sun. We also saw `monkeys` playing and jumping around, which was very funny. There were tall `giraffes` eating leaves from high trees, and `elephants` having a bath in a big pool. I even saw some `pandas`, which are very special in Chengdu.
    我们到那里的时候，我非常`兴奋`，因为有好多不同种类的`动物`。`例如`，我们看到了大`狮子`和`老虎`在阳光下`睡觉`。我们还看到了`猴子`在玩耍和`跳来跳去`，那`非常有趣`。有高高的`长颈鹿`在吃高树上的`叶子`，还有`大象`在`大水池`里`洗澡`。我甚至看到了`熊猫`，它们在`成都`非常`特别`。

3.  **表达感受** (Express feelings)

    **I felt really happy and a bit amazed to see all these `animals` up close.** It was `like` watching a `nature show` on TV, but it was `real life`. I also `learned some new things` about how these `animals` live and what they `eat`. It was a `wonderful day` for me and my `family`, and I felt very `close to nature`.
    我`感到`非常`开心`，也有点`惊讶`能`近距离`看到所有这些`动物`。这`就像`在电视上看`自然节目`，但它是`真实生活`。我还`学到了一些新东西`，关于这些`动物`如何`生活`以及它们`吃什么`。对我`和我的家人`来说，那是`美好的一天`，我`感觉`离`大自然`很`近`。
