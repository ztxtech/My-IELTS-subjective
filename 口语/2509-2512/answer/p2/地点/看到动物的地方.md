# 看到动物的地方

## 题目

### P2

Describe a place where you saw many animals You should say: When you went there Who you went with What animals you saw there And explain how you felt about the place

### P3

1. Why do stories and movies for children always feature animals?
2. How did animals help people in the past?
3. How do animals help us today?
4. Do you think people are more interested in animals now than they were in the past?
5. Why should we protect animals?
6. Do you think children love animals more than old people do?

## 回答

> Describe a place where you saw many animals You should say: When you went there Who you went with What animals you saw there And explain how you felt about the place
> 描述一个你看到许多动物的地方，你应该说：你什么时候去的，你和谁一起去的，你在那里看到了什么动物，并解释你对这个地方的感受

A:

1. 直接回应问题，介绍地点和时间
**Well, I'd like to talk about** the `Chengdu Research Base of Giant Panda Breeding`. I went there last summer with my roommate, `Ruijie Liu`. 
嗯，我想谈谈成都大熊猫繁育研究基地。我去年夏天和我的室友刘瑞杰一起去的。

2. 说明同行的人和看到的动物
We saw many `giant pandas` there. They were eating `bamboo` and playing around. Some were even climbing trees. It was really cute to watch.
我们在那里看到了很多大熊猫。它们在吃竹子和玩耍。有些甚至在爬树。看着它们真的很可爱。

3. 描述对这个地方的感受
I felt really happy and excited about this place. The `pandas` looked so peaceful and well taken care of. It was a great experience to see these amazing animals up close.
我对这个地方感到非常开心和兴奋。熊猫看起来非常平静而且被照顾得很好。能近距离看到这些神奇的动物是一次很棒的经历。

4. 补充细节，加强感受描述
The environment was also very beautiful with lots of green trees and clean air. It made me appreciate nature and wildlife even more.
那里的环境也非常美丽，有很多绿树和清新的空气。这让我更加欣赏自然和野生动物。

> 1. Why do stories and movies for children always feature animals?
> 1. 为什么儿童故事和电影总是以动物为主角？

A:

1. 给出观点
**I think** animals in stories make them more fun for kids.
我认为故事中的动物让故事对孩子们来说更有趣。

2. 解释原因
**Because** animals can do funny things and teach good lessons.
因为动物可以做有趣的事情并教给人们好的道理。

3. 举例说明
**For example**, a story about a `clever rabbit` can show children how to be smart.
例如，一个关于聪明兔子的故事可以教孩子们如何变得聪明。

> 2. How did animals help people in the past?
> 2. 过去动物是如何帮助人们的？

A:

1. 给出观点
In the past, animals helped people with many jobs.
过去，动物帮助人们完成很多工作。

2. 具体说明
**For instance**, `horses` carried people and things, and `dogs` protected homes.
例如，马匹运送人和物品，狗保护家园。

3. 补充说明
They were very important for farming and transportation.
它们对农业和交通非常重要。

> 3. How do animals help us today?
> 3. 如今动物如何帮助我们？

A:

1. 给出观点
Today, animals still help us in many ways.
如今，动物仍然以多种方式帮助我们。

2. 具体说明
`Dogs` can be `police dogs` or help blind people, and some animals give us food like milk and eggs.
狗可以当警犬或帮助盲人，一些动物给我们提供像牛奶和鸡蛋这样的食物。

3. 情感支持方面
**Also**, `pets` make people happy and less lonely.
而且，宠物让人们开心并减少孤独感。

> 4. Do you think people are more interested in animals now than they were in the past?
> 4. 你认为现在人们比过去对动物更感兴趣吗？

A:

1. 给出观点
**Yes, I believe** people are more interested in animals now.
是的，我认为现在人们对动物更感兴趣。

2. 解释原因
**Because** we have more `zoos`, `animal documentaries`, and people keep more `pets`.
因为我们有更多的动物园、动物纪录片，而且人们养更多宠物。

3. 补充说明
We also understand now that animals are important for our world.
我们现在也理解动物对我们的世界很重要。

> 5. Why should we protect animals?
> 5. 我们为什么要保护动物？

A:

1. 给出观点
We should protect animals because they are important for nature.
我们应该保护动物，因为它们对自然很重要。

2. 解释原因
**If** animals disappear, it can cause problems for our environment.
如果动物消失，可能会给我们的环境带来问题。

3. 道德层面
**Also**, they have the right to live safely, just like people.
而且，它们有权安全地生活，就像人类一样。

> 6. Do you think children love animals more than old people do?
> 6. 你认为孩子比老人更爱动物吗？

A:

1. 给出观点
**I think** children often love animals more.
我认为孩子通常更爱动物。

2. 解释原因
**Because** children are more curious and find animals exciting and fun.
因为孩子更好奇，觉得动物令人兴奋和有趣。

3. 补充说明
But many old people also love animals, especially their pets.
但是很多老人也爱动物，特别是他们的宠物。

## 分析

### 话题词汇/短语

1. `Chengdu Research Base of Giant Panda Breeding` - 成都大熊猫繁育研究基地
2. `giant pandas` - 大熊猫
3. `bamboo` - 竹子
4. `police dogs` - 警犬
5. `pets` - 宠物
6. `zoos` - 动物园
7. `animal documentaries` - 动物纪录片
8. `clever rabbit` - 聪明的兔子

### 实用词汇/短语/句式

1. **Well, I'd like to talk about** - 嗯，我想谈谈...（开始描述）
2. **I think** - 我认为（表达观点）
3. **Because** - 因为（解释原因）
4. **For example** - 例如（举例说明）
5. **For instance** - 例如（具体说明）
6. **Also** - 而且（补充信息）
7. **If** - 如果（条件句）
8. **Yes, I believe** - 是的，我认为（肯定回答）
