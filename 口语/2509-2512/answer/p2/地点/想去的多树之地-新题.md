# 想去的多树之地

## 题目

### P2

Describe a place with a lot of trees that you would like to visit (e.g. a forest, oasis). You should say: Where it is, How you knew this place, What it is like, And explain why you would like to visit it

### P3

1. Why do people like visiting places with trees or forests?
2. Are natural views better than city views?
3. Do all people need some nature?
4. Are people hard-wired to protect environment?

## 回答

> Describe a place with a lot of trees that you would like to visit (e.g. a forest, oasis). You should say: Where it is, How you knew this place, What it is like, And explain why you would like to visit it
> 描述一个你想去的有很多树的地方（例如森林、绿洲）。你应该说：它在哪里，你是怎么知道这个地方的，它是什么样子的，并解释你为什么想去那里

A:

1. 直接回应问题，介绍地点

**I'd like to talk about** `Jiuhua Mountain` in my hometown Chizhou, Anhui. 
我想谈谈我家乡安徽池州的九华山。

2. 说明如何知道这个地方

**I've known about this place since** I was a child because it's very famous in our area.
我从小就知道这个地方，因为它在我们的地区很有名。

3. 描述这个地方的样子

**It's a beautiful mountain area** with many ancient trees and temples. The air is very fresh and clean there.
这是一个美丽的山区，有许多古树和寺庙。那里的空气非常清新干净。

4. 第一个想去的理由

**I would like to visit it because** I enjoy peaceful natural places where I can relax and take photos with my `camera`.
我想去那里是因为我喜欢宁静的自然地方，可以在那里放松并用我的相机拍照。

5. 第二个想去的理由

**Another reason is that** it's close to my hometown, so I can visit my family at the same time.
另一个原因是它离我的家乡很近，所以我可以同时看望我的家人。

6. 补充说明个人兴趣

**I'm really into** photography, and this place would be perfect for taking beautiful nature pictures.
我真的很喜欢摄影，这个地方非常适合拍摄美丽的自然照片。

7. 总结陈述

**So overall**, Jiuhua Mountain seems like a wonderful place to experience nature and practice my hobby.
所以总的来说，九华山似乎是一个体验自然和练习爱好的好地方。

> 1. Why do people like visiting places with trees or forests?
> 为什么人们喜欢参观有树木或森林的地方？

A:

1. 提出观点并解释原因

**I think people enjoy these places because** trees make the air clean and help people feel relaxed. 
我认为人们喜欢这些地方是因为树木使空气清新，帮助人们感到放松。

2. 举例说明

**For example**, after spending time in a forest, people often feel less stressed and more peaceful.
例如，在森林里度过时间后，人们通常感到压力更小，更加平静。

3. 总结观点

**So basically**, natural places with trees provide a nice escape from busy city life.
所以基本上，有树木的自然场所提供了一个逃离繁忙城市生活的好去处。

> 2. Are natural views better than city views?
> 自然景观比城市景观更好吗？

A:

1. 表达个人偏好

**In my opinion**, natural views are often better because they are more peaceful and beautiful.
在我看来，自然景观通常更好，因为它们更宁静、更美丽。

2. 说明原因

**The main reason is that** nature helps people relax and forget about their daily problems.
主要原因是自然帮助人们放松，忘记日常问题。

3. 承认不同观点

**However**, some people might prefer city views because they enjoy busy urban environments.
然而，有些人可能更喜欢城市景观，因为他们喜欢繁忙的城市环境。

> 3. Do all people need some nature?
> 所有人都需要一些自然吗？

A:

1. 肯定回答

**Yes, I believe** everyone needs some connection with nature for their well-being.
是的，我相信每个人都需要与自然有一些联系来保持健康。

2. 解释原因

**This is because** being in nature reduces stress and improves mental health.
这是因为身处自然中可以减少压力，改善心理健康。

3. 举例说明

**For instance**, even a short walk in a park can make people feel happier and more relaxed.
例如，即使在公园里短暂散步也能让人们感到更快乐、更放松。

> 4. Are people hard-wired to protect environment?
> 人们天生就有保护环境的倾向吗？

A:

1. 表达不确定但倾向于肯定的观点

**It seems to me that** people naturally want to protect beautiful natural places.
在我看来，人们自然想要保护美丽的自然场所。

2. 解释原因

**I think this is because** we understand that a clean environment is important for our health and future.
我认为这是因为我们理解清洁的环境对我们的健康和未来很重要。

3. 举例说明

**For example**, most people don't like to see trash in beautiful natural areas and will try to keep them clean.
例如，大多数人不喜欢在美丽的自然区域看到垃圾，会尽量保持它们清洁。
