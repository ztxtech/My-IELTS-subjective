# 想再去一次的城市

## 题目

### P2

Describe a city that you have been to and would like to visit again You should say: When you visited it What you did there What it was like And explain why you would like to visit it again

### P3

1.  What's the difference between the city and the countryside?
2.  Some people say large cities are suitable for old people. What do you think?
3.  Do you think it is possible that all of the population move to cities?
4.  Do you think people in the countryside are friendlier than people in the city?
5.  Are there any changes in your city?
6.  What should the government do to improve citizens' safety?

## 回答

> Describe a city that you have been to and would like to visit again.
> 描述一个你去过并且想再次访问的城市。

A:

1.  **思路与结构**: 回答问题的引入，点明要描述的城市是成都，并提及访问时间。
    **Well, I'd like to talk about `Chengdu`, which is the `city` I live in now, but it's also a `city` I would definitely want to `visit again` if I left. I first came here about two years ago for `university`.**
    嗯，我想说说`成都`，这是我现在居住的`城市`，但如果我离开了，它也绝对是我想`再次访问`的`城市`。我大约两年前为了上`大学`第一次来到这里。

2.  **思路与结构**: 描述在成都做了什么，突出当地特色。
    **When I first moved here, I spent a lot of time just `walking around` and trying all the different `local foods`. For example, I went to `Kuanzhai Alley` and `Taikoo Li`, which are very famous `places` to `explore`. I also loved going to small `restaurants` to eat `hot pot` and `Chengdu snacks`.**
    我刚搬到这里的时候，花了很多时间只是`到处走走`，尝试各种不同的`当地美食`。例如，我去了`宽窄巷子`和`太古里`，它们都是非常著名的`地方`可以`探索`。我还喜欢去小`餐馆`吃`火锅`和`成都小吃`。

3.  **思路与结构**: 描述成都的特点和感受，强调宜居性。
    **What I really liked about `Chengdu` is its `slow pace of life`. People here seem very relaxed and friendly. It feels like a very comfortable and `livable city`. Also, there are many `parks` and `green spaces`, which makes the `air feel fresh` even though it's a `big city`. It’s also known as a `food paradise`, so there are so many delicious things to eat everywhere.**
    我真正喜欢`成都`的是它`慢节奏的生活`。这里的人们看起来非常放松和友好。它感觉像一个非常舒适和`宜居的城市`。此外，这里有许多`公园`和`绿地`，这使得`空气`即使在`大城市`里也`感觉清新`。它也被称为`美食天堂`，所以到处都有很多美味的东西可以吃。

4.  **思路与结构**: 解释为什么想再次访问，总结理由。
    **I would love to `visit Chengdu again` because there are still so many `places` I haven't seen and so many `foods` I haven't tried. Every time I find something new, and I always have a good time here. It's a `city` that helps me feel relaxed and happy, and I really enjoy that `kind of atmosphere`.**

A:

1.  **思路与结构**: 直接给出否定回答，并从空间不足角度解释。
    **No, I don't think it's `possible` for `all people` to `move to cities`. First, there just `isn't enough space` in `cities` for `everyone`. `Cities` already feel `crowded`, and if `more people` came, it would be `too full`.**

2.  **思路与结构**: 从资源和环境角度进一步解释不可行性。
    **Also, who would `grow our food`? `Farms` are mostly in the `countryside`, and we need `farmers` to `plant food` for us. If no one lives in the `countryside`, we might not have `enough food`. Plus, `too many people` in one `place` can cause `big problems` like `more pollution` and less `fresh water`.**
    而且，谁来`种植我们的食物`呢？`农场`大多在`乡村`，我们需要`农民`为我们`种植食物`。如果`没有人`住在`乡村`，我们可能`没有足够的食物`。另外，`太多人`在同一个`地方`会导致`大问题`，比如`更多污染`和更少的`淡水`。
