# 受欢迎的运动场所

## 题目

### P2

Describe a popular place for sports (e.g. a stadium) that you've been to. You should say: Where it is When you went there What you did there And explain how you felt about this place

### P3

1. Do young people like to do sports?
2. What are the benefits of sports for children?
3. Is it necessary to build public sports spaces?
4. What do you think of companies donating sports venues for poor children? Is technology helpful for people to do sports?
5. Do you think local sports teams can help increase community connections?

## 回答

> Describe a popular place for sports (e.g. a stadium) that you've been to. You should say: Where it is When you went there What you did there And explain how you felt about this place
> 描述一个你去过的受欢迎的运动场所（例如体育场）。你应该说：它在哪里，你什么时候去的，你在那里做了什么，并解释你对这个地方的感受

A:

1. (直接回答问题，介绍地点)  
**I want to talk about the gym near my school.** It's in the shopping mall close to my university.  
我想谈谈我学校附近的健身房。它在我大学附近的商场里。

2. (说明访问时间)  
**I go there almost every week**, usually on weekends. I started going there last year.  
我几乎每周都去，通常在周末。我从去年开始去那里。

3. (描述活动内容)  
**At the gym, I do many things.** I use the running machine, lift weights, and sometimes take yoga classes. I also meet my friends there.  
在健身房里，我做很多事情。我用跑步机、举重，有时上瑜伽课。我也在那里见到我的朋友。

4. (表达感受和原因)  
**I really like this place because** it has good equipment and friendly people. It makes me feel healthy and happy.  
我真的很喜欢这个地方，因为它有好的设备和友好的人。它让我感到健康快乐。

5. (补充具体细节)  
**The atmosphere is always positive.** Everyone is working hard but also smiling. The staff are very helpful too.  
氛围总是很积极。每个人都在努力锻炼但也面带微笑。工作人员也很乐于助人。

6. (总结整体体验)  
**Overall, it's a great place** to exercise and relax. I always feel better after going there.  
总的来说，这是个锻炼和放松的好地方。去完之后我总是感觉更好。

> 1. Do young people like to do sports?
> 1. 年轻人喜欢做运动吗？

A:

1. (给出观点)  
**Yes, I think most young people enjoy sports.**  
是的，我认为大多数年轻人喜欢运动。

2. (解释原因)  
**Sports are fun and exciting** for them. They like to play games and be active with friends.  
运动对他们来说有趣又刺激。他们喜欢玩游戏和和朋友一起活动。

3. (举例说明)  
**For example,** many students at my school play basketball or go swimming after class.  
例如，我学校的许多学生课后打篮球或去游泳。

> 2. What are the benefits of sports for children?
> 2. 运动对儿童有什么好处？

A:

1. (陈述主要好处)  
**Sports help children stay healthy and strong.**  
运动帮助儿童保持健康强壮。

2. (说明社交好处)  
**They also learn to work with others** and make new friends through team sports.  
他们也通过学习团队运动来与他人合作和交新朋友。

3. (提到心理益处)  
**Playing sports makes children happier** and helps them feel more confident.  
做运动让孩子们更快乐，帮助他们感到更自信。

> 3. Is it necessary to build public sports spaces?
> 3. 有必要建设公共运动空间吗？

A:

1. (明确回答)  
**Yes, it's very important to have public sports spaces.**  
是的，拥有公共运动空间非常重要。

2. (解释必要性)  
**These spaces give everyone a chance** to exercise, especially people who can't afford gym memberships.  
这些空间给每个人锻炼的机会，特别是负担不起健身房会员的人。

3. (说明社区价值)  
**They also help build stronger communities** by bringing people together.  
它们也通过把人们聚集在一起来帮助建设更强大的社区。

> 4. What do you think of companies donating sports venues for poor children? Is technology helpful for people to do sports?
> 4. 你对公司为贫困儿童捐赠运动场馆有什么看法？科技对人们做运动有帮助吗？

A:

1. (回答第一部分)  
**I think it's a wonderful idea** for companies to help poor children this way.  
我认为公司通过这种方式帮助贫困儿童是个很好的主意。

2. (解释好处)  
**It gives these children opportunities** to play and be healthy like other kids.  
这让这些孩子有机会像其他孩子一样玩耍和保持健康。

3. (回答科技部分)  
**Yes, technology is very helpful** - fitness apps and videos make it easier to exercise at home.  
是的，科技很有帮助 - 健身应用和视频让在家锻炼更容易。

> 5. Do you think local sports teams can help increase community connections?
> 5. 你认为本地运动队能帮助增加社区联系吗？

A:

1. (肯定回答)  
**Definitely, local sports teams bring people together.**  
当然，本地运动队把人们聚集在一起。

2. (解释如何连接)  
**When people cheer for the same team,** they become friends and feel like they belong.  
当人们为同一支队伍加油时，他们成为朋友并感到归属感。

3. (举例说明)  
**For instance,** my neighborhood soccer team organizes games where everyone can participate.  
例如，我社区的足球队组织每个人都能参与的比赛
